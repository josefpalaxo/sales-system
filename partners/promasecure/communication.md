# PromaSecure communication plan

Use an initial discovery conversation to test customer demand and cooperation models. This is a proposed sequence; no outreach or meeting completion is recorded.

## Audience and message

The intended audience is PromaSecure's partnership and delivery decision-makers; roles and participants need confirmation. Lead with customer problems and potential implementation and recurring services value. Explore resale, Australian hosting and joint Australia–GCC opportunities as options.

Keep current product capabilities separate from longer-term vision. Validate Circularo claims against approved records, and verify partner capabilities before using them as facts. Do not imply agreed margins, local procurement eligibility or an approved hosted offering.

## Proposed sequence

| Step | Channel | Purpose and material | Timing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prepare | Internal review | Assign owner; verify claims and audience in the [partnership brief](content/partnership-brief.md) | Before external sharing |
| Discover | Meeting or call | Test priority segments, use cases, operating capabilities and cooperation models | Unscheduled |
| Recap | Email, subject to human approval | Summarise confirmed points, open questions, owners and next actions | After the discussion |
| Demonstrate | Focused demonstration or workshop | Address selected use cases and assess delivery feasibility | If discovery supports proceeding |

Record permitted summaries and business-system references in [activity](activity.md). Keep raw correspondence and personal contact details in the owning business system.
