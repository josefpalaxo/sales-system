# PromaSecure partnership strategy

The working hypothesis is that Circularo could complement PromaSecure's reported cybersecurity, integration, hosting and managed services business with document approval and signing workflows. Explore customer needs and delivery fit before proposing commercial commitments.

## Objectives

- Test whether a local delivery relationship could help serve Australian customers.
- Assess demand and feasibility for a partner-operated Australian hosting model.
- Explore joint opportunities and referrals between Australia and the GCC.

These are proposed objectives, not agreed commitments or qualified pipeline.

## Cooperation models to test

| Proposed model | Potential partner value | What must be established |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reseller and implementation | Potential subscription margin plus architecture, configuration, integration, migration, training and support services | Partner eligibility, commercial terms, delivery skills and customer ownership |
| Australian-hosted managed service | Potential recurring infrastructure, operations and security services alongside platform revenue | Customer demand, deployment feasibility, data handling, support access, security requirements and operating responsibilities |
| Joint Australia–GCC opportunities | Potential referrals, consulting, integration and broader customer access | Actual market presence, relevant relationships, opportunity ownership and referral arrangements |

The models may be combined. No margin, licence entitlement, exclusivity, procurement eligibility or signed agreement is established by this strategy.

## Reported business context

The initial analysis describes consulting, assurance and audits, technology integration, managed security and hosting as possible revenue streams. It also reports Australian hosting capability and operations connecting Australia with the Middle East, including Dubai and Muscat. These claims require primary evidence; use the [profile](README.md) for metadata and evidence gaps.

## Proposed positioning

Lead with practical customer workflows and services that PromaSecure could implement and operate. The hypothesis is that the relationship could generate implementation and recurring services work as well as potential resale revenue.

A possible joint solution could connect customers' Microsoft 365, ERP or CRM systems with identity, approvals, signing or sealing, evidence and archiving. Specific integrations and capabilities must be checked against current approved Circularo records and the proposed architecture before being promised.

## Discovery questions

1. Which customer segments and document processes create an immediate need?
2. Which integration, implementation, hosting and support capabilities can PromaSecure demonstrate?
3. Is Australian hosting required, and what procurement, security and data handling requirements apply?
4. Which geography and customer relationships can each party actually support?
5. Which cooperation model, responsibilities and commercial arrangement would fit an initial opportunity?

## Proposed success criteria

A useful first discussion would identify one or two priority segments, two or three use cases, and accountable next steps. A subsequent opportunity should have a defined customer need, delivery scope and agreed qualification before being described as pipeline.
