# Circularo × PromaSecure
## Partnership opportunities for discussion

**Prepared for the PromaSecure partnership discussion | 2 October 2026**

> Internal draft pending factual and sharing approval. The supplied narrative below contains claims awaiting source validation, as listed in the YAML evidence gaps. Cooperation models are proposals, and the preparation date does not establish that a discussion occurred.

Circularo and PromaSecure could combine trusted document workflows with local implementation, cybersecurity and managed hosting to serve customers in Australia and the GCC.

This brief sets out our initial thinking ahead of a discussion. The aim is to identify practical customer opportunities and a cooperation model that creates value for both companies.

## 1. What Circularo brings

Circularo is a digital trust platform that helps organisations prepare, approve, sign and manage important business documents. It brings document workflows, digital signatures, identity verification and evidence-based archiving into a connected process, helping organisations move from manual administration to more controlled and traceable digital workflows.

Typical starting points include contracts, internal approvals and formal correspondence. Circularo provides the document and signing capabilities, while integrations connect these processes with the systems people already use. Existing integrations and a REST API provide routes for incorporating Circularo into wider customer solutions.

For PromaSecure, the potential opportunity extends across solution design, implementation, integration and ongoing operation. We would like to explore where these services could address Australian customer needs, including whether there is demand for a locally hosted and managed offering.

The platform is available as Circularo-managed SaaS and as Circularo Ultimate for self-hosted / multitenant deployments. Ultimate enables customers or delivery partners to operate the platform within their own infrastructure and security environment.

Circularo's UAE references include TDRA GovSign, Digital Dubai DigiSign and Sharjah Sign, alongside enterprise deployments. This experience provides a foundation for organisations seeking controlled deployment, identity integration and auditable document processes.

## 2. Our current priorities

- **Develop an Australian delivery partnership:** combine Circularo's platform with local customer access, procurement knowledge, implementation and ongoing support.
- **Explore locally hosted services:** assess demand for an Australian-hosted, partner-operated Circularo offering for customers with specific data residency and operational requirements.
- **Create joint enterprise opportunities:** connect document approvals and signing with customers' identity, Microsoft 365, ERP, CRM and other business systems.
- **Extend collaboration across the GCC:** identify opportunities where PromaSecure's services and Circularo's regional experience complement each other.

The immediate focus is on customer needs we can address with the existing platform. Our longer-term direction includes trusted records and governed execution for AI-enabled workflows.

## 3. Why the fit could work

PromaSecure describes its business as cybersecurity consulting and managed security services, supported by technology services and Australian hosting capability. These capabilities could complement Circularo's trusted transaction layer: approvals, signing, sealing, evidence and records.

Together, we could offer customers a complete service covering the platform, integration, deployment, security and ongoing operation. The opportunity for PromaSecure would extend across subscription margin, project delivery and recurring services.

## 4. Three cooperation models

| Model | Proposed approach | Potential revenue for PromaSecure | Value for Circularo |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Reseller and implementation partner** | PromaSecure originates opportunities, resells Circularo and delivers configuration, integrations, training and agreed support. | Recurring subscription margin; implementation and integration fees; support services. | Australian market access and a local delivery capability. |
| **Australian-hosted managed service** | Assess a PromaSecure-operated Circularo Ultimate offering on Australian infrastructure, with agreed responsibilities and service levels. | Platform margin; hosting; managed operations; security and support subscriptions. | A locally operated deployment option and recurring platform revenue. |
| **Joint Australia–GCC opportunities** | Pursue selected projects together, combining customer relationships, regional experience and technical capabilities. | Referral or resale income; consulting, security and integration projects. | Qualified opportunities and broader delivery reach. |

These models can be combined. Commercial terms, delivery responsibilities and customer ownership would be agreed around the selected model and opportunities.

## 5. Initial customer opportunities to test

| Customer need | Possible joint offering |
|---|---|
| Fragmented approvals and document signing | Integrated workflows with electronic signatures, access controls and audit evidence. |
| Sensitive documents requiring local hosting | Circularo Ultimate deployed within an agreed Australian hosting and operating environment. |
| Disconnected business systems | Identity, Microsoft 365, ERP or CRM integrations that connect document processes to existing systems. |
| Recurring official document issuance | Electronic sealing and controlled issuance with verifiable evidence. |
| Limited internal delivery resources | A managed service covering implementation, platform operation and ongoing support. |

Government, regulated enterprises and organisations with complex document processes are potential segments to explore. We would welcome PromaSecure's view on which segments are most accessible and where Australian procurement requirements favour a local delivery partner.

## 6. Suggested first step

Use the first conversation to select one or two customer segments and two or three concrete use cases. From there, we could identify an initial opportunity, run a focused demonstration and outline a joint solution and commercial approach.

For a hosted offering, the next step would be to assess demand, deployment architecture, data handling, support access, security requirements and operating responsibilities. Australian procurement eligibility and any required assessments would need to be established for the proposed service and customer context.

**The central question:** Where can Circularo help PromaSecure win or expand customer engagements while building a sustainable recurring services business together?
