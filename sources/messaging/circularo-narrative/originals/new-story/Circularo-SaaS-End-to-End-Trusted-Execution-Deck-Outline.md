## Slide 2 - The Problem: Fragmented Tools Fragment Accountability

Most organizations have digitized individual parts of their processes.

**Typical environment:**

Create → Collaborate → Workflow → Approve → Sign → Store → Search

But each stage may happen in a different application.

**Every disconnected handoff can lose context:**

-   Which version was authoritative?
-   Who participated?
-   Who was permitted to decide?
-   What exactly was approved?
-   Which identity or assurance was used?
-   Which trust services were applied?
-   What actually happened?
-   Where is the resulting evidence?
-   Can the complete chain be reconstructed and proven?

> **The problem is not simply fragmented applications. It is fragmented
> accountability and evidence.**

**Visual:** Several disconnected application boxes with broken
context/evidence links between them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Slide 3 - Digital Processes Need More Than Digital Signatures

Traditional eSignature thinking concentrates on one important event:

> **Who signed this document?**

But consequential business processes require broader questions:

> Who created it?\
> Which version was reviewed?\
> Who approved it?\
> Under what authority?\
> What assurance was required?\
> What was signed, sealed or timestamped?\
> What evidence remains afterwards?

**Core idea:**

> **The signature is an important trust event. It is not the entire
> trust model.**

**Speaker focus:**\
Circularo's advantage is not abandoning eSignature. It is connecting
eSignature to the complete trusted process around it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Slide 10 - Trusted Execution Is More Than Signing

Different actions require different forms of assurance.

### Workflow Assurance

Who approved? Was the correct process followed?

### Personal Assurance

Who personally authorized or signed? Can their identity be established?

### Organizational Assurance

Did this officially originate from the organization? Can authenticity
and integrity be verified?

### Evidence Assurance

Can the organization subsequently prove the content, participants,
decisions, trust events and process history?

**Core message:**

> **Circularo combines the assurance required by the process rather than
> treating every transaction as simply "send document for signature."**

# Internal Positioning Guardrails

-   Electronic signatures remain a core Circularo capability; the
    narrative expands the context around them.
-   Do not position Circularo as replacing every enterprise application,
    identity provider or trust service.
-   The platform advantage is **connection, orchestration, execution and
    evidence**.
-   Use **Trust Orchestration** for the platform capability and
    **Trusted Execution** for the outcome.
-   Treat **Unified Trust API, integrations and automation** as a core
    part of the SaaS platform story.
-   Treat **Agentic AI readiness** as an architectural extension and
    future-facing advantage, not the main SaaS narrative.
-   Current product, integration, compliance and AI claims must be
    validated against canonical approved knowledge before external use.
