# Circularo Partner Deal Registration Guide

## Purpose

This guide explains how partners register sales opportunities, obtain opportunity protection, request special pricing, and maintain active deal registration.

It complements the:

- Circularo Partner Program Guide
- Circularo Partner Pricing & Special Pricing Guide
- Partner Agreement

If any conflict exists, the signed Partner Agreement and applicable Order Form prevail.

## 1. Why Register a Deal?

Deal registration helps protect the partner's sales investment by establishing opportunity ownership and enabling Circularo to provide commercial and sales support.

Registered opportunities may qualify for:

- Opportunity protection
- Special pricing consideration
- Joint sales engagement
- Technical pre-sales assistance
- Executive sponsorship
- Marketing support
- Commercial approvals

Deal registration is encouraged as early as possible in the sales cycle.

## 2. When to Register

Partners should register an opportunity when they have:

- Identified a qualified sales opportunity.
- Established contact with the customer.
- Confirmed a genuine customer requirement.
- Begun active sales engagement.

Partners should not register speculative opportunities without meaningful customer engagement.

## 3. Registration Requirements

A registration request should include:

- Customer legal name
- Opportunity name
- Business problem or project description
- Products and plans being proposed
- Estimated users or transaction volume
- Expected contract value
- Expected close date
- Sales stage
- Work completed to date
- Known competitors (if applicable)
- Planned next steps

Circularo may request additional information before approving the registration.

## 4. Registration Approval

A registration becomes active only after written approval by Circularo.

Submitting a registration request does not automatically provide opportunity protection or commercial benefits.

Circularo reserves the right to approve, reject, or request additional information before accepting a registration.

## 5. Opportunity Protection

The first complete registration accepted by Circularo normally receives opportunity protection.

Opportunity protection applies only to:

- The approved customer
- The approved opportunity
- The approved scope

Opportunity protection does not automatically extend to:

- Other business units
- Other subsidiaries
- Future projects
- Unrelated opportunities

## 6. Protection Period

Unless otherwise agreed, opportunity protection remains active for up to **six (6) months** from the approval date.

Protection may be extended where:

- The sales opportunity remains active.
- The customer procurement process requires additional time.
- The partner continues demonstrating meaningful sales progress.

Extensions remain subject to Circularo approval.

## 7. Maintaining an Active Registration

To maintain opportunity protection, partners should:

- Keep opportunity information accurate.
- Report significant progress.
- Respond promptly to Circularo requests.
- Maintain an active sales plan.
- Continue engaging the customer.
- Remain in good commercial standing.

Regular communication helps ensure appropriate support throughout the sales cycle.

## 8. Registration Changes

Partners should notify Circularo whenever there is a significant change to the opportunity, including:

- Customer scope
- Proposed solution
- Product selection
- Estimated value
- Expected close date
- Customer decision makers
- Competitive situation

Circularo may review or amend the registration where material changes occur.


## 9. Cancellation of Protection

Circularo may cancel opportunity protection where appropriate, including:

- Extended inactivity
- Lack of customer engagement
- Materially inaccurate information
- Customer request
- Duplicate or conflicting registrations
- Commercial or compliance concerns
- Partner not remaining in good standing

Where practical, Circularo will communicate with the partner before cancelling protection.

## 10. Special Pricing Requests

Where additional commercial support is required, partners may submit a Special Pricing Request.

A request should include:

- Registration ID
- Customer name
- Proposed products and plans
- Expected contract value
- Contract term
- Requested customer discount
- Commercial justification
- Competitive situation
- Customer decision deadline

Special pricing is evaluated individually and is never automatic.

Approval remains entirely at Circularo's discretion.

## 11. Joint Sales Engagement

Certain opportunities require direct participation by Circularo.

Examples include:

- Opportunities exceeding Partner Quoting Limits
- Ultimate deployments
- Government opportunities
- Sovereign cloud projects
- White-label opportunities
- Strategic enterprise accounts
- Multi-country deployments
- Custom commercial arrangements
- Product roadmap discussions
- Major competitive opportunities

Joint engagement benefits both the partner and the customer by providing additional commercial and technical expertise.

## 12. Renewals

Partners are encouraged to begin renewal discussions well before subscription expiry.

Renewal pricing and commercial support may consider:

- Customer relationship management
- Customer satisfaction
- Subscription expansion
- Contract duration
- Partner performance
- Commercial standing

Renewals may require updated commercial approval where significant changes are proposed.

## 13. Master Distributor Opportunities

Where a Master Distributor and an Authorized Reseller participate in the same opportunity:

- Both parties must be identified.
- Circularo must approve the channel structure.
- Commercial responsibilities should be clearly defined.
- Customer ownership should be transparent.
- Commercial support will be coordinated through the approved channel.

Commercial arrangements between distributors and resellers remain their responsibility unless otherwise agreed with Circularo.

## 14. Professional Services

Professional Services are not automatically included in partner deal registration.

Implementation, consulting, training, migration, and custom development are quoted separately and may require direct Circularo involvement.

## 15. Confidentiality

The following information is confidential:

- Deal registration details
- Commercial approvals
- Special pricing approvals
- Partner Buy Prices
- Internal commercial communications

Partners must not disclose confidential commercial information to customers or other partners without Circularo's prior written approval.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does submitting a registration automatically protect the opportunity?

No.

Opportunity protection begins only after Circularo approves the registration.

### How long does protection last?

Normally up to six months, unless extended by Circularo.


### Can two partners register the same customer?

Yes.

However, opportunity protection is normally granted to the first complete registration approved by Circularo for the specific opportunity.

### Does deal registration guarantee special pricing?

No.

Deal registration allows Circularo to evaluate commercial support but does not guarantee special pricing approval.


### Can Circularo participate in registered opportunities?

Yes.

Joint sales engagement is encouraged for strategic, complex, or high-value opportunities and is intended to improve the likelihood of a successful outcome for both the partner and the customer.
