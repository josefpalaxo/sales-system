# Circularo Partner Program FAQ

This FAQ provides quick answers about the Circularo Partner Program. For the complete SaaS discovery, configuration, and quoting process, see [Selling Circularo SaaS as a Partner](<Selling Circularo SaaS as a Partner.md>).

## Program and tiers

### Who can join this program?

The current program covers SaaS Resellers and Master Distributors.

### What are the partner tiers?

Authorized, Advanced, and Strategic.

### Is Master Distributor a tier?

No. Master Distributor is a partner type. A Master Distributor also receives an Authorized, Advanced, or Strategic tier.

### How is tier determined?

Tier considers revenue, pipeline, certification, customer success, renewal performance, reporting, payment performance, and program compliance. Exact thresholds are `TBD`.

## Pricing and margin

### What is customer list price?

It is Circularo's recommended/reference customer price before discount.

Every list price has an explicit Price Basis: Annual, Per Event, Per Pack, or One-Time.

### Can every price be converted to a monthly price?

No. Only an Annual price may be shown as a monthly equivalent by dividing it by 12. Per Event, Per Pack, and One-Time prices must not be divided by 12 or presented as monthly subscriptions.

### What is partner buy price?

It is the confidential price Circularo charges the partner.

### Can the partner set the customer price?

Yes. The partner independently sets its customer resale price, subject to applicable law and its contractual obligations.

### What is realized partner gross margin?

`(Customer Sell Price − Partner Buy Price) ÷ Customer Sell Price`

It is calculated after customer discount.

### Is a 30% buy discount the same as a 30% partner margin?

No. A discount from list price and realized partner gross margin use different calculation bases. For example, if list price is $100, buy price is $70, and the partner sells for $80, realized margin is 12.5%.

### Is 30% guaranteed?

No. It is the maximum standard recurring ceiling for eligible Strategic Business or Enterprise opportunities. It is not a guaranteed or automatically funded margin.

### What are the partner-tier ceilings?

- Authorized: 15%
- Advanced: 20%
- Strategic: 30%

The lower of the partner-tier ceiling and plan ceiling applies.

### What are the plan ceilings?

- Start: 10%
- Pro: 15%
- Business: 30%
- Enterprise: 30%

The lower of the tier ceiling and plan ceiling applies.

### Can first-year margin exceed 30%?

Only in a CSO-approved Strategic Business or Enterprise new-customer opportunity that is registered and demonstrates strategic commercial value. The maximum first-year realized margin is 40%. This exception is case by case and does not automatically continue into renewal.

### What customer discount can a partner offer?

The ordinary band is up to 20% from list. Larger discounts require special-pricing review, and exceptional high-volume or strategic deals may reach 50% with CSO approval.

Circularo's review determines the funding Circularo provides; it does not automatically set the partner's independent resale price.

### Does deal registration automatically provide a special buy price?

No. Registered-deal pricing is evaluated case by case. It is specific to the approved opportunity, scope, and commercial period and does not automatically apply to another customer, expansion, or renewal.

### Will Circularo always protect my margin if I discount?

No. A partner may choose to reduce its own margin. Circularo is not required to lower the buy price to preserve a target margin.

## Deal registration

### Why register a deal?

Registration protects partner investment, improves pricing coordination, and reduces channel conflict.

### Who receives priority?

The first partner to submit a complete registration that Circularo accepts.

### Does the earliest submission automatically win?

No. The submission must be complete, credible, and accepted.

### How long does protection last?

Up to six months.

### What protection does registration provide?

For materially comparable scope, the registered partner receives the best Circularo-funded price or discount relative to later partners and protection from Circularo undercutting the registered offer.

### Can registration be cancelled?

Yes. Reasons include inactivity, inaccurate information, material scope change, customer request, compliance/payment issues, or loss of partner good standing.

### Does registration give permanent ownership of the customer?

No. Protection applies to the accepted opportunity, scope, and period.

### What if the customer requires Circularo to contract directly?

Circularo may preserve the registered partner's approved economics or role through a fee, rebate, subcontract, or another written arrangement.

## Renewals

### Do partners earn margin on renewals?

Yes, when they actively manage adoption, customer support coordination, and renewal and remain in good standing.

### Is renewal margin automatic?

No.

### What happens if Circularo manages the renewal?

Partner renewal margin may be reduced or set to 0%.

### Does a first-year special margin continue?

No. Renewal follows the normal tier and plan rules unless Circularo agrees otherwise in writing.

## Products and services

### Which products participate?

Eligible SaaS subscriptions, add-ons, consumption services, trust services, certificates, and storage.

### Which SaaS plans may partners quote independently?

Partners may independently quote Start, Pro, Business, and Enterprise within the approved Partner Quoting Limits.

Start is primarily intended for individuals and very small teams purchasing directly through the Circularo website. Partners should normally focus on Pro, Business, and Enterprise opportunities.

Ultimate is not available for independent partner quotation.

### Which subscription models are available?

- Start: User-Based
- Pro: User-Based
- Business: User-Based or Transaction-Based
- Enterprise: User-Based or Transaction-Based

Every subscription must use one subscription model. User-Based and Transaction-Based licensing cannot be combined within the same subscription.

### What are the Partner Quoting Limits?

| Plan | Subscription Model | Maximum Independent Quoting Authority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Start | User-Based | Up to **15 licensed users** |
| Pro | User-Based | Up to **25 licensed users** |
| Business | User-Based | Up to **100 licensed users** |
| Business | Transaction-Based | Up to **20,000 annual document transactions** |
| Enterprise | User-Based | Up to **250 licensed users** |
| Enterprise | Transaction-Based | Up to **50,000 annual document transactions** |

Opportunities above these limits must be jointly qualified and quoted with Circularo.

### Can partners sell Add-Ons separately?

No. Add-Ons extend an existing subscription and are not standalone products. Partners must verify that each Add-On is supported by the customer's selected plan.

### Is REST API access included in a subscription?

No. REST API access requires the REST API Access Add-On.

Automated signing always requires the API eSigning Transactions Add-On. Automated sealing always requires the API eSealing Transactions Add-On. This requirement applies regardless of whether the subscription is User-Based or Transaction-Based.

### Are trust services included?

Advanced trust services, including identity verification, KYC, SMS OTP, qualified signatures, qualified seals, and qualified timestamps, require the applicable Add-On or entitlement where available.

UAE Pass Identity Verification events are currently free for eligible UAE SaaS customers. A self-hosted Ultimate deployment requires an annual configuration entitlement, configuration, and DDA approval. UAE Pass signing requires an annual configuration entitlement for both SaaS and Ultimate customers; signing events are currently free but may become chargeable in the future.

NAFATH Identity Verification is available for eligible KSA SaaS customers and self-hosted Ultimate customers in KSA. It requires an annual configuration entitlement. The customer must obtain and maintain its own valid NAFATH Client ID and Client Secret and pays NAFATH directly; Circularo currently adds no per-event charge.

### Is document archiving included?

Transactional document archiving is included. Standalone document management requires the DMS & Archiving Add-On, and additional archive storage requires that Add-On.

### Are Circularo professional services discounted?

Not under the standard partner product model. Services require a separate quotation or statement of work.

### Can partners sell their own services?

Yes, subject to the Partner Agreement, competence, scope, and customer commitments.

## Quoting and Circularo engagement

### When must a partner engage Circularo before issuing a proposal?

Circularo engagement is required for:

- Ultimate deployments
- Self-hosted or private cloud deployments
- Government or sovereign cloud opportunities
- White-label or OEM opportunities
- Opportunities above the Partner Quoting Limits
- Custom pricing or commercial terms
- Product roadmap commitments
- Custom development
- Regulatory or legal exceptions

When in doubt, contact your Circularo Channel Manager before issuing a quotation.

### Can a partner choose a lower plan to stay within its quoting limit?

No. The recommended plan and subscription model must reflect the customer's actual requirements. Opportunities exceeding the applicable limit must be jointly managed with Circularo.

## Payment and reporting

### Who invoices the customer?

The Reseller or Master Distributor normally invoices the customer.

### What if the customer pays late?

The partner must still pay Circularo according to the agreed payment terms.

### What reporting is required?

End customer, customer sell price, products, quantities, term, registration ID, renewal owner, and downstream reseller where applicable.

### Are partner buy prices confidential?

Yes. They must not appear on customer quotations.

## Distributor questions

### Can a Master Distributor sell directly?

Yes, where its agreement permits.

### Can a Master Distributor work through downstream Resellers?

Yes. The downstream Reseller and end customer must be disclosed.

### Do both Distributor and Reseller receive the full margin?

No. One total approved channel economic envelope is divided according to responsibilities.

## Governance

### Who approves exceptional pricing?

The CSO.

### What documents govern the relationship?

The signed Partner Agreement and Order Form prevail. Program policies and guides explain the operating model.

### Are regional rules identical?

Not necessarily. Country-specific legal, tax, currency, and pricing rules may apply. Regional variations are `TBD`.
