# Investor preparation

Reusable investor context is maintained by subject across the shared library. Specific organizations and outreach stay in [investor relationships](../../../relationships/investors/README.md).

- [Investor materials index](investor-materials-master.md) — confidential draft executive view across company, product, finance, market and strategy.
- [Data quality and open questions](data-quality-and-open-questions.md) — unresolved evidence, source and approval gaps.
- [Investor evidence and handling rules](governance.md) — actuals, forecasts, scenarios, roadmap and publication boundaries.
- [Investor relationship template](../../templates/investor-relationship/README.md)
- [Generic investor materials](../../assets/investors/README.md) — current scaffold and drafting destinations.
- [Source register](../../../sources/README.md) and [original mandate](../../../sources/investor-context/setup.md).

The `investor-master:*` IDs survive relocation; that namespace does not grant master authority under the shared `record_role` model. All migrated context remains draft/confidential, with unassigned owners and review dates preserved as unknown. Do not turn a setup mandate or strategic thesis into a verified company or product claim.
