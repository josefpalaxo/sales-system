# **Circularo Positioning Framework**

Every sales conversation should begin by positioning **what Circularo is**.

## **Primary Positioning**

**Circularo is a Digital Trust Platform** that enables organizations to securely create, collaborate, approve, sign, execute, integrate, verify, and preserve trusted digital transactions throughout their entire lifecycle.


Unlike traditional e-signature solutions, Circularo combines document collaboration, workflow automation, digital signatures, identity services, archiving and evidence preservation, and long-term governance within a single integrated platform.

Circularo is an end-to-end Digital Trust Platform.

For commercial organizations, we position it around Trusted Execution.
For governments and regulated industries, we position it around Sovereign Trust Services.

## **Core Business Themes**

Every sales conversation should reinforce one or more of these themes:

* Trusted Execution  
* Identity  
* Authority  
* Evidence  
* Compliance  
* Governance  
* Sovereignty

Avoid positioning Circularo primarily as an electronic signature solution.

Electronic signatures are one capability within a much broader trusted execution platform.

## **End-to-End Platform**

Position Circularo as the platform that supports the complete lifecycle of trusted digital transactions.

From:

* Creating documents  
* Collaboration and co-authoring  
* Reviews and approvals  
* Digital signatures and seals  
* Identity verification  
* Workflow automation  
* API-driven execution  
* Evidence generation  
* Secure archiving  
* Long-term governance

All delivered through one unified platform with a consistent user experience and a unified REST API.

## **Two Sales Motions**

### **Trusted Execution (SaaS)**

Best suited for commercial organizations focused on digitizing business processes.

Emphasize:

* Digital signing & sealing
* Digital approvals
* Workflow automation
* Document collaboration
* Contract execution
* Secure archiving
* Evidence preservation
* API integration
* Business productivity
* Compliance

The emphasis is on business productivity, compliance, and trusted execution.

### **Sovereign Trust Services (Self-Hosted)**

**Circularo Sovereign Trust Services enable organizations or entire governments to own, operate, and govern their own  digital trust services under their complete control.**

Best suited for governments, regulated industries, and organizations requiring full operational control.

Emphasize:

* Digital sovereignty
* Trusted execution
* Identity
* Authority
* Governance
* Compliance
* National trust infrastructure
* Shared services
* Customer-controlled operations

Typical deployment:

* Dedicated Sovereign Platform
* Sovereign Shared Services Platform

## **Positioning Principles**

Always position Circularo as:

- A Digital Trust Platform
- An end-to-end digital execution platform
- More than eSignatures
- Easy to use
- Easy to integrate
- API-first
- Identity-ready
- Built for compliance
- Built for governance
- Designed to evolve into broader digital trust services

Never position Circularo as:

- Just another eSigning solution
- Just another workflow product
- A document management system only
- An on-premise alternative to SaaS
