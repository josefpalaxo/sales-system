# Support Plans and Services

Essential Support is included in every Subscription. Standard, Plus, and Premium are optional support upgrades.

Onboarding is a one-time service. Implementation is project-based or delivered through a service-based Add-On. Self-hosted operations use the applicable standard plan and maintenance arrangement.
