# Shared commercial knowledge

This area contains structured representations of product, edition, plan, subscription, deployment, add-on, support, and cross-cutting entitlement rules used by all sales domains.

The active [Commercial Model](<../../master-documents/Circularo Commercial Model.md>) and [Add-On Model](<../../master-documents/Circularo Add-On Model and How it Works.md>) are the governing full master documents. These smaller records support agent use and must remain reconciled with the master revision identified in their metadata. Current approval, review dates, and recorded conflicts still constrain reuse.

Precise limits and eligibility rules must be structured in their authoritative record and referenced elsewhere by stable ID.
