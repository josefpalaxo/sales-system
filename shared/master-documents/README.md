## Authority and maintenance

Edit the full master first for changes to its business definition. Preserve its stable ID, increment its revision for semantic changes, and reconcile its derived records. Do not advance a derived record's `master_revision` until that representation has been checked against the revised master. Open conflicts and review dates continue to constrain reuse.

The original sales-repo Commercial Model differs from the approved sales-system revision and is preserved as [historical comparison](../../sources/archived-snapshots/sales-repo-commercial-model-original.md). It is not a second active master. The original source folder remains untouched. See the [migration report](../../work/commercial-master-migration/README.md) for file coverage and limitations.
