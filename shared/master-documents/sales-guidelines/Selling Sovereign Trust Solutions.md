# **Selling Sovereign Trust Solutions**

## **Purpose**

This guide explains how to position and sell **Circularo Sovereign Trust Solutions**.

Unlike traditional SaaS offerings, Sovereign Trust Solutions enable organizations to own, operate, and govern their own digital trust services while maintaining full control over their data, identity, security, compliance, and operations.

The platform can be deployed for a single organization or operated as a sovereign shared service supporting multiple independent organizations through a common, governed trust infrastructure.

Remember

Customers are not buying self-hosting.

They are investing in a Sovereign Trust Platform that they own, operate, and govern according to their own security, compliance, and operational requirements.

# **When to Position a Sovereign Trust Solution**

Position a Sovereign Trust Solution when customers require:

* Digital sovereignty  
* Data residency  
* Regulatory compliance  
* Customer-controlled infrastructure  
* Security and governance  
* Integration with national identity and trust services  
* Shared digital services for multiple organizations

Do **not** position a Sovereign Trust Solution simply because a customer prefers on-premise software.

# **Deployment Models**

## **Dedicated Sovereign Platform (Single Tenant)**

A dedicated sovereign deployment operated for a single organization.

### **Best for**

* Governments  
* Ministries  
* Banks  
* Healthcare providers  
* Critical infrastructure operators  
* Regulated enterprises  
* Large organizations requiring complete operational control

### **Customer Controls**

* Infrastructure  
* Data  
* Identity  
* Encryption keys  
* Security policies  
* Compliance  
* Data residency  
* Governance  
* Operations


## **Sovereign Shared Services Platform (Multi-Tenant)**

A sovereign platform operated by one organization to deliver trusted digital services to multiple independent organizations through a single governed environment.

### **Typical Operators**

* Governments  
* Digital Government Authorities  
* National Trust Service Providers  
* Cloud Providers  
* Telcos  
* Shared Service Centers  
* Large Holdings  
* System Integrators

### **Proven Deployments**

* GovSign  
* DigitalSign  
* Sharjah Sign  
* Mokham  
* Moro Sign

Each customer organization operates independently while sharing the same platform, with complete logical separation of:

* Organizations  
* Users  
* Documents  
* Workflows  
* Administration  
* Data  
* Permissions

This enables operators to deliver trusted digital services at national or enterprise scale while maintaining centralized governance, operational efficiency, and a consistent customer experience.

# **Positioning**

## **Position as**

* Sovereign Trust Platform  
* National Digital Trust Infrastructure  
* Enterprise Trust Platform  
* Government Shared Services Platform  
* Foundation for Digital Trust Services

## **Do Not Position as**

* A digital signature solution
* “SaaS installed on-premise”  
* Another hosting option  
* A digital signature server  
* An infrastructure deployment

Customers are investing in a **Sovereign Trust Platform**, not simply where the software runs.

# **Core Sales Message**

**Circularo Sovereign Trust Platform enable organizations or entire governments to own, operate, and govern their own  digital trust services under their complete control.**

Customers typically begin with trusted digital signing and approvals, then expand over time into document management, collaboration, AI, identity verification, and additional digital trust services—all on the same sovereign platform without replacing the underlying infrastructure.
