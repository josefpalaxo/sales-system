# Circularo Partner Pricing & Special Pricing Guide

## Purpose

This guide explains how Circularo partner pricing works, including published list prices, partner buy prices, customer pricing, partner margins, customer discounts, and special pricing approvals.

It complements the Circularo Partner Program Guide and Partner Agreement.

In the event of any inconsistency, the signed Partner Agreement and applicable Order Form prevail.

## 1. Commercial Principles

Circularo partners purchase subscriptions from Circularo and independently resell them to end customers.

Partners are responsible for:

- Setting the final customer price.
- Negotiating commercial terms with customers.
- Managing their own commercial margin.
- Complying with all applicable pricing, competition, and reseller laws.

Circularo publishes recommended list prices and confidential partner buy prices but does not prescribe the final resale price.


## 2. Commercial Terms

### Published List Price (MSRP)

Circularo's published Manufacturer's Suggested Retail Price (MSRP) for a subscription, Add-On, or service before any customer discount.

Published List Prices are used as the commercial reference for quotations, partner pricing, and discount approvals.

The applicable price basis is stated for each product:

| Price Basis | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Annual | Annual subscription or entitlement |
| Per Event | Each chargeable consumption event |
| Per Pack | A stated pack of events or credits |
| One-Time | A finite service or explicitly one-time item |

Only Annual prices may be converted to a monthly equivalent by dividing by 12. Per Event, Per Pack, and One-Time prices must not be annualized in this way.

### Partner Buy Price (Net Price)

The confidential net price charged by Circularo to the partner.

Partner Buy Prices are confidential and must not be disclosed to customers or other partners.

### Customer Discount

The percentage discount applied by the partner to the Published List Price.

**Formula**

Customer Discount (%) = (Published List Price − Customer Sell Price) ÷ Published List Price × 100

### Customer Sell Price

The final resale price determined independently by the partner.

Partners are solely responsible for customer pricing and any discounts they choose to offer.


**Formula**

Customer Sell Price = Published List Price × (1 − Customer Discount)


### Realized Partner Gross Margin

The partner's actual commercial margin after applying any customer discount.

Realized margin depends on:

- Partner Buy Price
- Customer Sell Price

Customer discounts reduce the partner's realized gross margin unless otherwise approved by Circularo.

**Formula**

Realized Partner Gross Margin (%) = (Customer Sell Price − Partner Buy Price) ÷ Customer Sell Price × 100

Customer discounts reduce the realized partner gross margin unless Circularo approves additional special pricing.

### Example

| Item | Amount |
|------|-------:|
| Published List Price | USD 100.00 |
| Customer Discount | 20% |
| Customer Sell Price | USD 80.00 |
| Partner Buy Price | USD 64.00 |
| Realized Partner Gross Margin | 20% |

Calculation:

Customer Sell Price = USD 100 × (1 − 20%) = USD 80

Realized Partner Gross Margin = (USD 80 − USD 64) ÷ USD 80 = 20%


## 3. Standard Partner Margin Guidelines

Circularo designs partner pricing to support approximately **20% realized gross margin** on typical commercial opportunities.

Maximum standard realized margins are determined by both:

- Partner Tier
- Product Plan

The lower applicable limit always applies.

### Partner Tier Guidelines

| Partner Tier | Maximum Standard Realized Margin |
|--------------|---------------------------------:|
| Authorized | 15% |
| Advanced | 20% |
| Strategic | 30% |

### Plan Guidelines

| Plan | Maximum Standard Realized Margin |
|------|---------------------------------:|
| Start | 10% |
| Pro | 15% |
| Business | 30% |
| Enterprise | 30% |

#### Examples

| Partner Tier | Plan | Maximum Standard Margin |
|--------------|------|------------------------:|
| Strategic | Start | 10% |
| Strategic | Pro | 15% |
| Advanced | Business | 20% |
| Strategic | Enterprise | 30% |

These margins are maximum program guidelines and are not guaranteed.

Actual realized margin depends on the customer's negotiated purchase price.

## 4. Customer Discount Authority

Partners may independently determine customer pricing within the following commercial framework.

| Customer Discount | Approval |
|------------------:|----------|
| 0–10% | Partner discretion |
| >10–20% | Normal commercial discount – reported and tracked |
| >20–35% | Special Pricing approval required |
| >35–50% | CSO approval required |
| Above 50% | Outside the standard partner program |

Customer discounts apply to the published list price.

Approval of a customer discount does not automatically increase the partner's commercial margin.

Circularo may use internal customer-price approval thresholds to determine when additional approval is required. These thresholds are internal governance controls only. They do not prescribe, fix, or restrict the resale price independently determined by the partner.

## 5. Special Pricing

Circularo may approve special pricing for opportunities with strategic commercial value.

Examples include:

- Enterprise-wide deployments
- Multi-year agreements
- Competitive replacement opportunities
- New customer acquisition
- Strategic reference customers
- High-volume commercial opportunities
- Public tenders
- Framework agreements

Special pricing is evaluated individually and is not automatically available.

Registered Deal Buy Prices are determined case by case. They are not standard price-book entitlements and must not be assumed from a previous opportunity, partner tier, or customer discount.

## 6. Strategic Opportunity Exception

The Chief Sales Officer (CSO) may approve up to **40% realized first-year gross margin** when all of the following conditions are satisfied:

- The partner is a Strategic Partner.
- The opportunity is a new customer.
- The opportunity has been properly registered.
- The plan is Business or Enterprise.
- The opportunity demonstrates strategic commercial value.
- Circularo commercial objectives are satisfied.

Unless otherwise approved, renewal pricing returns to the applicable standard partner margin guidelines.

This exception sits outside standard partner pricing and requires explicit case-by-case CSO approval. It is not generated automatically by the standard partner price book.

## 7. Margin Protection

Approved special pricing applies only to the specific registered opportunity for the approved commercial period.

Commercial protection is normally provided only for opportunities that have been properly registered in accordance with the Circularo Partner Program.

Circularo reserves the right to decline special pricing for opportunities that are not registered.

## 8. Pricing Governance

Partners must not:

- Disclose confidential Partner Buy Prices.
- Represent Partner Buy Prices as published customer prices.
- Offer pricing outside their approved authority.
- Commit Circularo to commercial terms without approval.
- Modify licensing rules or subscription models.
- Promise future pricing or commercial exceptions.
- Treat a zero, blank, draft, or placeholder list price as an approved free product.
- Convert Per Event, Per Pack, or One-Time prices into monthly subscription prices.

Any non-standard commercial request must be approved by Circularo before being offered to the customer.


## 9. Frequently Asked Questions

#### Can partners choose their own customer price?

Yes.

Partners independently determine the final resale price, subject to applicable laws and the Circularo Partner Program.

---

#### Does a higher customer discount automatically increase my buy discount?

No.

Special pricing is evaluated independently by Circularo.

---

#### Can a partner reduce its own margin?

Yes.

Partners may choose to reduce their own commercial margin in order to win business.

---

#### Does every opportunity qualify for special pricing?

No.

Special pricing is discretionary and depends on the commercial merits of each opportunity.

---

#### Is special pricing available without deal registration?

Generally, no.

Registered opportunities receive priority consideration for commercial protection and special pricing approvals.
