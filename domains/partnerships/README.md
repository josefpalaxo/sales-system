# Partnerships

Partner-led and partner-assisted sales motions, including partner strategy, ideal partner profiles, program design, recruitment, onboarding, enablement, activation, co-sell, referrals, resale, services, technology alliances, deal registration, attribution, and conflict management.

Partnerships may introduce domain-specific entities such as partner types, ideal partner profiles, partner programs, benefits, requirements, tiers, routes to market, and partner plays. Shared company, product, commercial, customer-market, proof, and positioning truth remains under `shared/`.

The [partner master document library](master-documents/README.md) holds the imported program, operations, pricing, partner enablement, CSVs, and workbook package from sales-repo. These materials retain their draft limitations and require the relevant owner approvals before use as program policy or externally. Shared Commercial Model and Add-On Model definitions remain under `shared/master-documents/`.
