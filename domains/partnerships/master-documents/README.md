# Partnership master document library

The [partner-management library](partner-management/README.md) contains the full partner-program and pricing documents imported from sales-repo. They remain draft proposals requiring owner, Finance, and applicable legal review; no price, margin, registration right, or external publication is approved by migration.

## Start here

- [Program policy](<partner-management/Circularo Partner Program Policy.md>) and [operations guide](<partner-management/Circularo Partner Program Operations Guide.md>).
- [Pricing architecture](<partner-management/Circularo Partner Pricing Strategy Architecture.md>) and [internal price book](<partner-management/Circularo Internal Partner Price Book.md>).
- [Partner program guide](<partner-management/Circularo Partner Program Guide.md>), [FAQ](<partner-management/Circularo Partner Program FAQ.md>), [special-pricing guide](<partner-management/Circularo Partner Pricing & Special Pricing Guide.md>), and [deal-registration guide](<partner-management/Circularo Partner Deal Registration Guide.md>).
- [SaaS partner sales catalog](<partner-management/Circularo SaaS Partner Sales Catalog.md>) and [selling guide](<partner-management/Selling Circularo SaaS as a Partner.md>).
- [Base price CSV](partner-management/partner-facing-price-list-saas.csv) and [add-on price CSV](partner-management/partner-facing-addon-pricelist-saas.csv) — preserved source data, not newly approved prices.
- [Workbook package](partner-management/pricing-book/README.md) — specification, documentation, source generator, and original workbook. The source unified pricing catalogue is not included, so the package is not yet self-contained for rebuilding.

Internal price-book documents, workbook, generator, and workbook documentation are restricted. Partner-facing intended audiences in the source do not authorize external sharing. See the [migration report](../../../work/commercial-master-migration/README.md).

Shared business definitions remain in the [Commercial Model](<../../../shared/master-documents/Circularo Commercial Model.md>) and [Add-On Model](<../../../shared/master-documents/Circularo Add-On Model and How it Works.md>). These documents do not inherit approval from either master merely by referring to it.
