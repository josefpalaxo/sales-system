# Circularo Partner Program Guide

## Welcome

The Circularo Partner Program helps Resellers and Master Distributors build recurring business around Circularo's Digital Trust Platform.

Partners can sell Circularo SaaS subscriptions and eligible add-ons, manage customer relationships, and earn recurring economics by supporting customer adoption and renewals.

This guide summarizes the program. The signed Partner Agreement and Order Form prevail.

## Partner types

### Reseller

A Reseller markets and sells Circularo, invoices the customer, coordinates onboarding and support, and manages renewal activity.

### Master Distributor

A Master Distributor may sell directly or supply approved downstream Resellers. It may provide local billing, credit, enablement, and channel management.

Every Reseller and Master Distributor is assigned one program tier.

## Program tiers

| Tier | Designed for | Indicative recurring economics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Authorized | Partners beginning to build Circularo capability and pipeline | 10–15% |
| Advanced | Partners delivering consistent opportunities, customer management, and renewals | 15–20% |
| Strategic | Highest-performing partners with strong volume, forecasting, and customer-success capability | 20–30% |

The figures are potential realized gross margins after customer discount. They are ceilings, not guaranteed discounts or earnings.

Exact tier thresholds and certification requirements are `TBD`.

## What partners can sell

The initial SaaS program covers:

- Start
- Pro
- Business
- Enterprise
- Eligible feature add-ons
- Consumption and trust services
- Certificates and storage

Circularo professional services are quoted separately and are not part of standard partner margin.

Ultimate and self-hosted opportunities are always joint Circularo engagements and cannot be quoted independently by partners.

## How pricing works

Circularo uses:

1. A customer list price
2. A confidential partner buy price
3. Special registered-deal pricing where justified

The partner sets its customer price independently.

Typical realized partner gross margin is approximately 20%. Maximum recurring margin is limited by both the partner tier and the selected plan.

Each product states its price basis:

| Price Basis | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Annual | Annual subscription or entitlement |
| Per Event | Each chargeable consumption event |
| Per Pack | A stated pack of events or credits |
| One-Time | A finite service or explicitly one-time item |

Only Annual prices may be converted to monthly equivalents by dividing by 12.

Special registered-deal pricing is determined case by case. It is not a standard tier entitlement and must not be assumed from another opportunity.

## Plan margin limits

| Plan | Maximum realized recurring partner gross margin |
| --- | ---: |
| Start | 10% |
| Pro | 15% |
| Business | 30% |
| Enterprise | 30% |

The effective ceiling is the lower of the plan ceiling and partner-tier ceiling.

A Strategic Business or Enterprise opportunity may receive first-year economics up to 40% only when:

- The partner is a Strategic Partner.
- The opportunity is a new customer.
- The opportunity is properly registered.
- The opportunity demonstrates strategic commercial value.
- The CSO explicitly approves the exception.

This exception is case by case, sits outside standard pricing, and does not automatically apply to renewal.

## Deal registration

Register opportunities early and completely.

The first complete registration accepted by Circularo receives protection for up to six months.

An active registered deal receives:

- Protection for the registered opportunity and scope
- The best Circularo-funded price or discount relative to later partners pursuing a materially comparable offer
- Protection against Circularo undercutting the registered partner on materially comparable terms

The partner must keep the registration current and demonstrate progress.

Circularo may cancel or amend protection if the deal becomes inactive, information is inaccurate, scope changes materially, the customer requests another route, or the partner is not in good standing.

## Customer discounts

Circularo list price is the recommended market reference.

Ordinary customer discounts are expected to remain within 20% of list. Larger strategic or volume discounts require registered-deal pricing and Circularo review. Exceptional discounts may reach 50% with CSO approval.

These rules govern Circularo's funding and approval. Partners remain responsible for setting their resale price in accordance with applicable law.

## Renewals

Recurring margin is earned through recurring work.

Partners retain renewal economics when they actively:

- Manage the customer relationship
- Support adoption
- Coordinate first-line support
- Forecast and manage renewal
- Keep account information current
- Remain in good standing

If Circularo takes responsibility for the customer lifecycle and renewal, renewal margin may be reduced or removed.

## Payment responsibilities

The partner invoices the customer and carries customer collection risk.

Circularo must be paid according to the agreed payment terms regardless of whether the partner has collected from the customer.

Credit terms and limits are assigned separately.

## Partner responsibilities

Partners must:

- Position Circularo accurately as a Digital Trust Platform
- Use approved product, plan, and pricing materials
- Use the stated Annual, Per Event, Per Pack, or One-Time price basis correctly
- Sell only permitted plan and add-on combinations
- Protect confidential partner pricing
- Report end-customer and sell-through information
- Keep registered deals current
- Deliver agreed customer-management activities
- Pay Circularo on time
- Comply with applicable law and the Partner Agreement

## Circularo commitments

Circularo will:

- Maintain approved product and price information
- Provide enablement and program guidance
- Review complete deal registrations
- Protect accepted registrations under the program rules
- Avoid undercutting an active registered partner on materially comparable terms
- Apply tier and pricing rules consistently
- Support approved customer opportunities
