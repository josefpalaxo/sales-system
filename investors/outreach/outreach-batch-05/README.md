# Circularo adviser outreach — batch 05

**Batch 05 is an adviser search, not another direct-investor list.** The research screens **30 distinct firms** and proposes **six immediate qualification conversations**, **six alternatives** and **eighteen opportunistic routes**. Twelve serious candidates have detailed profiles and transaction evidence. The recommendation is to interview advisers before appointing one lead; no firm has yet accepted the mandate or provided engagement economics.

## Confirmed transaction brief

**Minority is preferred; majority ownership and operational control are also acceptable**, confirmed by Josef for this batch. USD 10–15M and approximately 25% are indicative starting points, not caps. Founder liquidity through a secondary component is mandatory; mixed primary/secondary is possible. The prior USD 4M total-investment screening floor is retained, with no fixed upper amount. A full 100% sale is outside the current supplied task.

Jahani is a relationship reactivation: Josef reports contact in **2021**. The person, exact date and outcome are unknown. The [updated task](taks.md) preserves those distinctions and qualifies Circularo's financial, customer and product-maturity context.
