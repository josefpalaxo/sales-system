## Qualification before an engagement

Ask for written answers or deal-specific references, rather than a generic credentials deck:

- **Mandate:** will you represent the selling shareholders/company in a minority-first process with mandatory founder liquidity? Would the same team support a majority/control alternative? A pure primary fundraising pitch fails the mandate.
- **Comparable cases:** three named recent software cases near our economic scale, their stake/structure, primary versus secondary, founder cash at closing, any deferred/contingent consideration and your precise role. Speak to at least two seller references if a mandate is seriously considered.
- **Buyers:** who personally owns relationships with relevant UAE/GCC operating companies or strategic holding groups? Ask for completed examples and categories of decision-makers; do not claim privileged access to e&, G42, Mubadala or others without evidence.
- **Execution:** named lead partner, regional counterpart, working team, estimated allocation of senior time, and a concrete plan for GCC and international approaches. Clarify whether a Dubai partner/member firm is a separate contracting entity.
- **Economics:** retainer, credit against success fee, minimum success fee, percentage/tier basis, VAT and reimbursable expenses. Specify whether the fee uses equity value, enterprise value, all capital raised, debt assumed, founder cash, rollover or contingent proceeds. Request minority-secondary and majority examples separately.
- **Terms:** term, exclusivity scope, milestones, termination rights, tail duration and covered buyer list, treatment of deferred/earn-out receipts, abort costs and expenses cap.
- **Pre-existing relationships:** attach a schedule drawn from all prior batches and the central register, including founders' prior strategic relationships and Jahani's history. Agree fee treatment and responsibilities for these names before signing.
- **Conflicts:** buyer-side mandates, prior advisory work, competing seller mandates and investment-arm interests. Separate the role of an adviser from a related fund's potential investment.
- **Entity and permission:** identify the exact legal entity, geography, broker-dealer/member-firm/co-adviser chain and applicable permission for the actual activity. Verify current public-register status and references before appointment. A firm footer, auditor licence or valuation licence alone does not answer this.

Current values for all these commercial engagement terms are **unknown**; this research has not obtained fee proposals. Compare expected **net founder cash**, not only headline transaction value. Do not assume a small-ticket adviser is inexpensive.

## Selection and process design

A practical process may use one technology lead with a specifically accountable GCC relationship partner. A second adviser is useful only with clearly divided origination responsibilities, a buyer list, combined fee economics and one execution owner. Avoid two overlapping exclusive mandates.

Request a short written mandate proposal after the qualification call: target shareholder categories, structure options, references, named team, deliverables, sequence and economics. Compare proposals qualitatively. Keep minority and majority alternatives visible; do not change to a full 100% sale by implication.

Begin buyer research with UAE/GCC strategic operating groups and international identity, security, enterprise-workflow and PE-backed software platforms. Use international interest to test strategic value, without assuming a UAE party will necessarily be the final shareholder. Private equity may contribute operating capabilities, but require a concrete strategic contribution rather than just financial capital.

Before a formal process, prepare a defined recurring-revenue bridge and financial period, cap table and founder liquidity objectives, customer concentration/retention, regional reference rights, IP and entity ownership, deployment/sovereignty constraints and product maturity. These are diligence inputs, not facts assumed in this package.
