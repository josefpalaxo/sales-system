# Adviser longlist — batch 05

Research date: **3 October 2026**. **30 distinct current adviser platforms: six Tier A, six Tier B, eighteen Tier C.** Tiers recommend qualification conversations; they are not scores or evidence of mandate acceptance. A/B have [detailed profiles](adviser-profiles.md); C has a preliminary service and fit screen only.

Use the [confirmed brief](taks.md): minority preferred, majority/control acceptable, mandatory founder liquidity, flexible amount and ownership. Published deal values below must not be confused with founder cash or a firm's minimum. No current fee proposal has been obtained.
