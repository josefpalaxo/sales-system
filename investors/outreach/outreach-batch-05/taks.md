# Circularo strategic transaction adviser search

## Objective

Research and produce a high-quality shortlist of investment banks / M&A advisers that could represent Circularo in finding a **strategic shareholder**, with a strategic minority transaction as the preferred starting discussion rather than a fixed ownership cap.

This is **not a conventional VC fundraising mandate** and not currently a 100% company sale.

### Proposed transaction

- Company: **Circularo**
- HQ: Dubai, UAE / DIFC
- Business: Enterprise digital trust, eSignatures, trusted document workflows, sovereign shared-service platforms, AI/eDocs and emerging Agentic Trusted Execution
- Business scale for adviser screening: approximately **USD 3M recurring revenue/ARR as described in the supplied brief**; definition, reporting date, reconciliation and external-use approval remain unconfirmed
- Profitability, founder ownership and no external funding are management-reported context pending supporting records
- Transaction sought: approximately **USD 10–15M as an indicative discussion range**, flexible rather than a target that excludes other suitable mandates
- Equity: approximately **25% as an indicative minority starting point**, not a fixed ceiling
- **A founder-secondary component is mandatory**; primarily secondary is preferred, but a mixed primary/secondary structure may be considered
- **Minority is preferred; majority ownership and operational control are also acceptable**, reconfirmed by Josef for batch 05 on 2026-10-03. Assess these routes separately rather than assuming a fixed 25% cap; any engagement still needs defined commercial terms
- Carry forward the existing **USD 4M minimum total investment** for screening; no fixed upper investment limit. The founder proceeds within that total must be stated separately
- Potential future liquidity / private equity/ IPO pathway
- Preference for a **strategic shareholder rather than conventional VC**
- Strong preference for an Emirati/UAE strategic partner if the right one can be identified

The strategic shareholder should ideally help Circularo materially accelerate revenue through its ecosystem, customer base, government relationships, technology portfolio or international distribution.

## Why Circularo is unusual

Do not treat Circularo as generic ~$3M ARR SaaS.

The supplied brief reports strategic infrastructure positions in the GCC, including the examples below. They are internal positioning context, not independently verified or approved customer/deployment claims. Check the investor master and reference rights before using named evidence externally:

- TDRA / GovSign — UAE federal government shared-service deployment
- Digital Dubai / DigitalSign — sovereign shared-service platform for Dubai government entities
- Sharjah Sign — government shared-service deployment
- e& / DigiSign — telco/service-provider distribution model
- TCC / Mokham — Saudi Arabia strategic partnership
- Enterprise customers including ICD, EDGE, RTA, Emaar and others

Circularo has evolved beyond conventional eSignature positioning toward:

**Digital Signatures → Digital Trust → Trusted Execution → Agentic Trusted Execution**

Longer-term technology thesis includes the concepts below. Preserve production, roadmap and vision distinctions; do not present eDocs or Agentic Trusted Execution as shipped solely because they appear here:

- Trust Orchestration
- Trusted Records
- eDocs / canonical trust objects
- identity + authority + evidence
- AI agent authorization
- trusted execution for autonomous agents

The adviser therefore needs to understand how to sell the **strategic value and platform optionality**, rather than valuing Circularo simply as a small eSignature SaaS company.

# Critical Analysis

Use the brief's reported approximately USD 3M business scale and indicative USD 10–15M transaction as screening context, without implying verified current ARR or fixed terms.

Determine whether the mandate is large enough for them.

### Can they reach the buyers we care about?

Especially UAE strategic parties.

Distinguish between:

"has a Dubai office"

and

"has demonstrated senior UAE strategic transaction relationships."

### Do they understand Circularo?

Prefer advisers capable of positioning Circularo as:

**sovereign digital-trust infrastructure / enterprise software / trusted execution**

rather than simply:

**small eSignature SaaS company.**

### Can they create international competitive tension?

The ideal adviser should potentially approach both:

**UAE/GCC strategic investors**
AND
**international enterprise software / cybersecurity / identity / PE-backed strategic buyers.**

This is important because international interest can establish valuation tension even if our preferred final shareholder is Emirati.
