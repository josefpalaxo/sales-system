## What to say and qualify in the room

Suggested conversational positioning, grounded in the draft strategic narrative and subject to Josef's review:

> Circularo is focused on digital trust around business document transactions—from approvals to signing. Our strategic direction is trusted transaction infrastructure for AI-driven workflows. We’re looking for a partner who adds market access or technology value as well as investment.

Keep current capability separate from future AI/agentic direction. Omit unapproved revenue, customer, compliance and production-agentic claims.

In a substantive private conversation, ask: **“Would your mandate accommodate founder liquidity through a secondary component, potentially within a majority or control transaction?”** If yes, clarify minimum company size, geography/entity requirements, total investment versus founder proceeds, decision-maker and timing. If no, ask for a named growth, buyout, strategic-acquirer or secondary-capable introduction.

A useful trip target is **three to five qualified private conversations**, supported by a few carefully chosen events. This is a planning target, not a forecast. Record whether each contact is a buyer, investor, introducer or operator; do not mark enthusiasm as investment interest. Follow up with a specific next step within one business day after a real conversation.
