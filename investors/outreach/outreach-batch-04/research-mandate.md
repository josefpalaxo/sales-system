| Investment ticket | At least USD 4m total investment; no fixed upper amount |
| Founder liquidity | Required secondary component purchasing existing founders' shares; a primary-only round does not qualify |

| Strategic contribution | Enterprise access, international distribution, operating expertise, digital trust and trusted transactions |
| Travel dates | October 8–15, 2026; Tech Week overlap October 12–15 |
| Unknown logistics | Arrival/departure times, accommodation area, available slots and meeting availability |
| External actions | Research and drafts only; no sending/contact requests or registration authorised |
