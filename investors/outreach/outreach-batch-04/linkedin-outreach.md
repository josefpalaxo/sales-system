# LinkedIn approach and Los Angeles meeting drafts

**Drafts for Josef to review and send; nothing has been sent.** Travel dates are October 8–15, 2026. Offer [two LA-time slots] after interest and checking actual availability. These messages use positioning direction and investment intentions, not verified metrics or a commitment to sell. They omit customer names, profitability claims, valuation and production agentic claims.

For six additional named event speakers and Tech Week-specific messages, see the [event plan](tech-week-plan.md). Individual event attendance remains organiser-reported, not personally confirmed.

## Recommended approach

1. Start now with K1, Arrowroot and Marlin, plus March and Greycroft for exploratory enterprise/AI conversations. Check the shortlist gates before approaching Composition or Diversis for a deal meeting.
2. Open each named profile and inspect mutual connections. Thomas Oh and Peter Chung are already shown as second degree; seek an introduction from someone Josef actually knows. Nishita Cummings, Ron Nayot and Kobie Fuller are other second-degree routes. Relationship strength remains unknown.
3. If already connected, use a direct message. Otherwise use a short personalised connection note, or InMail if available in the account. Follow the UI's actual channel and limits; a displayed Message button alone does not establish cost or availability.
4. Personalise the reason using the investor's stated focus, keep the digital-trust proposition concrete, and ask for a 20-minute conversation tied to the trip. Choose one initial contact per firm; use alternatives when appropriate.
5. Qualify founder secondary and company scale in the first substantive exchange. For VC, ask for a suitable vehicle or introduction if the secondary structure is outside remit. Do not describe the transaction as an ordinary primary-only fundraising round.
6. Offer two precise slots in Los Angeles local time when a person responds. Make one polite follow-up after roughly two business days if the trip is approaching; avoid repeated nudges or duplicate approaches to colleagues.

These are practical recommendations, not claims that LinkedIn delivers meetings or that a connection request is authorised. [Contacts](contacts.md) and [the shortlist](investor-shortlist.md) show evidence and unresolved gates.
