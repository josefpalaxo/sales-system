# Circularo investor outreach batches

All outreach batches and the central contact register now live in `investors/outreach/`, moved from `investors/work/` on 2026-10-03. Josef explicitly requested Git versioning for all outreach artifacts, including saved conversations. See [relocation checks and tracking policy](relocation-verification.md).

Keep each investor selection with the mandate, evidence and recommendations that produced it. Batch numbering separates research packages or sourcing approaches; it does not imply that outreach was sent or that investor interest was confirmed.

**Track actual outreach in the [central contact register](contact-register/README.md)**: 86 people across all four batches, editable activity history and saved LinkedIn conversations. Eight messages sent by Josef on October 3 are recorded; no replies were visible at capture.
