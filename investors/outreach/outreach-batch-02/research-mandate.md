# Investor research mandate for outreach batch 02

Source: Josef's instruction in this conversation on 2 October 2026. These are research-selection parameters, not approved or agreed transaction terms.

| Dimension | Mandate |
| --- | --- |
| Investor route | Non-VC; operating companies, holding/principal groups, growth equity and private equity |
| Minimum investment ticket | **USD 4 million** |
| Maximum ticket | **Not fixed** |
| Ownership percentage | **Not fixed; the previous 25% cap is removed** |
| Secondary component | **Required in every acceptable transaction**, providing liquidity to founders |
| Primary component | May accompany secondary; primary-only is excluded |
| Minimum cash to founders | Not specified separately from total investment ticket |
| Geographic priority | US, Europe and UAE/GCC remain equally important |
| Strategic objective | Business/technology advantage, enterprise access, distribution and international expansion |
| Control / founder independence | No fixed equity percentage; prior preference for founder-led independence retained, with control/rollover terms to be resolved |
| Excluded small tickets | Routes limited to less than USD 4m are not eligible; small-ticket policies must be evidenced rather than inferred |
| Conventional VC routes | Excluded; a parent's non-VC corporate-development route is assessed separately where relevant |

## Screening method

Build a practical first screen with eight candidates per main region, including operating groups as actively as financial investors. This focused screen is not a claim to exhaust the investor universe. Prior ANZ, Asian and global routes remain preserved in batch 01 for later assessment; they are not disqualified by geography.

Assess strategic value and transaction feasibility separately. Public investment/acquisition history can establish activity but cannot establish willingness to buy founder shares in Circularo. Record:

1. Explicit shareholder-liquidity or recapitalisation policy, if published.
2. Evidence of purchases from existing owners, with any reinvestment/rollover distinguished from a standalone minority investment.
3. Primary-only precedents without upgrading them to secondary support.
4. Published cheque, revenue, profitability, domicile and control criteria; keep enterprise value separate from equity ticket.
5. Potential commercial/technology contribution, which remains a hypothesis until supported by an operating sponsor and commitments.

The USD 4m floor is a minimum, not a target valuation or a requirement that all shortlisted investors write USD 4m checks. No valuation is calculated from an unspecified ownership percentage. No current approved Circularo financial metrics or legal-entity evidence were used to claim that investor eligibility gates are met.

## Contact gate

Before external use, identify the eligible investor vehicle, decision-maker, secondary appetite, realistic cheque and governance expectations. Corporate candidates require both an operating sponsor and corporate-development/investment approval; a commercial partnership is not a share purchase.

Digital trust, sovereign trusted transactions and the future AI/agentic direction provide discovery context.
