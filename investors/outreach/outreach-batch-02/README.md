# Circularo investor outreach batch 02

This is a new **24-candidate screen** for a transaction that includes founder liquidity. It is not a VC fundraising list. The universe gives equal attention to **UAE/GCC, the United States and Europe: eight routes each**, with 12 operating-company routes and 12 private-equity/principal-investment routes. Sixteen organisations were reassessed from batch 01; eight were added. The regions describe sourcing routes, not automatic eligibility for Circularo's legal entity.

**Mandate:** investment ticket **at least USD 4 million**; mandatory purchase of existing founders' shares as part of the transaction; no fixed upper amount or ownership percentage. Primary growth capital may accompany secondary proceeds, but a primary-only transaction fails the mandate. The minimum is applied to the investment ticket; no separate minimum founder cash proceeds was supplied. Full mandate: [research mandate](research-mandate.md).

No candidate has confirmed willingness to transact with Circularo. Some publish shareholder-liquidity strategies; others only have acquisitions or primary-investment precedents. Every profile states that distinction and the next qualification gate. Existing founder-led independence is retained as a preference from batch 01; an uncapped percentage does not approve a control sale.
