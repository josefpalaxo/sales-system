# Lightspeed Venture Partners

**Disposition: prioritise qualification for enterprise/AI relevance and a documented secondary strategy; Circularo transaction fit remains unconfirmed.** Josef supplied [Lightspeed's website](https://lsvp.com/). Screening follows the [batch 03 mandate](../README.md#screening-mandate). No messages or connection requests have been sent.

## Why it may fit

The [current homepage](https://lsvp.com/) describes investment from seed through later stages and shows enterprise-security investments including Wiz, Cyera, Rubrik and Netskope. This is a plausible enterprise/security/AI investor rather than an investor whose interest must depend solely on eSignature SaaS.

Two more specific signals matter:

- **Reliable agent workflows:** [17 February 2026 Temporal investment announcement](https://lsvp.com/stories/announcing-our-investment-in-temporal-agents-make-mistakes-your-workflows-cant/) explains Lightspeed's interest in durable, recoverable execution for AI agents and enterprise processes. Anoushka Vaswani and Ravi Mhatre are among its authors. This creates a relevant discussion route for trusted execution and auditability, although Temporal's infrastructure proposition differs from Circularo's document-transaction proposition.
- **Identity risk:** [Lightspeed's Alloy profile](https://lsvp.com/company/alloy/) describes identity risk management for financial products and records its 2021 Series C investment. Identity, fraud and compliance are adjacent to digital trust; this does not establish that Circularo is an identity-verification or fraud-detection provider.

**Fit hypothesis:** Lightspeed could understand an enterprise platform combining document execution, evidence and trust, with a GCC sovereign-market opportunity and a longer-term agentic direction. Potential contributions include enterprise introductions, security/infrastructure expertise and help with international positioning. No portfolio integration, reseller commitment or GCC operating sponsor is established.

Use the [investor master](../../../master/investor-materials-master.md) to distinguish approved evidence from draft strategy, deployed capabilities from roadmap and actual GCC proof from a market thesis. Do not present Circularo as equivalent to Temporal or claim shipped agentic functionality from this adjacency.

## Secondary strategy and transaction evidence

Lightspeed's [14 June 2024 Capital Markets announcement](https://lsvp.com/stories/how-private-market-transactions-can-help-sustain-companies-for-the-long-haul/) explicitly describes identifying opportunities to purchase private-company stock and helping companies with employee liquidity. The [current Jack Fowler biography](https://lsvp.com/team-member/jack-fowler/) confirms that he leads Capital Markets and works on liquidity strategy and opportunistic secondary transactions. His prior Goldman Sachs work included secondary block trades involving founders; that is prior-employer experience, not a Lightspeed founder-liquidity policy.

Completed issuer-announced precedents strengthen the screen:

- **21 August 2024:** [Grafana announcement](https://grafana.com/press/2024/08/21/grafana-labs-soars-past-250m-arr-and-5000-customers-completes-270m-primary-and-secondary-transaction-and-named-a-leader-in-the-gartner-magic-quadrant-for-observability-platforms/) reports a roughly **USD 270m mixed primary/secondary transaction led by Lightspeed**, with proceeds to the company and certain stockholders. Lightspeed's allocation between primary and secondary is not disclosed.
- **30 September 2025:** [Grafana announcement](https://grafana.com/press/2025/09/30/grafana-labs-surpasses-400m-arr-and-7000-customers-gains-new-investors-to-accelerate-global-expansion/) reports a completed secondary transaction led by Ontario Teachers', with Lightspeed among the existing investors taking part. Lightspeed's amount and seller identities are not disclosed.

These establish a published secondary strategy and participation in completed secondary transactions. **They do not establish founder sale proceeds, a current minimum cheque or willingness to buy Circularo founders' shares.** Grafana was already a Lightspeed portfolio company; confirm whether comparable liquidity is available for a new investment.

## Next qualification and lessons

1. Qualify investment fit with an enterprise/growth sponsor and founder liquidity with Capital Markets, preferably as one coordinated discussion.
2. Confirm USD 4m+ total investment, founder-secondary allocation, new-company eligibility, vehicle, geography, company scale and governance terms.

Existing-portfolio liquidity may differ from new-investment liquidity.
