## Investment route and potential fit

Crescent's [private-equity page](https://www.crescentcap.com.au/private-equity) describes an active investment model with operational support. It also identifies a separately managed **Growth Fund** for smaller investments in the Australian/New Zealand lower mid-market. Equity routes are the relevant starting point; the firm's credit, listed-equity and evergreen investor-access products do not automatically provide the required founder-share transaction.

**Fit hypothesis:** operational support could help Circularo build its international enterprise sales organisation, strengthen execution and pursue acquisitions or channel development. This is a financial investor with operating resources; do not treat it as a SaaS reseller or committed technology partner. Ask for specific enterprise introductions and software-market expertise.

For historical technology context, [Orica's 20 December 2017 announcement](https://announcements.asx.com.au/asxpdf/20171220/pdf/43qb2dswdxds5y.pdf) records an agreement to acquire GroundProbe from Crescent. This supports past industrial-technology exposure, not a current SaaS/digital-identity specialism or a founder-secondary buying precedent. GroundProbe should not be presented as a current Crescent holding.

Frame Circularo around the wider digital-trust and trusted-document proposition while preserving current capability versus roadmap/vision boundaries in the [investor master](../../../master/investor-materials-master.md). No Circularo valuation, financial threshold or production AI capability is established by this screen.
