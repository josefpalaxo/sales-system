Screening follows the [batch 03 mandate](../README.md#screening-mandate). No messages or connection requests have been sent.

## Investment scope and potential contribution

The [official homepage](https://lightwarrior.com/) describes Light Warrior as the Sali family's family office and investment group, with public/private assets, property and operating businesses. Its [investment overview](https://lightwarrior.com/our-investments/) describes global investing and responsible-investment/ESG assessments.

The [Ventures page](https://lightwarrior.com/our-investments/ventures/) targets scalable operating businesses and identifies FMCG, wellness, media/entertainment and funds management as preferred sectors. It seeks proven management, differentiation, a credible financial/capital plan, substantial ownership and an opportunity to contribute its expertise. Listed examples include Anthem, myDNA and OurCrowd. These illustrate breadth; they do not establish a current enterprise-software or digital-trust mandate. The page is undated in its body, and listed investments need a current-status check.

**Fit hypotheses:** founder/operator experience and an Australian business network could help Circularo build local relationships and sharpen its expansion plan. A family-office structure may permit an individual discussion of primary and secondary capital, but flexibility is not established by the published evidence. Ask what specific enterprise introductions, operating resources or co-investment support it could contribute.

Use Circularo's broader digital-trust and trusted-document positioning to test relevance. Do not invent an ESG outcome, quantified paper/carbon saving, healthcare proposition or production AI capability to manufacture alignment. The [investor master](../../../master/investor-materials-master.md) retains the distinction between current evidence, roadmap and vision.

## Next qualification and lessons

1. Test whether enterprise software and Circularo's geography fall within the current direct-investment remit.
2. Confirm USD 4m+ appetite, mandatory founder secondary and ownership/control expectations together.
3. Identify specific Australian enterprise-market support; compare that contribution with specialist investors before allocating substantial outreach effort.

**Recommendation:** keep as a secondary-priority qualification lead unless a relationship or concrete operating synergy strengthens the case. The available evidence supports an investor-type match more strongly than a technology/sector match.

**Lessons:** a family office is a possible route, not proof of bespoke secondary appetite. Historic founder liquidity and purchases of other founders' shares are different evidence. Validate preferred sectors and actual operating contribution rather than treating a broad portfolio as an enterprise channel.
