# Battery Ventures

**Disposition: promising candidate to qualify; not yet a qualified transaction target.** Josef supplied [Battery's website](https://www.battery.com/). Screening follows the [batch 03 mandate](../README.md#screening-mandate), including VC firms, the minimum investment ticket and required founder-liquidity component. No contact has been made.

## Why it may fit

Battery's [published strategy](https://www.battery.com/about/) covers application and infrastructure software across early, growth and buyout investments, with a US/European presence. It also describes portfolio support in go-to-market, business development, talent and finance. **Fit hypothesis:** this combination could support Circularo's enterprise expansion and positioning around digital trust and trusted transactions. These services are published capabilities, not promised contributions to Circularo.

The [Nitro case study](https://www.battery.com/blog/nitro-case-study/) records Battery leading a USD 15m Series B in 2014 in a business offering PDF workflows and eSignature. This provides historical sector relevance. That round amount is neither Battery's individual cheque nor a current ticket policy, and the source does not establish founder secondary. Use Circularo's wider digital-trust narrative when evaluating differentiation rather than reducing it to eSignature.

The [investor master](../../../master/investor-materials-master.md) remains the reference for Circularo evidence boundaries. Sovereign deployments and future AI/agentic architecture must retain their draft, reported or vision qualifications; this lead note does not verify them.

## Relevant transaction precedents

- **Signal AI, 2025:** its [24 September announcement](https://signal-ai.com/insights/press-release/signal-ai-announces-165-million-investment-round-led-by-battery-ventures-to-redefine-risk-and-reputation-intelligence) described a USD 165m round led by Battery, a majority position, continued founder-led management and existing investors retaining minority holdings. Signal AI's [current company timeline](https://signal-ai.com/about) reports completion in 2025. This supports an AI/enterprise growth route and control discussion. The round total is not a disclosed Battery cheque or proof of founder-secondary proceeds.
- **Sustainability at Work Group, September 2026:** Battery [reported taking a majority stake](https://www.battery.com/blog/battery-ventures-acquires-majority-stake-in-germanys-sustainability-at-work-group-lays-foundation-for-continued-growth/) while Jan Henke and Norbert Schmitz retained ownership and leadership roles. This illustrates retained management ownership in a majority transaction. Neither founder cash proceeds nor consideration was disclosed in the checked announcement; it is not a software-minority-secondary precedent.

These examples support qualification, not an assumption that Battery accepts Circularo's transaction terms. Do not use fund-level liquidity totals as evidence of purchases from founders.

## Next qualification and observations

1. Establish current company-scale and vehicle criteria, and whether a transaction including mandatory founder secondary is viable.
2. Clarify ticket appetite, primary/secondary split, control and retained founder role without reinstating a fixed equity cap.
3. Check current document-workflow/security portfolio overlap and identify an appropriate sponsor before detailed disclosure.
4. Test concrete enterprise expansion and technology ecosystem contributions against the digital-trust thesis. Keep AI/agentic future direction distinct from production claims.

**Lesson:** a firm with “Ventures” in its name can offer relevant growth and buyout routes. Assess the actual vehicle and transaction evidence. Conversely, a majority acquisition with founders remaining in management does not disclose how much liquidity those founders received.
