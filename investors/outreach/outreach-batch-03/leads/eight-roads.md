## Why it may fit

Eight Roads' [Europe & Israel team](https://eightroads.com/en/teams/europe-israel) targets technology scale-ups and global expansion. Its [platform description](https://www.eightroads.com/en/about) identifies regional teams and Fidelity backing; the US route is explicitly presented as sister fund F-Prime. Do not assume these vehicles share one investment mandate or that Fidelity affiliation guarantees regulated-enterprise distribution.

**Fit hypothesis:** enterprise software and cloud-data protection experience could help Circularo develop international sales and its digital-trust positioning. Eight Roads [led Odaseva's 2020 Series B](https://eightroads.com/en/news/odaseva-raises-25-million-in-series-b), which addressed enterprise data protection, privacy and trust in cloud deployments. This supports sector familiarity, not sovereign-GCC access or acceptance of a founder-secondary deal. The round total is not a disclosed individual investor cheque.

Keep Circularo's verified current offering distinct from future trusted-record/agentic architecture. The [investor master](../../../master/investor-materials-master.md) remains the reference for evidence and draft positioning limits.

## Secondary evidence: direction matters

The transaction adviser [Samvad's published summary](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/samvad-partners_exits-stake-capital-activity-7352918028939907073-IRWY) describes Eight Roads' partial secondary **sale** of holdings in MoEngage, Whatfix and Shadowfax to TR Capital. This establishes Eight Roads as a seller in that transaction, not a purchaser providing liquidity to the companies' founders. It is not used to qualify the founder-secondary gate.

Likewise, portfolio exits and primary fundraising announcements do not establish willingness to purchase Circularo founders' shares. No direct matching precedent was established by the checked sources in this preliminary screen; that does not demonstrate a prohibition.
