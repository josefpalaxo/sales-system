# Norwest

**Disposition: promising candidate to qualify, with relevant founder-liquidity evidence; Circularo-specific eligibility remains unconfirmed.** Josef supplied [Norwest's website](https://www.norwest.com/). Screening follows the [batch 03 mandate](../README.md#screening-mandate). No communication or contact request has been sent.

## Why it may fit

Norwest offers both [growth equity](https://www.norwest.com/growth/) and [venture](https://www.norwest.com/venture/) investment routes. Growth equity explicitly includes minority investments, recapitalisations and buyouts. This offers a plausible route for a founder-liquidity transaction without assuming a fixed equity percentage.

**Strategic-fit hypothesis:** experience in compliance software, public-sector workflows and digital identity could make Circularo's digital-trust and trusted-transaction direction relevant. [GeoComply's 2023 announcement](https://www.geocomply.com/news/geocomply-targets-new-markets-with-latest-investors/) records a Norwest minority investment in geolocation security/compliance and describes expansion into financial services. Norwest's [PSD Citywide investment](https://www.norwest.com/blog/our-strategic-partnership-with-psd-citywide/) adds municipal-software adjacency. Neither establishes guaranteed government introductions or GCC procurement access for Circularo.

Norwest's [September 2026 Beltic thesis](https://www.norwest.com/blog/trust-infrastructure-for-a-frictionless-future-why-were-partnering-with-beltic/) addresses verification, identity and authorisation for agents transacting on behalf of people and businesses. This provides a concrete reason to discuss the broader trust-infrastructure narrative. It also creates a potential overlap question; it is not proof of a complementary integration or permission to disclose Circularo's architecture.

Use the [investor master](../../../master/investor-materials-master.md) for Circularo evidence boundaries. Keep sovereign deployment claims and AI/agentic vision qualified; this research does not establish product readiness or current financial eligibility.

## Founder-liquidity evidence

- **Avetta:** Norwest's [case study](https://www.norwest.com/case-study/avetta-norwest-building-a-forever-company-together/) describes founders choosing partial liquidity alongside a growth partner, a minority initial investment, and subsequently requesting a larger Norwest stake for further liquidity. It also describes their later move into board/adviser roles as operating leadership changed. This is relevant direct historical founder-liquidity evidence, without a disclosed cash amount or promise of equivalent terms for Circularo.
- **Kendra Scott:** Norwest's [case study](https://www.norwest.com/case-study/kendra-scott-norwest-growing-a-jewelry-icon-with-heart-and-strategy/) explicitly describes its 2014 minority investment providing founder liquidity and growth capital. This supports structuring precedent, but it is a consumer/retail transaction rather than enterprise software.
- **Envisage:** the [public-safety SaaS case study](https://www.norwest.com/case-study/envisage-norwest-advancing-public-safety-training-and-compliance/) describes management evaluating liquidity and growth capital and a majority investment in 2020. This strengthens sector relevance; the checked narrative does not disclose a founder-secondary amount.

The [GeoComply announcement](https://www.geocomply.com/news/geocomply-targets-new-markets-with-latest-investors/) establishes a minority investment and digital-trust adjacency. It does not itself specify founder-secondary proceeds, so it is not used here to establish that gate.
