# Circularo investor outreach batch 03

This batch collects investor tips supplied by Josef, together with preliminary screening, evidence, uncertainties and recommended next steps. It uses a new sourcing approach and the [batch 02 research mandate](../outreach-batch-02/research-mandate.md) with the batch-specific amendment below. Batch 02 retains its original non-VC mandate.

## Screening mandate

Source: Josef's instruction, "Include vc here as well", on 2026-10-02. **VC firms are eligible for consideration in batch 03**, alongside operating companies, holding groups, growth equity and private equity. This amendment supersedes the batch 02 investor-type exclusion for batch 03 only.

All other screening parameters remain: investment of at least USD 4 million, a mandatory secondary component providing founder liquidity, and no fixed upper ticket or equity percentage. Primary capital may accompany secondary; primary-only transactions remain excluded. The linked batch 02 mandate remains the reference for unchanged geographic priorities, strategic objectives and control considerations. These are research parameters, not agreed transaction terms. Inclusion of VC does not establish any firm's willingness to meet them.

Use the [tip register](tip-register.md) to track additions. Each lead note should distinguish published facts from fit hypotheses and unknown deal terms, include available company and professional LinkedIn locators, and retain its source URLs and check dates. Duplicate tips should update the existing lead instead of creating another record.

## Observations and recommendations

- Preserve user tips even when initial evidence conflicts with the mandate, and make the conflict visible before prioritising outreach.
- Greycroft's venture focus is now an eligible investor type for this batch. Qualify ticket size and founder-secondary appetite before prioritising outreach; these remain unconfirmed in the checked sources.
- Battery's growth/buyout experience and historical Nitro investment make it worth qualifying. Its majority-deal precedents do not prove founder cash proceeds or acceptance of a minority secondary for Circularo. Historical document-workflow experience can indicate sector knowledge and possible overlap; establish the current position before detailed disclosure.
- Norwest has published founder-liquidity precedents and relevant compliance/software experience. Qualify company scale against its growth-equity range before prioritising outreach. Its agent-trust investment in Beltic makes the broader narrative relevant, while creating a potential portfolio-overlap question.
- Singtel Innov8 is a plausible operating-ecosystem route, with published typical initial tickets of USD 1–5m. The new AI Growth Fund needs separate ticket and product-maturity qualification; its fund size does not prove a larger cheque. fileAI exposure creates an AI/document-workflow overlap question.
- Eight Roads has relevant enterprise/cloud-data exposure, but the USD 5–50m European cheque range found is from a 2021 fund announcement. Its sale of existing portfolio shares is not evidence that it buys founders' shares. Confirm regional eligibility and current policy.
- Use published investment strategy and transaction evidence to test deal fit. Portfolio exits, fund size or unsupported directory cheque ranges do not demonstrate willingness to buy founders' shares in Circularo.
- Point72's private venture/growth route and legal-AI investments merit qualification. Round totals do not establish its cheque or secondary appetite. Recheck team affiliations: Sri Chandrasekar has moved to AI House; historical deal authorship and stale team links are insufficient contact evidence.
- Light Warrior is an eligible family-office route, but its published preferred sectors provide less direct enterprise-software adjacency. Qualify sector appetite, founder secondary and significant-ownership expectations before prioritising it. The founder's historical exit does not establish the group's current cheque or secondary policy.
- Crescent's operating support is relevant, but its ANZ focus requires early eligibility qualification. Its published enterprise-value range is not an investment ticket; the smaller Growth route needs separate criteria. Founder partial-liquidity commentary is a discussion signal, not transaction-policy confirmation. Avoid evidence from similarly named overseas firms.
- Kleiner Perkins has strong enterprise-AI/workflow relevance and current growth capital, but founder secondary remains unconfirmed. Its 2019 Rippling cheque is historical evidence, not current policy. Preserve personal-investment versus firm-portfolio distinctions and Circularo's production/roadmap boundaries when assessing fit.
- Ribbit's current scope explicitly includes identity and AI, with Persona/AIUC adjacency and a completed ID.me secondary tender. Prioritise qualification, while distinguishing employee/early-investor liquidity from founder proceeds and transaction totals from Ribbit's individual cheque. Portfolio relevance does not establish committed integrations or GCC distribution.

- Lightspeed combines enterprise/agent-workflow relevance with a published secondary strategy and completed Grafana transaction participation. Pair the enterprise sponsor with Capital Markets for qualification; confirm founder proceeds, ticket and whether secondary is available for a new investment rather than only existing portfolio liquidity. Prior-employer founder trades do not establish Lightspeed policy.

- Innovation Endeavors has direct document infrastructure and agent/API relevance. Qualify founder secondary, ticket and company stage early; its 2019 expansion strategy is historical, while the current site emphasises early-stage founders. Extend signals both sector expertise and a portfolio-boundary question. A combined seed/Series A fundraising total is not the investor's individual cheque.
