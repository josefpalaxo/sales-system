# Circularo Strategic Investor Longlist

This 100-organisation discovery universe follows the [research mandate](<Circularo Strategic Investor Research Brief.md>). US, Europe and UAE/GCC each have 25 candidates. ANZ has 5, Asia 10 and global PE/growth 10. Parent companies and their corporate arms share one row. Sovereign-linked groups and fund portfolio relationships can overlap; these are not 100 independent capital pools.

The 20 detailed candidates include 18 priority diligence organisations, a G+D parent route with a venture mandate mismatch, and a conditional Level Equity alternative. No organisation has confirmed Circularo interest or acceptance of founder-secondary proceeds. Circularo-specific upside is a hypothesis. Relationships and capabilities supplied in the brief remain management-reported; production, roadmap and vision require separate proof.

Use [the CSV](investor-longlist.csv) for complete risk and transaction fields, [detailed profiles](priority-profiles.md) for the shortlist and [source register](evidence-register.json) for evidence quality. Most non-shortlist rows are discovery screens. A company website or profile establishes business context, not investment appetite.
