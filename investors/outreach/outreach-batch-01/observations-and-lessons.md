## Research lessons

- Validate organisation identity before retaining a company URL. IHC, MGX, Hg, Insight Partners, Al Nowais and TCC searches produced namesakes or misleading matches. A familiar name or first search result is insufficient.
- Use company About pages for self-reported location and website; do not treat them as legal-entity, ownership or investment-authority proof.
- Date events separately from announcements. OneSpan's Build38 acquisition closed 27 February 2026 and was announced 2 March; these are compatible dates.
- Treat fund size, total primary funding and acquisition consideration as different from typical investor cheques. None proves current available capital.
- Distinguish parent corporate development, venture arms, operating subsidiaries and financial funds. Their cheque, geography, control and use-of-proceeds rules can differ.
- Crunchbase's feed contains AI predictions. They were not used as evidence of appetite, capacity or valuation. Authenticated Signicat research corroborated identity/ownership context only.
- A restricted page can still supply indexed evidence, but the access limit must remain visible. Elm and some G42/OneSpan materials required this qualification.
- Qualitative strategic hypotheses are more useful than invented scores. A specific account or integration commitment is a better follow-up test than generic claims of “network value.”
- A 100-row universe is a discovery tool. Eighty rows have lighter screening than the 20 profiles and should not be represented as equally qualified.
- Sovereign ecosystem links and sponsor-backed companies mean the universe can contain related economic interests even after parent/venture-arm deduplication.
