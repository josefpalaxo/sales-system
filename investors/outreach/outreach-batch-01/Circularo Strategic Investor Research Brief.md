# Circularo Strategic Investor Research Brief

## Objective

Identify and research potential **strategic investors / shareholders for Circularo**.

This is **not a conventional VC fundraising exercise**.

Circularo is looking primarily for a strategic shareholder that can combine capital with significant commercial leverage, market access, institutional relationships, distribution capabilities and strategic credibility.

The ideal investor should be capable of helping Circularo materially accelerate revenue growth across the UAE, GCC and ultimately international markets.

The research should identify **specific organisations and, where appropriate, high-net-worth strategic individuals/family offices**, together with the actual decision-makers Circularo should approach.

---

# 1. Transaction Context

Circularo's founders are prepared to explore selling a minority portion of their existing equity to a strategic investor.

Indicative parameters:

- Transaction size: approximately **USD 10–15 million**
- Equity: **up to approximately 25%**
- Structure: primarily **purchase of existing founders' shares**
- Circularo remains founder-led and operationally independent
- This is not primarily a capital-raising exercise
- Long-term possibility of additional liquidity / investment at a future institutional round or IPO
- Long-term ambition includes the possibility of a **Dubai/UAE IPO pathway**

The strategic investor should therefore evaluate Circularo not simply as a financial investment, but as a technology platform that could create significant strategic value across the investor's existing ecosystem.

---

# 2. Circularo Overview

Circularo is a Dubai-headquartered digital trust technology company founded in 2014.

The company provides technology for:

- Digital signatures
- Trusted document workflows
- Digital approvals
- Trusted and verifiable records
- Digital identity integration
- Sovereign digital trust infrastructure
- Multi-tenant government/shared-service platforms
- AI-enabled document intelligence
- Trusted execution of digital transactions
- Emerging Agentic Trusted Execution capabilities

Circularo has evolved beyond traditional eSignatures toward a broader **Digital Trust / Trusted Execution platform**.

The strategic direction can be summarized as:

**Digital Signatures → Digital Trust → Trusted Execution → Agentic Trusted Execution**

Circularo's long-term opportunity is to become part of the trust infrastructure required when humans, enterprise systems and AI agents increasingly execute transactions autonomously.

---

# 3. Current Strategic Position

Circularo is already established in the GCC, particularly the UAE.

Important characteristics include:

- Approximately USD 3M recurring revenue scale
- Profitable
- Bootstrapped / founder funded
- No traditional institutional funding history
- Strong recurring revenue
- Enterprise and government customer base
- UAE, KSA and EU infrastructure
- SaaS, sovereign/private-cloud and on-premise deployment
- Multi-tenant shared-service architecture
- Strong GCC localisation
- Arabic support
- UAE PASS integration
- Nafath integration
- Enterprise REST APIs
- Strong data-residency / sovereignty positioning

Circularo should therefore **not** be researched as an early-stage startup seeking seed or Series A investors.

It should be viewed as an established technology platform seeking a strategic shareholder to accelerate the next stage of growth.

---

# 4. Sovereign / Government Position

One of Circularo's strongest strategic assets is its position inside UAE digital-government infrastructure.

Important deployments include:

### TDRA / GovSign
Federal-level shared-service digital signing infrastructure supporting UAE federal government entities.

### Digital Dubai / Digital Sign
Sovereign digital signing platform supporting Dubai government entities.

### Sharjah Sign
Shared digital signing infrastructure for Sharjah government entities.

### e& / DigiSign
Circularo technology deployed as a co-branded digital signing service through e&.

### Saudi Arabia / TCC / Mokham
Circularo technology deployed through a Saudi strategic partner with local hosting and Nafath integration.

These deployments demonstrate that Circularo technology can operate as infrastructure serving entire ecosystems rather than simply individual enterprise customers.

This **platform multiplication effect** is central to the investment thesis.

---

# 5. Why Circularo Wants a Strategic Investor

Circularo does not primarily need capital to survive or fund normal operations.

The objective is to find an investor capable of accelerating distribution.

The fundamental question when evaluating every candidate should therefore be:

> **Could this investor realistically help Circularo multiply its current revenue through its own companies, customers, government relationships, technology ecosystem or international network?**

The investor should ideally help Circularo:

1. Accelerate UAE growth.
2. Expand into Saudi Arabia and the wider GCC.
3. Expand internationally.
4. Scale sovereign/shared-service deployments.
5. Expand enterprise SaaS distribution.
6. Enter large banking, telecom and regulated-industry accounts.
7. Develop Circularo's Digital Trust / Trusted Execution category.
8. Strengthen Circularo's UAE identity and institutional position.
9. Build credibility and distribution for eventual international expansion and potentially an IPO.

Capital without strategic leverage should receive substantially lower priority.

## Global Research Principle

Do **not** assume the best Circularo investor must come from the UAE.

Compare different forms of strategic value.

For example:

**UAE strategic investor**
→ sovereign access + GCC distribution + UAE positioning

**US technology company**
→ global distribution + technology ecosystem + potential strategic acquisition pathway

**European digital-trust player**
→ eIDAS/trust infrastructure + European distribution + GCC expansion synergy

**Australian strategic investor**
→ APAC distribution + enterprise/government access

**Global technology PE**
→ scaling expertise + M&A + internationalization + future capital markets

The objective is to identify investors where:

**Capital + Distribution + Technology Synergy + Strategic Positioning + Future Optionality**

creates substantially more value than capital alone.

# 15. Important Research Principle

Do not optimize primarily for:

> "Who invests in SaaS companies?"

Optimize for:

> **"Who could own 10–25% of Circularo and materially increase the value of the company because they can accelerate distribution, sovereign adoption, enterprise access or international expansion?"**

The ideal outcome is an investor where:

**Investment + Distribution + Strategic Alignment > Capital Alone**

A strategically valuable USD 10M shareholder capable of helping Circularo create tens of millions of dollars of additional enterprise and sovereign revenue is more relevant than a conventional financial investor offering the same capital.
