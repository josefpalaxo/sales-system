# Circularo Strategic Investor Research — Recommendations

Circularo should first qualify a balanced group of operating partners: **e&, SITE, Beyon, Entrust, Okta Ventures, Cisco Investments, Signicat and Namirial**. These eight offer identifiable technology or distribution routes and useful public transaction precedents. They are recommended for qualification, not reported as interested investors. None has confirmed acceptance of the proposed founder-secondary transaction.

The research delivers **100 discovery candidates, 20 detailed profiles, 100 company LinkedIn links and 30 browser-verified personal LinkedIn profiles across 31 named contacts**. SITE CEO Saad Saleh Alaboodi's personal LinkedIn remains unresolved. An additional verified Databricks contact is included outside the 20 profiles. Sources and role discrepancies are recorded rather than silently resolved.

The [research brief](<Circularo Strategic Investor Research Brief.md>) describes a USD10–15m transaction, up to 25% ownership, primarily founder liquidity, with Circularo remaining independent and founder-led. These are management-supplied working objectives. This structure is the central qualification challenge: most relevant public corporate precedents are primary funding, acquisitions or operating ventures.

## Questions to qualify before a full process

1. Will the investing entity purchase existing founder shares at the proposed size and ownership level? If it requires primary capital, what allocation and purpose would it accept?
2. Can Circularo retain founder leadership, independent positioning and control of its roadmap? Which board rights, vetoes, exclusivity or IP arrangements would be required?
3. Who owns investment approval, and which operating executive would own the commercial programme? Can both sponsor the proposal?
4. What concrete accounts, integrations, distribution resources and commitments would ownership unlock? Who would deliver them and on what timetable?
5. What stage, recurring-revenue, geographic or current-product requirements could prevent investment? Separate production capability from roadmap and vision.
6. What information is needed for initial qualification, and what can be shared after owner review and appropriate confidentiality arrangements?

These are proposed qualification questions. No outreach has been performed.
