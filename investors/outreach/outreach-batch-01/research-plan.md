# Research plan

## Discovery and balance

Build approximately 25 UAE/GCC, 25 US and 25 European candidates, with 25 additional ANZ, Asian and global PE/growth candidates. Geography is a coverage dimension; fund/operating-company type is a separate dimension. Deduplicate related vehicles and parents where they represent the same likely decision-maker. A subsidiary may be a route to a parent, not an additional economic investor.

## Evidence and qualification

For each organisation record an official website, activity evidence, company LinkedIn where observed, strategic mechanism, deal-structure evidence and specific uncertainties. Acquisition history proves strategic interest, not willingness to purchase a minority founder-secondary stake. Corporate venture participation proves investment activity, not eligibility for Circularo's stage or secondary structure.

Use qualitative priorities: priority diligence, strategic exploratory, financial/execution alternative, lower fit / structure risk. No numerical investor scores. Never fill a typical ticket from a fund's AUM or a single acquisition price. Use “not disclosed / not established” when unsupported.

## Shortlist and contacts

Select 20 for depth, preserving equal opportunity for the three main regions. Verify named current roles against official leadership/team pages where possible. Record LinkedIn profile provenance separately from role provenance; unresolved match/current-role uncertainty stays visible. Public profile identifiers are held locally. Identify 1–3 relevant people per priority organisation where evidence supports them.

## Source handling

Primary sources take precedence: official strategy, portfolio, announcements, annual reports and leadership pages. LinkedIn and Crunchbase support discovery/identity matching, not assumed investment appetite. Preserve publication dates, accessed date 2026-10-02, fact scope and hypotheses. Do not infer warm introductions from mere adjacency; mutual connections indicate a possible route requiring user validation.
