# Circularo — Current Investor Narrative

**Purpose:** Canonical narrative for investor decks, briefs, meetings and strategic-partner discussions.  
**Status:** Internal / Confidential  
**Last updated:** October 2026

## Narrative in One Sentence

Circularo is evolving from a profitable GCC digital-signature and workflow platform with unusually strategic sovereign deployments into a broader digital-trust and trusted-execution platform designed to provide verifiable institutional evidence for human, system and AI-agent transactions.

## 1. The Starting Point: Digital Signatures

Circularo began by solving how organizations securely prepare, approve, sign, execute and retain important documents digitally.

Doing this properly for governments and regulated enterprises required more than placing signatures on PDFs. Circularo had to solve identity, authentication, authority, approvals, lifecycle, evidence, auditability, signatures/seals, timestamps, data residency, integration and secure records.

That became the foundation for something broader than eSignatures.

## 2. From Product to Infrastructure

Circularo's strongest validation came from environments where trust and sovereignty matter most.

The platform has been selected for government/shared-service deployments including federal UAE, Digital Dubai and Sharjah, alongside strategic partner models with e& in UAE and TCC in KSA.

These are not simply isolated software subscriptions. A shared-service deployment can become infrastructure used across many entities, departments and use cases.

**One platform relationship → many organizations → many workflows → increasing recurring usage.**

This creates potential to scale through ecosystems as well as customer-by-customer SaaS acquisition.

## 3. The Business Today

Circularo enters its next phase from an established base rather than a pre-revenue technology thesis.

Current management context indicates approximately USD 3M recurring-revenue scale, profitable operations, bootstrapped/no external funding, strong gross margins and positive adjusted EBITDA, low churn and NRR above 100%, established government and enterprise customers, SaaS/self-hosted/multi-tenant deployment models, and a particularly strong UAE/GCC footprint.

The central question is not whether Circularo can build a product or win demanding customers. It is whether the company can turn its strategic position into materially greater scale.

## 4. The Embedded Growth Opportunity

The growth thesis has four layers:

### Layer 1 — Expand existing platforms
Increase adoption across existing government/shared-service deployments.

### Layer 2 — Expand enterprise SaaS
Grow enterprise adoption and use cases within existing accounts.

### Layer 3 — Strategic distribution
Use telcos, technology companies, SIs and a strategic shareholder to distribute Circularo more broadly.

### Layer 4 — Replicate the model
Apply the sovereign/shared-service architecture proven in UAE to additional governments, holdings, regulated ecosystems and markets.

This is why the right strategic shareholder may be materially more valuable than passive financial capital.

## 5. The Category Is Expanding

The first digital transformation wave asked:
> How do we replace paper signatures?

The next asked:
> How do we make digital transactions trustworthy and verifiable?

The AI era introduces:
> How does an institution know that a human, system or AI agent was authorized to act, that the correct action was executed, and that reliable evidence exists afterward?

Circularo's strategic evolution is:

**Digital Signatures → Digital Trust → Trusted Execution → Trusted Records / Institutional Evidence → Agentic Trusted Execution**

## 6. Why Circularo Has a Credible Right to Play

Do not claim that operating an eSignature platform automatically makes Circularo an AI trust platform.

Credibility comes from underlying problems already solved: verified identities and national digital identity, organizational users/roles, approval and signing authority, controlled workflows, legally relevant actions, audit trails/timestamps, evidence/secure records, sovereign/private deployments, API-driven transactions and regulated customers.

These are foundational ingredients of trusted execution.

The long-term opportunity is to generalize them beyond document signing so they can govern actions by humans, systems and eventually autonomous agents.

## 7. eDoc and Trusted Records

Traditional files primarily represent content.

The eDoc concept is intended to represent the transaction itself: intent, participants, authority, lifecycle, state, content and evidence in a canonical machine-readable and human-readable trust object.

This moves Circularo from:
> signing a document

toward:
> maintaining an authoritative record of a trusted transaction.

Trusted Records can preserve institutional evidence regardless of whether an action originated from a person, enterprise system or AI agent.

## 8. Trusted Execution

The next layer is not simply AI automation. It is **governed execution**.

Institutions need to know who/what requested an action, what authority they have, under which policy, the organizational context, what action is permitted, what actually happened, what evidence was produced and whether it can be audited later.

The next-generation architecture is being designed around these questions. AUTHOS, HOROS, ACTUS, SANTOS and eDoc should always carry explicit status labels separating production capability from roadmap and vision.

## 9. Why This Matters in the Agentic AI Era

AI assistants generate/analyze information. AI agents increasingly take actions.

As agents approve, initiate, modify, purchase, instruct and execute on behalf of organizations, identity alone will not be enough. Institutions will require authority, policy, organizational context, controlled execution, evidence, accountability and durable trusted records.

Circularo's long-term ambition is to provide this trust infrastructure. This is strategically adjacent to its existing domain rather than an unrelated AI pivot.

## 10. Strategic Investment Opportunity

Circularo's founders are exploring a strategic shareholder, not simply capital.

Current contemplated transaction:
- USD 10–15M;
- up to approximately 25% ownership;
- primarily acquisition of existing founders' shares.

The preferred partner should help accelerate Circularo through network, portfolio, customers, government relationships or international reach.

**Circularo technology + sovereign position + strategic partner distribution → materially larger company.**

## 11. The Long-Term Story

**eSignatures created the entry point.** They forced Circularo to master identity, authority, workflow and evidence.

**Sovereign deployments created strategic validation.** They demonstrated the ability to operate as trusted infrastructure across multi-entity environments.

**Platform Multiplication creates near-term growth potential.** Government and partner platforms can distribute Circularo beyond ordinary direct SaaS economics.

**Trusted Records and Trusted Execution expand the category.** Circularo can move from recording signed documents toward authoritative evidence of institutional actions.

**Agentic AI increases the importance of this layer.** As machines increasingly act for organizations, governed authority, execution and evidence become more important.

## 12. Narrative Guardrails

Do not:
- describe roadmap concepts as production;
- treat addressable government entities as paying customers;
- treat strategic upside as contracted pipeline;
- inflate ARR with future scenarios;
- describe IPO as committed;
- imply fixed transaction terms unless confirmed;
- position Circularo as an AI startup disconnected from the existing business.
