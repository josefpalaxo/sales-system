# Circularo — Investment Thesis

**Purpose:** Canonical evidence structure for valuation work, investor decks and strategic-partner discussions.  
**Status:** Internal / Confidential  
**Last updated:** October 2026

## Core Valuation Question

Is an investor acquiring exposure to an approximately USD 3M eSignature SaaS company — or to a profitable digital-trust platform with an embedded path to materially larger recurring revenue, a difficult-to-replicate sovereign position, scalable platform distribution and a credible role in trusted execution for the Agentic AI era?

Investor materials should provide the evidence required to answer this question. Do not assume the conclusion.

## Thesis 1 — Established, Profitable Base

Circularo is not a pre-revenue venture. Current working management context includes approximately USD 2.96M ARR at December 2025, USD 2.79M 2025 net revenue, 69.2% gross margin, 30.7% adjusted EBITDA margin, 2024 NRR of 113.19%, 2024 churn of 1.05%, 117 paying customers as of August 2025, and a bootstrapped history with no external funding.

**Investor implication:** the strategic transaction is built on an operating business with recurring revenue, retention and profitability.

**Diligence:** reconcile latest ARR, recognized revenue, customer count, EBITDA, cash/debt and 2026 YTD performance before external use.

## Thesis 2 — Scarce Sovereign / Shared-Service Position

Circularo has established shared-service positions across federal UAE, Dubai and Sharjah, plus strategic platform relationships with e& and TCC. These are strategically different from ordinary enterprise logos because they can provide access to ecosystems of downstream entities.

**Investor implication:** the value of these relationships may exceed current ARR if adoption expands materially.

**Required proof:** for each platform maintain current revenue, live entities, addressable entities, usage, renewal terms, expansion mechanisms, pipeline and supporting evidence.

## Thesis 3 — Platform Multiplication Can Change Growth Economics

Circularo can potentially grow through strategic platforms rather than solely through direct customer acquisition.

**Platform win → ecosystem access → entity adoption → workflow expansion → recurring usage → strategic lock-in**

**Investor implication:** if demonstrated, this can justify analyzing Circularo differently from a small standalone eSignature vendor.

**Required proof:** quantify historical adoption curves and revenue conversion for Digital Dubai, TDRA, Sharjah, e&, TCC and major holdings. Never value addressable entities as if already converted.

## Thesis 4 — Strategic Shareholder Can Be a Growth Multiplier

Circularo is seeking a shareholder capable of creating commercial value, not merely supplying capital.

Potential levers include portfolio-company deployments, government access, enterprise distribution, telco/service-provider channels, sovereign/shared-service replication, international market access and integration into broader technology offerings.

**Investor implication:** strategic fit should be assessed by identifiable commercial pathways the shareholder can unlock.

**Required proof:** build an investor-specific opportunity map for each candidate. Keep scenario ARR separate from qualified pipeline.

## Thesis 5 — GCC Concentration Is Both Strategic Depth and Risk

Approximately 98% of ARR being GCC-related demonstrates regional specialization. Potential advantages include Arabic/RTL localization, national identity integrations, data residency, sovereign deployment capability, regulatory familiarity and local references.

It also creates geographic concentration risk.

**Investor implication:** do not hide concentration. Show how UAE/KSA depth could support expansion while explicitly identifying diversification requirements.

## Thesis 6 — Deployment Flexibility Is Strategically Important

Circularo supports locally hosted SaaS, self-hosted/on-premise deployments and multi-tenant shared services. This matters in government, regulated enterprise and sovereign environments where cloud-only architecture may be unsuitable.

**Investor implication:** deployment flexibility is part of the strategic moat when combined with localization, identity integrations and evidence/audit capabilities.

## Thesis 7 — Revenue Quality Provides a Foundation for Expansion

Low churn and NRR above 100% suggest that existing customer relationships can provide a base for expansion rather than requiring constant replacement of lost revenue.

**Investor implication:** valuation work should examine revenue durability, customer cohorts, expansion ARR and concentration, not merely headline growth.

## Thesis 8 — The Product Category Can Expand Beyond eSignatures

Circularo's experience spans identity, authority, approvals, workflows, evidence, audit trails, trusted records, API-driven execution and sovereign deployments.

The strategic evolution is:

**Digital Signatures → Digital Trust → Trusted Execution → Trusted Records / Institutional Evidence → Agentic Trusted Execution**

**Investor implication:** category expansion could increase strategic relevance and addressable value if Circularo converts existing foundations into defensible products/protocols.

**Critical caveat:** distinguish production capability, development, roadmap and long-term vision. Do not capitalize speculative architecture as if it were current revenue-producing IP.

## Thesis 9 — eDoc Can Become a Bridge From Documents to Transactions

The eDoc direction treats the document/transaction as a canonical trust object containing intent, participants, authority, lifecycle, state, content and evidence. This is intended to support human-readable and machine-readable trusted transactions.

**Investor implication:** if executed, eDoc can move Circularo from signing files toward maintaining authoritative transaction records that are more useful in automated and AI-driven environments.

## Thesis 10 — Agentic AI Creates a New Trust Problem

As AI evolves from generating content to taking institutional actions, organizations will need to establish who/what may act, under what authority and policy, what occurred, and what evidence remains.

Circularo's long-term architecture — including eDoc, AUTHOS, HOROS, ACTUS, SANTOS and Trusted Records — is intended to address this problem.

**Investor implication:** this provides strategic optionality beyond the current eSignature category.

**Critical caveat:** this is a future thesis. It must be supported by milestones, product status and evidence rather than presented as current market leadership.

## Thesis 11 — The Transaction Structure Is Deliberately Strategic

Current contemplated parameters are approximately USD 10–15M for up to approximately 25% ownership, primarily through purchase of founders' existing shares rather than a conventional primary funding round.

The rationale is that Circularo does not primarily require capital to survive. It is seeking a shareholder whose network and assets can accelerate the business.

**Investor question to answer:** why should a strategic buyer pay a meaningful premium for secondary shares, and what economic value can the combination create?

## Thesis 12 — Long-Term Optionality

Current strategic ambition includes materially scaling the company with a strategic shareholder, potentially bringing in further capital/ownership changes later, and preserving the possibility of a future Dubai/UAE IPO pathway.

An IPO is an ambition, not a committed exit plan.

# Value-Creation Framework

Investor analysis should separate five layers:

1. **Existing value** — current ARR, profitability, customers and IP.
2. **Embedded expansion** — realistic growth inside existing customers/platforms.
3. **Strategic-partner acceleration** — opportunities unlocked specifically by the investor.
4. **Replicable platform growth** — new shared-service deployments and international expansion.
5. **Category optionality** — Trusted Records, Trusted Execution and Agentic Trusted Execution.

Do not blend all five into a single forecast without clearly showing assumptions.

# Main Risks to Carry Into Every Investor Analysis

- modest historical scale relative to company age;
- GCC geographic concentration;
- concentration in major government/strategic relationships;
- potentially slow conversion of shared-service reach into paid adoption;
- global and regional competition;
- execution risk in international expansion;
- strategic shareholder dependency;
- founder-liquidity perception around secondary sale;
- roadmap/vision execution risk;
- risk of overstating AI/agentic positioning before commercial validation.

# Evidence Standard

Every important investor claim should be tagged internally as one of:

**CONFIRMED | MANAGEMENT ESTIMATE | CONTRACTED | PIPELINE | FORECAST | SCENARIO | ROADMAP | VISION | NEEDS VERIFICATION**

The strongest investor story is not the most aggressive one. It is the one where investors can clearly see which value already exists, which value is embedded, and which value remains optionality.
