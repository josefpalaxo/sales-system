# Circularo — Investor Context

**Purpose:** Persistent context for Codex/AI agents and humans preparing investor work.  
**Status:** Internal / Confidential  
**Last updated:** October 2026

## 1. How to Think About Circularo

Circularo should not be understood simply as an eSignature SaaS company seeking capital.

Circularo is a profitable, founder-funded digital trust company that has built an unusual position inside UAE and GCC government, enterprise and shared-service environments. The company began with digital signatures and document workflows, but succeeding in these environments required capabilities in identity, authority, approvals, trusted workflows, evidence, records, data sovereignty and integration.

The investor opportunity has two connected propositions:

1. **Scale what already exists:** convert sovereign deployments, strategic partnerships and enterprise footprint into materially larger recurring revenue through platform multiplication, SaaS expansion and strategic distribution.
2. **Expand the category:** evolve from digital signatures and trusted document workflows toward Digital Trust, Trusted Records, Trusted Execution and eventually Agentic Trusted Execution.

The future thesis must remain anchored in existing customers, deployments, capabilities and revenue. Do not present Circularo as a speculative AI startup.

## 2. Company Today

Circularo is headquartered in Dubai/DIFC and has been built without external funding.

Commercial models include locally hosted SaaS, self-hosted/on-premise Ultimate, multi-tenant sovereign/shared services, API/embedded use cases, direct enterprise/government sales and strategic partner distribution.

Core geographic strength is the GCC, particularly UAE and KSA.

## 3. What Is Unusual About Circularo

The strongest investment evidence is not simply the feature set. Circularo has secured positions that are difficult for a small independent software company to obtain:

- **TDRA / GovSign:** federal-level UAE shared-service environment.
- **Digital Dubai / DigitalSign:** sovereign shared-service platform with 80+ Dubai government entities in scope.
- **Sharjah / Sharjah Sign:** multi-entity government shared-service environment with significant expansion potential.
- **e& / DigiSign:** co-branded service/distribution model with one of the region's largest telecommunications groups.
- **TCC / Mokham:** locally adapted route into KSA, including Nafath and sovereign/local deployment requirements.
- **Strategic enterprises:** references including ICD, EDGE, RTA and Emaar.

These provide evidence that Circularo can operate in regulated, security-sensitive and mission-critical environments.

## 4. Platform Multiplication

A critical investor concept is **Platform Multiplication**.

Traditional SaaS:
> win one customer → earn revenue from one customer.

Circularo shared services:
> win one strategic platform → reach tens or hundreds of downstream entities → expand usage and recurring revenue across an ecosystem.

This can apply to federal/local government, telcos, large holdings and service providers.

Always distinguish current ARR, contracted revenue, active deployments, qualified pipeline, identified opportunity, management scenario and long-term strategic upside.

Never describe downstream addressable entities as existing paying customers unless supported by evidence.

## 5. Why Growth Could Accelerate

Potential acceleration can come from existing foundations:

1. **Existing sovereign platforms:** more entities, users, transactions and use cases.
2. **SaaS expansion:** direct enterprise growth in UAE, KSA and beyond.
3. **Strategic distribution:** telcos, technology companies, SIs and a future strategic shareholder.
4. **KSA:** expand from TCC/Mokham and local regulatory/identity foundations.
5. **Enterprise expansion:** more departments, workflows, users, transactions and integrations.
6. **New shared services:** replicate the model across governments, holdings and regulated ecosystems.
7. **Category expansion:** trusted records, authority, execution and AI-era trust infrastructure.

## 6. Product and Category Evolution

Preferred investor framing:

**Digital Signatures → Digital Trust → Trusted Execution → Trusted Records / Institutional Evidence → Agentic Trusted Execution**

This is an evolution, not a replacement of the existing business.

## 7. Next-Generation Architecture Context

Strategic architecture work includes:

- **eDoc:** canonical trust object for transaction/document, state, parties, authority, content and evidence;
- **ACTUS:** trusted execution protocol;
- **AUTHOS:** authority/authorization context;
- **HOROS:** organizational structure/context;
- **SANTOS:** sentient interaction/orchestration;
- **Trusted Records / institutional evidence.**

Always classify capabilities as **Production / existing**, **In development / foundational**, **Roadmap**, or **Long-term vision**.

Never imply conceptual architecture is already commercially deployed unless confirmed.

## 8. Strategic Investor Objective

Circularo is not primarily seeking a conventional VC financing round.

The founders are exploring a **strategic shareholder** capable of materially accelerating revenue and strategic reach.

Current contemplated parameters:

- up to approximately 25% ownership;
- indicative transaction value approximately USD 10–15M;
- primarily purchase of existing founders' shares rather than new primary capital.

These are contemplated parameters, not universally fixed terms. Check the latest transaction master before external use.

Ideal strategic contribution includes GCC government access, enterprise distribution, portfolio-company adoption, telco/service-provider distribution, technology ecosystem access, sovereign opportunities, international expansion and strategic credibility.

## 9. Long-Term Ownership / Exit Context

Current strategic thinking includes bringing in a strategic shareholder now, materially scaling Circularo together, potentially changing ownership/capital again at a later pre-IPO stage, and preserving a longer-term possible Dubai/UAE IPO pathway.

Do not present an IPO as committed or scheduled.

## 10. Positioning Guardrail

Avoid reducing Circularo to **a smaller regional DocuSign competitor** and avoid presenting it as **an AI agent startup**.

Preferred framing:

> Circularo is a profitable digital-trust platform with established enterprise and sovereign deployments, an unusual shared-service position in the GCC, and a credible path from trusted digital transactions toward trusted execution and institutional evidence for the AI era.

## 11. Questions Investors Will Examine

Proactively address:

- Why is Circularo still around USD 3M scale after more than a decade?
- Why have major shared-service deployments not yet produced much larger ARR?
- What does ~98% GCC concentration mean for risk and opportunity?
- Why secondary founder liquidity rather than primary growth capital?
- How much future growth depends on the strategic shareholder?
- What prevents larger/global competitors from addressing the opportunity?
- Which Trusted Execution / Agentic capabilities exist now versus roadmap?

## 12. Working Rule

Always ask:

> Are we presenting Circularo as it actually exists today, while making the future opportunity visible without confusing evidence with ambition?
