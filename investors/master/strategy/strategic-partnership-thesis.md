# Circularo — Strategic Partnership Thesis

**Status:** Internal / Confidential  
**Purpose:** Define why Circularo is seeking a strategic shareholder, what Circularo offers, what it expects from the partner, and how potential investors should be evaluated.  
**Last updated:** October 2026

---

## 1. Core Thesis

Circularo is not primarily seeking passive financial capital.

The objective is to bring in a **strategic shareholder capable of materially accelerating Circularo's growth** through assets and relationships that Circularo cannot efficiently build alone.

The ideal partner should help Circularo transform:

**technology + sovereign position + existing customer base**

into:

**materially larger recurring revenue + broader GCC leadership + international expansion.**

---

## 2. Current Transaction Concept

Current contemplated parameters are:

- **USD 10–15M indicative transaction value**
- **up to approximately 25% ownership**
- primarily **purchase of existing founders' shares**
- not a conventional primary VC funding round

These are current working parameters rather than immutable transaction terms.

The latest transaction master must always take precedence.

---

## 3. Why Circularo Is Not Primarily Raising Capital

Circularo is:

- profitable;
- recurring-revenue based;
- founder funded;
- without external institutional funding to date.

The principal opportunity is therefore not simply:

> Give Circularo money so it can continue operating.

The strategic objective is:

> Bring Circularo a shareholder that can materially change the speed and scale at which the company can commercialize its existing position.

Capital is useful.

**Strategic leverage is more important.**

---

## 4. What Circularo Wants from the Partner

The central question for every candidate should be:

> What can this shareholder help Circularo achieve during the next 3–5 years that Circularo would struggle to achieve alone?

Potential contributions include:

### Portfolio Adoption

Deploy Circularo across companies the investor owns or influences.

### Enterprise Distribution

Introduce or distribute Circularo across an existing enterprise customer base.

### Government Access

Accelerate relationships with government and sovereign entities.

### Shared-Service Platforms

Create new national, government, holding-company or service-provider platforms powered by Circularo.

### Geographic Expansion

Accelerate entry into KSA, wider GCC and international markets.

### Technology Ecosystem

Integrate Circularo into broader cloud, cybersecurity, identity, AI or digital-transformation offerings.

### Infrastructure

Provide sovereign cloud, data center or platform infrastructure.

### Strategic Credibility

Strengthen Circularo's position as a major UAE/GCC technology platform.

### Corporate Development

Support future acquisitions, strategic partnerships or consolidation.

### Capital Markets

Support future institutional financing and potential IPO readiness.

---

## 5. What Circularo Offers the Partner

The strategic shareholder receives exposure to an existing business rather than a pre-product startup.

Circularo offers:

- approximately USD 3M recurring-revenue scale;
- profitability;
- founder-led ownership;
- no previous external funding;
- established UAE/GCC position;
- major enterprise references;
- sovereign/shared-service deployments;
- SaaS;
- self-hosted Ultimate;
- multi-tenant sovereign architecture;
- API/embedded capabilities;
- KSA expansion foundation;
- Digital Trust product expansion;
- long-term Trusted Execution optionality.

The future optionality should never be presented as though already reflected in current revenue.

---

## 6. Preferred Strategic Partner Profile

Particularly relevant categories include:

- UAE strategic investment groups;
- sovereign-linked technology companies;
- telecommunications groups;
- digital infrastructure companies;
- large enterprise holdings;
- major technology platforms;
- organizations with significant government and enterprise distribution.

The partner does not necessarily need to be a traditional investment company.

An operating company with substantial distribution may be strategically more valuable.

---

## 7. Emirati Strategic Ownership

Circularo's current preference is to explore the opportunity with the right Emirati strategic partner before defaulting to international financial investors.

The rationale is strategic rather than symbolic.

Circularo already has important UAE government and enterprise positions.

An Emirati shareholder could help align:

```text id="ofr4kw"
UAE Digital Ambition
        +
Circularo Technology
        +
Existing Sovereign Deployments
        +
Emirati Strategic Ownership
        +
Regional / International Commercialization
```

This could help establish Circularo increasingly as a **UAE technology company with international potential**, rather than simply an external technology supplier operating in the UAE.

## 8. From Supplier to Strategic Platform

A central ambition is to change Circularo's position from:

> software supplier

toward:

> strategic digital-trust platform originating from the UAE.

This becomes particularly relevant to concepts such as:

- UAE Trust-as-a-Service;
- sovereign Trusted Records;
- government shared services;
- sovereign AI;
- Trusted Execution;
- Agentic Trusted Execution.

Strategic ownership could strengthen alignment around this evolution.

The ideal shareholder should itself become another Platform Multiplication mechanism.

Conceptually:

```text id="f9vguu"
Strategic Shareholder
        ↓
Owned Companies
        +
Portfolio Companies
        +
Enterprise Customers
        +
```

## 10. Investor-Specific Opportunity Map

Every serious candidate should have:

`opportunity-map.md`

Structure:

### Owned Companies

Where could Circularo be deployed directly?

### Portfolio Companies

Which portfolio organizations are plausible customers?

### Existing Customers

Could Circularo be distributed through the investor's commercial relationships?

### Government Relationships

What sovereign/shared-service opportunities could the investor enable?

### Infrastructure

Could Circularo become part of the investor's cloud, cybersecurity, identity or AI infrastructure?

### International Reach

Which markets could become accessible?

### Scenario Revenue

What ARR could these opportunities potentially produce?

Every opportunity must be classified:

- EXISTING;
- CONTRACTED;
- QUALIFIED PIPELINE;
- IDENTIFIED OPPORTUNITY;
- SCENARIO.

---

## 11. Strategic Partner Evaluation

Do not evaluate candidates solely on valuation.

Assess:

### Commercial Leverage

Can the partner generate meaningful Circularo revenue?

### Government Access

Does the partner have relevant sovereign relationships?

### Portfolio Fit

Are there obvious internal/portfolio deployments?

### Distribution

Does the partner already sell to Circularo's target customers?

### Geographic Reach

Can the partner accelerate KSA, GCC or international expansion?

### Technology Fit

Does Circularo complement the partner's infrastructure or product portfolio?

### Strategic Alignment

Does the partner understand Circularo's long-term Digital Trust direction?

### Founder Alignment

Can the parties work together for many years?

### Governance

Are the requested rights proportionate to the ownership?

### Capital Markets

Can the partner support the next institutional phase?

---

## 12. Valuation Versus Strategic Value

The highest valuation is not automatically the best transaction.

For example:

**Investor A**

Offers higher valuation but little commercial leverage.

**Investor B**

Offers somewhat lower valuation but can potentially generate substantial recurring revenue through its portfolio and distribution.

The economic comparison should consider:

```text id="h3u11p"
Entry Valuation
      +
Incremental ARR Created
      +
Future Valuation Uplift
      +
Strategic Position
      +
Exit Optionality
```

This is particularly important because founders will retain substantial ownership.

Creating a much larger company may be economically more valuable than maximizing today's secondary price.

---

## 13. Strategic Commitments

A strategic investment should not rely only on verbal expectations.

Where appropriate, Circularo should explore mechanisms around:

- joint business planning;
- portfolio-company introductions;
- distribution;
- named strategic initiatives;
- sovereign opportunities;
- co-development;
- go-to-market resources;
- executive sponsorship;
- periodic strategic reviews.

Not every commitment needs to be legally guaranteed.

But the strategic rationale should be concrete enough to measure after
