## 1. How to Use This Document

This is not a sales rebuttal sheet.

For every investor concern:

1. acknowledge the legitimate question;
2. answer using evidence;
3. distinguish fact from management interpretation;
4. identify what remains to be proven.

If the evidence is weak, say so.

Investor credibility is more important than forcing a positive answer.

# 2. "Circularo has existed for more than 10 years. Why is revenue still only around USD 3M?"

## Investor Concern

The company may have limited product-market fit or an inherently low growth ceiling.

## Answer

Historical growth should not be presented as hypergrowth.

Circularo spent much of its history building the product, serving complex enterprise environments and transitioning from customized/on-premise projects toward standardized SaaS, self-hosted and shared-service offerings.

It did this while remaining bootstrapped.

The company has now reached a different strategic position:

- profitable recurring business;
- sovereign/shared-service deployments;
- major government references;
- strategic distribution relationships;
- repeatable SaaS and Ultimate products.

The forward thesis is therefore not:

> Circularo has always grown rapidly.

It is:

> Circularo now controls distribution and platform opportunities that did not exist during much of its history.

## Evidence Required

- annual revenue history;
- ARR history;
- productization timeline;
- SaaS vs custom-project evolution;
- major platform win dates;
- profitability history.

## Still to Prove

That the new platform positions materially change the future growth rate.

# 3. "Why haven't Digital Dubai, TDRA and Sharjah already generated much more revenue?"

## Investor Concern

The strategic importance of these deployments may be much greater than their actual economics.

## Answer

This is one of the most important questions investors should ask.

A sovereign platform can become technically and institutionally embedded before downstream entities, workflows and transaction volumes are fully adopted.

The relevant sequence can be:

**Central platform → entity onboarding → workflow adoption → usage → expansion → monetization**

Therefore current ARR may not represent the full strategic opportunity.

But this argument only works if Circularo can demonstrate actual progression.

## Evidence Required

For each platform:

- current ARR;
- contract structure;
- live entities;
- addressable entities;
- users;
- transactions;
- historical adoption;
- expansion pipeline;
- commercial mechanism for increased revenue.

## Still to Prove

That sovereign platform penetration converts into substantial recurring revenue.

# 4. "Is Circularo's sovereign position really defensible?"

## Answer

No single Circularo feature should be presented as an unassailable moat.

The defensibility thesis comes from the combination of:

- sovereign references;
- self-hosted architecture;
- multi-tenancy;
- national identity;
- Arabic localization;
- data residency;
- APIs;
- security/compliance;
- implementation knowledge;
- procurement history;
- institutional trust.

A competitor can reproduce individual features.

The harder question is whether it can reproduce the complete position and displace an embedded platform.

## Still to Prove

- renewal strength;
- switching friction;
- customer dependency;
- new sovereign wins;
- international repeatability.

# 6. "Why are the founders selling shares instead of raising money into the company?"

## Investor Concern

Secondary liquidity can sometimes indicate reduced founder commitment.

## Answer

Circularo is profitable and does not currently require external capital simply to continue operating.

The proposed transaction is designed primarily to introduce a strategic shareholder capable of accelerating growth through commercial leverage.

The founders have also financed and built Circularo without external investors for more than a decade.

A secondary transaction recognizes part of that accumulated founder value.

## Important

Management must clearly explain:

- founder ownership after transaction;
- continuing operational commitment;
- governance;
- long-term incentives.

# 9. "Is Agentic Trusted Execution just AI hype?"

## Answer

It will be hype if Circularo presents it as something already achieved.

It becomes credible when presented as a long-term adjacency.

The underlying problem is real:

> When AI agents take consequential institutional actions, organizations need authority, policy, execution control, evidence and accountability.

Circularo already has relevant trust foundations.

But generalized Agentic Trusted Execution still requires:

- product development;
- customer validation;
- design partners;
- standards work;
- commercial validation.

It should therefore be valued as optionality rather than current revenue.

# 10. "Why Circularo rather than an IAM or cybersecurity company?"

## Answer

Identity and access management primarily answer:

> Who are you and what systems/resources can you access?

Circularo's proposed Trusted Execution domain is broader:

> Under what organizational authority can this actor perform this particular action, how should that action be executed, and what evidence should exist afterward?

Circularo should complement IAM and security infrastructure rather than claim to replace it.

## Answer

Use four explicit classifications in all investor materials:

### PRODUCTION

Capabilities demonstrably available and used today.

Examples include, subject to confirmation against the latest product master:

- enterprise eSignatures;
- approvals and workflows;
- UAE Pass integration;
- Nafath integration;
- audit trails and evidence;
- timestamps and certificates;
- document management and archiving;
- REST API;
- SaaS;
- self-hosted Ultimate;
- multi-tenant shared services;
- sovereign deployments.

### IN DEVELOPMENT

Capabilities actively being developed and for which there is a confirmed product initiative.

### ROADMAP

Capabilities that Circularo intends to develop but that are not currently available in production.

### LONG-TERM VISION

Strategic architecture and category direction that may include:

- eDoc as the canonical trust object;
- HOROS;
- AUTHOS;
- ACTUS;
- SANTOS;
- generalized Trusted Records;
- Trusted Execution;
- Agentic Trusted Execution.

The exact status of each component must be verified against the current Product Strategy before external use.

Never allow an investor deck to visually or verbally imply that long-term architecture is already production.

## 38. Management Rule for Difficult Questions

Do not try to win every argument.

Use this structure:

> **What we know:** evidence.

> **What we believe:** management interpretation.

> **What we expect:** forecast or scenario.

> **What remains to be proven:** risk.

This should become the standard for investor conversations.

---

## 39. Core Investor Answer

When the conversation becomes overly focused on either today's small scale or tomorrow's large vision, return to the central investment question:

> Is Circularo merely a ~$3M eSignature SaaS business, or has it built a profitable digital-trust platform with unusually strategic sovereign positions, an embedded path to materially larger recurring revenue, scalable platform distribution and credible long-term optionality in Trusted Execution?

Circularo's job is not to tell investors which answer to choose.

Circularo's job is to provide enough evidence for them to reach an informed conclusion.
