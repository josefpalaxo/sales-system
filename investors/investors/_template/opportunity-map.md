## Quantification and attribution

Record unit assumptions, eligible population, adoption, price source ID, timing, recurring/non-recurring treatment, sales channel, probability basis if used, dependencies and overlap with other opportunities. Do not copy commercial price rules; resolve the master references.

Never present scenario ARR as pipeline, aggregate incompatible categories, count the same account twice, or attribute existing revenue to the prospective investor without evidence.
