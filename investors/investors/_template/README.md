# Investor workspace template

Copy this directory to `investors/<normalized-investor-name>/`. Replace `{{investor-name}}`, `{{owner}}` and `{{date}}` throughout the copied workspace. Do not create a real opportunity from a name in a source brief without evidence it is relevant.

| Question | Working record |
| --- | --- |
| Who are they? | [Investor profile](investor-profile.md) |
| Why could they care? | [Strategic fit](strategic-fit.md) |
| How could they accelerate Circularo? | [Opportunity map](opportunity-map.md) |
| Who are we speaking with; what happened? | [Contacts](contacts.md) and [interaction log](interaction-log.md) |
| What should we tell them? | [Meeting preparation](meeting-prep.md) and [deliverables](deliverables/README.md) |
| What happens next? | [Next actions](next-actions.md) |
| What remains unresolved? | [Questions and objections](questions-and-objections.md) |

Use [research](research/README.md), [meetings](meetings/README.md), [correspondence](correspondence/README.md) and [analysis](analysis/README.md) for supporting work. Private contact data and raw correspondence stay in the approved business system or ignored local files.

Read the [investor master](../../master/investor-materials-master.md) first. Link company facts instead of copying them. Separate evidence about the investor from hypotheses about fit. Never turn a portfolio relationship or scenario into pipeline. External messages and deliverables require approval before sending/sharing.
