# **Circularo — Investor Positioning Brief**

**Confidential — October 2026**

## **1\. Purpose**

This brief is the strategic context required to position Circularo consistently, identify the right investor universe, and develop the investor materials and outreach strategy.

Circularo should **not be positioned simply as a \~\$3M+ ARR eSignature SaaS company**, nor as a conventional Seed / Series A fundraising opportunity.

Circularo is a profitable, founder-funded digital trust technology company that has already established unusually strategic positions within UAE government, enterprise, and shared-service infrastructure.

**Investment thesis:**  
**Proven profitable business → durable recurring revenue → embedded expansion → platform multiplication → sovereign/shared-service position → Trust Orchestration → Trusted Records → Agentic Trusted Execution**

The immediate opportunity is to multiply what Circularo has already built. Future opportunities should strengthen the strategic investment case, but should not be presented as current ARR, contracted revenue, or released capability unless confirmed.

## **2\. Transaction Context**

Circularo has been built without external institutional equity funding and does not require new capital to fund existing operations or establish product-market fit. The founders are now considering introducing the **first external shareholder** to accelerate the next stage of growth.

This is therefore not primarily a conventional fundraising process. The objective is to find the **right shareholder and the right transaction**.

### **Current preferred strategic scenario**

- **Transaction size:** approximately USD 10–15M  
- **Ownership:** up to approximately 25%  
- **Founder/shareholder liquidity:** meaningful secondary component required  
- **Primary capital:** optional where it creates additional strategic value  
- **Investor profile:** strategic value strongly preferred in addition to capital

The previous USD 6M primary raise / USD 40M pre-money concept should **not be treated as the fixed transaction structure or valuation anchor**.

Circularo is open to a predominantly secondary transaction, a combination of secondary liquidity and primary growth capital, or a larger institutional growth-equity transaction incorporating meaningful founder liquidity.

> **Any transaction must include meaningful liquidity for the existing founders/shareholders.**

We would like to determine which structures, investor categories, and transaction sizes produce the strongest combination of valuation, liquidity, strategic contribution, and long-term alignment.

## **3\. The Investor We Are Looking For**

Circularo is looking for more than financial capital. The preferred shareholder should ideally contribute:

**Capital \+ Network \+ Distribution \+ Strategic Access \+ Institutional Credibility**

Potential investors include UAE/GCC strategic investors, family offices and investment holding companies, corporate strategics/CVCs, sovereign or sovereign-linked organizations, growth-equity investors, selected PE investors, and international investors with relevant GCC, government, enterprise, technology, or digital-infrastructure exposure.

A UAE or GCC strategic shareholder is particularly attractive, but the process should not be geographically restricted if an international investor offers a compelling combination of valuation, founder liquidity, strategic capability, and long-term alignment.

> **Can this shareholder help Circularo multiply revenue through its ecosystem, portfolio companies, government relationships, enterprise relationships, or distribution channels?**

We would like to systematically explore the broader investor universe and identifying opportunities Circularo would not necessarily reach directly.

## **5\. Core Positioning**

Circularo is a **digital trust technology company** focused on helping organizations and governments keep consequential digital processes connected, controlled, and verifiable.

**Who acted? → What were they authorized to do? → What was approved? → What was executed? → What evidence remains afterwards?**

Circularo's strategic proposition is **Trust Orchestration**: bringing content, identity, authority, workflows, approvals, trust services, and evidence together around the business process.

The intended outcome is **Trusted Execution**: a consequential digital action carried out under appropriate controls, with evidence of what was authorized and what occurred.

> **Circularo orchestrates trust. Trusted Execution is the outcome.**

## **6\. eSignature Was the Entry Point — Not the Strategic Endpoint**

**Digital Signatures → Digital Trust → Trusted Execution → Agentic Trusted Execution**

This describes expanding strategic scope, not four completed product generations. eSignature gave Circularo an entry point into identity, approvals, organizational authority, trust services, evidence, and records.

| Area | Strategic role |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Content & Collaboration** | Connect preparation, versions, and review to the subsequent decision |

## **15\. Trusted Records — Value Continues After Execution**

A completed process creates more than a signed document.

**Content \+ Provenance \+ Authority \+ Decisions \+ Trust Events \+ Process Evidence**

This evidence can support audit, investigation, compliance, dispute resolution, institutional memory, future automation, and better-grounded AI context.

## **16\. Agentic AI — Longer-Term Strategic Optionality**

Circularo's AI opportunity should **not** primarily be described as adding generative AI features to an eSignature product.

AI is progressing from:

**Know → Advise → Act**

Once AI agents initiate consequential actions, organizations must establish identity, authority, permitted context, required approvals, permitted execution, and retained evidence.

> **Intelligence does not create institutional authority.**

Circularo's strategic opportunity is at the **execution boundary**: where a proposed action becomes an authorized organizational action.

**Identify actor → Establish mandate → Check proposed action → Obtain required approval → Apply required trust services → Execute through permitted path → Retain evidence**

This creates a potential future role for Circularo as the **trust, authority, execution, and evidence layer between AI agents and consequential business actions**.

This is strategic direction and category optionality, not current forecast revenue. A separate brief, **Circularo — AI Opportunity in the Agentic Era**, provides the deeper thesis.
