# **Circularo – Investor Process & Transaction Context**

## **Purpose**

Ahead of our discussion, we wanted to provide additional context regarding Circularo's objectives for engaging with investors.

**The Pre-Call Memo** provides a useful overview of the company and identifies several areas we should discuss further. However, our thinking regarding the type of investor, transaction structure and strategic objective has evolved beyond the conventional fundraising structure currently reflected in the memo.

The following points are therefore an important context for our discussion.

## **1\. Investment Context — Where Circularo Is Today**

### **1\. We are already a profitable, bootstrapped business**

Circularo has been built without external equity funding and is profitable and debt-free.

We are therefore not approaching investors from the conventional position of a startup that requires capital to continue operating or prove product-market fit.

We have already built the technology, established the business and demonstrated that customers are willing to deploy Circularo in demanding enterprise and government environments.

The question for us now is not whether Circularo can become a viable business.

The question is **how we multiply what has already been built.**

### **2\. We have established unusually strategic infrastructure positions in the UAE**

Circularo should not be viewed simply as a software company with several government customers.

Our technology already powers major shared-service and sovereign digital-trust platforms, including:

- **TDRA / GovSign** — federal-government digital signing infrastructure  
- **Digital Dubai / DigitalSign** — shared infrastructure serving the Dubai Government ecosystem  
- **Sharjah Government / Sharjah Sign** — shared-service infrastructure across Sharjah Government  
- **e& / DigiSign** — strategic telco distribution opportunity  
- **TCC / Mokham** — sovereign digital-signature and trust platform in Saudi Arabia

These deployments demonstrate something materially more valuable than individual enterprise contracts: **Circularo technology can become shared infrastructure distributed across entire government, enterprise or partner ecosystems.**

We believe this is one of the most important elements of the Circularo investment thesis.

### **3\. The next phase is multiplication, not product-market-fit discovery**

Circularo's growth opportunity comes from multiplying proven positions through several reinforcing engines:

**Sovereign Shared Services**  
Expand existing government platforms and replicate the model across additional governments and jurisdictions.

**Strategic Distribution**  
Use telcos, technology companies, service providers and large institutional partners to distribute Circularo across their customer and portfolio ecosystems.

**Enterprise SaaS**  
Continue expanding direct enterprise adoption, particularly across regulated and high-value sectors.

**Geographic Expansion**  
Build on our UAE position and KSA partnership to expand across the GCC and selectively into international markets.

The investment proposition is therefore fundamentally about **Platform Multiplication**: turning a relatively small number of strategic platform relationships into access to potentially hundreds or thousands of organizations.

## **2\. Why We Are Considering an External Shareholder Now**

Circularo has reached a stage where the right shareholder could materially accelerate this multiplication.

We therefore want to find **the right shareholder, not simply capital or the highest financial bidder.**

An attractive investor could be:

- a UAE/GCC strategic investor;  
- family office or investment holding company;  
- corporate strategic investor or CVC;  
- sovereign or sovereign-linked investment organization;  
- growth-equity investor;  
- selected private-equity investor;  
- international investor with substantial GCC, government, enterprise or technology relationships.

Ideally, the shareholder should be capable of helping Circularo create commercial opportunities through its network, portfolio companies, government relationships, enterprise relationships or distribution capabilities.

Our preferred investor should therefore bring some combination of:

**Capital \+ Network \+ Distribution \+ Strategic Access \+ Institutional Credibility**

## **3\. Transaction Objective**

The USD 6M primary raise / USD 40M pre-money structure referenced in the initial Pre-Call Memo reflects an earlier fundraising concept and should not be treated as the current fixed transaction.

The founders are prepared to consider selling a meaningful minority interest in Circularo.

Our current preferred strategic scenario is approximately:

**USD 10–15M transaction**  
**Up to approximately 25% ownership**  
**Meaningful founder/shareholder liquidity**

A fundamental requirement is that:

**Any transaction must include a meaningful secondary component providing liquidity to the existing founders/shareholders.**

However, we deliberately do not want to over-prescribe the structure before testing the market.

We are open to:

- predominantly or entirely secondary transactions;  
- a combination of secondary founder liquidity and primary growth capital;  
- larger institutional growth-equity transactions incorporating meaningful founder liquidity.

Primary capital may be considered where it creates additional strategic value, but a conventional primary capital raise alone is not our objective.

We would particularly value advice on the optimal transaction structure and how different structures affect the available investor universe and valuation.

## **4\. Valuation — We Prefer to Test the Market**

We do not want to mechanically anchor the current process to the previous USD 40M pre-money concept.

Circularo's valuation should reflect not only current revenue but also:

- profitable and bootstrapped operating history;  
- recurring revenue quality;  
- long-term government and enterprise relationships;  
- sovereign/shared-service infrastructure positions;  
- expansion potential inside existing platforms;  
- strategic distribution opportunities;  
- regulatory, sovereignty and national-identity barriers to entry;  
- the potential multiplication effect of government and strategic partner ecosystems;  
- the company's future Digital Trust and Trusted Execution opportunity.

We  would value independent perspective based on relevant transactions, investor appetite and market feedback.

## **5\. Where We Want to Take Circularo**

Our ambition extends materially beyond building a larger eSignature company.

Circularo has evolved from:

**Digital Signatures → Digital Trust → Trusted Execution → Agentic Trusted Execution**

Our existing business provides the commercial and technological foundation.

The longer-term opportunity is to establish Circularo as infrastructure for trusted digital interactions between people, organizations, systems and eventually autonomous AI agents.

This includes trusted identity, authority, execution, evidence and records — areas that become increasingly important as AI systems begin taking actions on behalf of organizations and individuals.

The right investor should therefore understand both:

**the proven, profitable business Circularo has today**

and

**the substantially larger Digital Trust / Trusted Execution opportunity we are building toward.**

## **6\. Long-Term Objective**

We want the right shareholder to help Circularo move from approximately **USD 3M+ recurring revenue today toward a materially larger scale**.

The objective is to:

- expand sovereign/shared-service deployments across the GCC and internationally;  
- multiply distribution through strategic partners;  
- accelerate enterprise SaaS;  
- establish Circularo as a significant UAE technology company;  
- develop the Digital Trust and Trusted Execution category;  
- strengthen governance and institutional maturity.

Over the longer term, our ambition is to build sufficient scale and institutional readiness to consider a potential **IPO pathway**.

The first external shareholder could therefore become an important long-term partner through subsequent growth phases and potentially future capital-market events.

## **7\. What We Would Like to Explore**

During our discussion, we would particularly value perspective on:

1. How Circularo should be positioned given its profitable, bootstrapped and relatively mature profile.  
2. Whether the opportunity should be positioned as strategic/growth equity rather than Seed / Series A.  
3. Which investor categories are most appropriate where founder liquidity is a mandatory component.  
4. The optimal balance between primary and secondary capital.  
5. How to communicate the strategic value of Circularo's sovereign/shared-service footprint.  
6. Appropriate valuation methodology and relevant comparable transactions.  
7. Investor appetite for a USD 10–15M+ transaction incorporating substantial secondary liquidity.  
8. How to prioritize GCC strategic investors versus international institutional investors.  
9. How to present the proven business today alongside the larger Digital Trust / Trusted Execution opportunity.  


## **Core Investment Proposition**

**Circularo is not looking for capital to prove its business model.**

We have built a profitable, bootstrapped technology company and established strategic digital-trust infrastructure positions across the UAE and GCC.

**The next phase is about multiplication.**

We are looking for the right external shareholder and transaction to help transform those positions into a substantially larger regional and international technology platform — while providing meaningful liquidity to the founders who have built and financed the company to date.
