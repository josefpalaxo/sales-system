#### **Q1. What is the company, how is it branded, and where is it based?** {#q1.-what-is-the-company,-how-is-it-branded,-and-where-is-it-based?}

**A:** Circularo is a **Digital Trust technology company headquartered in Dubai, UAE**, developing infrastructure that enables governments and enterprises to execute trusted digital processes with verifiable identity, authority, control and evidence.

Circularo’s strategic proposition is **Trust Orchestration** — connecting identity, organizational authority, workflows, approvals, trust services, execution and trusted records within a unified platform.

It owns 100% of **Circularo MENA \- FZE (Dubai)**, responsible for GCC operations, and **Circularo Europe s.r.o.**, supporting European operations.

Circularo is headquartered and commercially anchored in the UAE, with product and engineering capabilities in Europe and customers and strategic deployments across the GCC and international markets.

#### **Q2. How does the company position itself?** {#q2.-how-does-the-company-position-itself?}

**A:** Circularo positions itself as a **Digital Trust and Trust Orchestration platform** for governments, regulated industries and large enterprises.

Circularo connects the critical components required for trusted digital action — **identity, organizational authority, workflows, approvals, trust services, execution, evidence and trusted records** — within a unified platform that can operate as SaaS, private infrastructure or sovereign shared services.

### **2\. Problem Definition** {#2.-problem-definition}

#### **Q1. What core problem is the company solving?** {#q1.-what-core-problem-is-the-company-solving?}

**A:** Many important business and government processes are still **paper-based, manual or only partially digitized**. Circularo helps organizations move these processes into secure digital workflows, replacing paper, manual approvals and fragmented document handling with controlled digital collaboration, approvals, signatures and records.

But digitization alone does not solve the deeper problem.

As processes become digital, the systems used to establish **identity, authority, approvals, execution and evidence are often fragmented across different applications, providers and records**.

This creates a fundamental trust problem: organizations need to establish **who or what performed an action, whether they were authorized to do so, what was approved, what was actually executed and what evidence remains afterwards**.

Circularo addresses this by **orchestrating the trust around digital processes and actions**, connecting content, identity, organizational authority, workflows, approvals, trust services, execution and evidence within a unified platform.

For governments and regulated industries, this also requires **data sovereignty, local identity integration, regulatory compliance, deployment control and institutional accountability**.

As AI progresses from assisting people to increasingly **acting on behalf of organizations**, the same problem extends further: intelligence alone does not establish authority. Organizations need infrastructure that determines **who or what may act, under whose authority, through which controls, and with what verifiable evidence is retained afterwards**.

**Circularo therefore solves the problem at two levels: helping organizations securely digitize important processes today, while providing the trust infrastructure required to keep increasingly digital and automated actions controlled, authorized and verifiable.**

---

#### **Q2. How does the company describe the magnitude or urgency of the problem?** {#q2.-how-does-the-company-describe-the-magnitude-or-urgency-of-the-problem?}

**A:** The need for trusted digital infrastructure is being driven by several structural shifts happening at the same time.

First, governments and enterprises are still moving large volumes of **paper-based, manual and fragmented processes into digital workflows**. As more important processes become digital, organizations need stronger assurance around identity, authority, approvals, integrity and evidence.

Second, **regulatory, data-residency and digital-sovereignty requirements are increasing**, particularly across government, financial services, critical infrastructure and other regulated sectors. Organizations increasingly need greater control over where sensitive data is processed, how trust services are delivered and how digital transactions can be independently verified.

Third, digital processes are becoming increasingly **interconnected and automated**. Transactions now span multiple organizations, applications, identity systems and jurisdictions, making fragmented trust infrastructure progressively harder to govern.

Finally, **AI is beginning to move from generating and analysing information toward initiating and executing actions**. This introduces a new level of urgency. Organizations will need to establish not only whether an AI-generated output is reliable, but whether an AI agent is authorized to act, under whose authority, within which policies and with what evidence retained afterwards.

These trends expand the problem from simply digitizing documents into a much larger requirement for **trusted digital execution**.

Circularo sees this convergence of **digitization, regulation, sovereignty, automation and Agentic AI** as creating a growing need for infrastructure that keeps digital actions secure, authorized, controlled and verifiable.

#### **Q2. What is their specific solution thesis?** {#q2.-what-is-their-specific-solution-thesis?}

The platform therefore brings together:

**Content → Identity → Authority → Workflow & Approval → Trust Services → Trusted Execution → Evidence & Trusted Records**

**Direct Enterprise Adoption**  
Circularo deployed directly within large organizations.

**Sovereign & Shared-Service Platforms**

Longer term, the same thesis extends naturally into Agentic AI. As humans, systems and AI agents increasingly participate in the same processes, Circularo’s role can expand from orchestrating trusted human workflows toward **governing trusted execution across human, system and autonomous actors**.

#### **Q3. How does the product work in practice?** {#q3.-how-does-the-product-work-in-practice?}

**A:** Circularo uses the same underlying Digital Trust platform across different operating and deployment models, allowing customers to adopt it according to their scale, security, sovereignty and integration requirements.

**For enterprise SaaS customers**, Circularo is provided as a managed cloud service with regional hosting. Organizations use secure workspaces to prepare and collaborate on documents, manage workflows and approvals, verify participants, execute digital signatures and retain complete evidence and records of the process.

**For governments, large holdings, telcos and service providers**, Circularo can operate as a multi-tenant shared-service platform. A single deployment can securely serve multiple independent organizations, departments or downstream customers, each with its own users, administration, branding, permissions and processes.

Circularo also integrates with existing enterprise and government ecosystems through **REST APIs, SSO, identity systems and trust-service integrations**, including regional capabilities such as UAE PASS and Nafath.

In practice, Circularo therefore does not require customers to replace their existing business systems. It can provide the **trust layer around their digital processes**, connecting:

**Content → Identity → Authority → Workflow & Approval → Trust Services → Execution → Evidence & Records**

This deployment flexibility is strategically important because the same core technology can serve an individual enterprise, operate inside a customer’s infrastructure, or become shared digital trust infrastructure serving an entire ecosystem.

REST APIs and enterprise integration capabilities allow Circularo to operate as a trust layer within existing ERP, CRM, DMS, government and industry applications rather than requiring customers to replace their existing systems.

**Regional Identity & Trust Integration**  
Native integration with regional identity and trust ecosystems, including UAE PASS and Nafath, allows identity, authentication and signing capabilities to be incorporated directly into customer processes.

**Enterprise & Government Localization**
