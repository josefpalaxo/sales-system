# Research

Research is supporting evidence and analysis, not accepted master truth. Separate external verification from internal estimates and hypotheses.

- [Market](market/README.md)
- [Competitors](competitors/README.md)
- [Transactions](transactions/README.md)
- [Valuation](valuation/README.md)
- [Strategic investors](strategic-investors/README.md)

Record source ID, exact URL/locator, author, publication date, accessed date, scope, methodology, evidence state and classification. Preserve contradictory findings. Promote only generally relevant, reviewed claims to the smallest master record. Investor-specific research belongs in its workspace.
