# Circularo Investor Customer Revenue & Retention Analysis

## Objective

Build a rigorous, investor-ready analysis of Circularo's historical customer revenue, recurring revenue quality, retention, expansion, churn, customer longevity, revenue concentration, product mix, geographic mix and sales-channel economics.

The analysis will support Circularo's strategic investment / valuation work with M-Capital.

The primary objective is to answer:

> How predictable, durable, scalable and expandable is Circularo's existing revenue base?

The analysis should identify both positive and negative findings. Do not optimize the analysis to prove a predetermined valuation.

---

# 1. Source Data & Hierarchy

We cannot rely on Odoo's Sales Order change/history log as an authoritative historical record.

Use the following hierarchy:

### Historical financial truth
**Posted customer invoices and credit notes**

Use these to establish:
- actual historical revenue;
- customer revenue by period;
- recurring vs non-recurring revenue where identifiable;
- revenue expansion/contraction;
- geographic/channel/product mix;
- customer concentration.

### Contractual / subscription context
**Sales Orders**

Use these to establish where reliable:
- products;
- plans;
- editions;
- quantities;
- users/transactions;
- subscription/contract dates;
- billing periods;
- commercial terms;
- partners/channels;
- renewal context.

Do NOT attempt to reconstruct historical state from unreliable Sales Order change logs.

### Subscription cross-check
Existing Active and Churned subscription exports can be used to validate:
- first contract year;
- active/churned status;
- churn year;
- current ARR.

Where sources disagree, flag the discrepancy rather than silently choosing a value.

+ New Logo ARR

# 5. Retention & Churn

Calculate separately:

## Logo retention

- Opening customers
- New customers
- Churned customers
- Closing customers
- Logo retention
- Logo churn

## Revenue retention

Calculate:

### Gross Revenue Retention (GRR)

Opening ARR  
− contraction  
− churn  

divided by Opening ARR

### Net Revenue Retention (NRR)

Opening ARR  
− contraction  
− churn  
+ expansion  

divided by Opening ARR

Calculate annually wherever historical data permits.

Do not substitute logo churn for ARR churn.

Validate any previously reported Circularo NRR/churn figures against the reconstructed data.

If definitions differ, explain why.

# 21. Analysis Principles

Investor credibility is more important than producing attractive metrics.

Therefore:

- do not present unsigned pipeline as ARR;
- do not confuse invoice revenue with ARR;
- do not confuse logo churn with revenue churn;
- do not annualize YTD figures without explicitly labeling the calculation;
- do not silently correct source data;
- document every material calculation;
- preserve reproducibility;
- flag low sample sizes;
- distinguish facts from analytical inference;
- use posted invoices as historical financial truth;
- use Sales Orders for contractual/product context;
- reconcile calculated metrics against reported management metrics and explain differences.

The final analysis should be capable of surviving investor financial due diligence.

# Desired Strategic Outcome

We specifically want to determine whether historical evidence supports the following investment thesis:

> **Circularo has built a durable recurring-revenue base in which customers remain for long periods, expand their usage over time, and increasingly adopt higher-value enterprise and shared-service deployments. This installed base, combined with scalable partner distribution and embedded expansion within strategic government and enterprise environments, creates a materially stronger growth profile than new-logo SaaS acquisition alone.**

Do not assume this thesis is correct.

Use the data to prove, qualify, or reject each component.
