# About Circularo

## Digital Trust Infrastructure for Organizations, Governments and the Agentic AI Era

Circularo is a digital trust technology company headquartered in Dubai, providing enterprise and government platforms for trusted digital processes.

Rather than treating electronic signing as an isolated action, Circularo brings together **business processes, trust services and trusted records** to provide a Trusted Execution Layer connecting people, organizations, applications and increasingly AI agents.

**DIGITAL → AUTOMATED → AI-ASSISTED → AGENTIC**
