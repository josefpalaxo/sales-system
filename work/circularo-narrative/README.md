## How to maintain this set

The dictionary owns working terminology. The Strategic Narrative owns the story. The other three narratives apply it. The explainers develop the reasoning for onboarding and internal alignment. Update definitions in the dictionary and reconcile their use across the narratives and explainers; avoid creating independent glossaries in individual decks.

Use **trusted action** for the act to be governed. Use **Trust Orchestration** for the mechanism and **Trusted Execution** for the governed outcome under applicable requirements, supported by verifiable evidence. Legal effect belongs in the intended outcome where applicable; actual claims require validated scope and evidence.

Keep current capability, integrations, development and strategic direction visibly distinct. This working set does not authorize product, corporate, customer, certification, regulatory or commercial claims. Approved governed records retain authority for their stated scope.
