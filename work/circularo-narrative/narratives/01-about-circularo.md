## Company introduction

Circularo is a digital trust technology company focused on helping organizations and governments keep trusted digital processes connected, controlled and verifiable.

An agreement, approval or official document carries more than content. It carries a decision: who acted, what they were authorized to do, what was approved and what evidence remains. Circularo's focus is the connection between those elements, from preparation and review through signing and the resulting record.

Our platform proposition is **Trust Orchestration**: bringing content, identity, authority, workflows, approvals, trust services and evidence together around a business process. The intended outcome is **Trusted Execution**: the governed completion of a trusted action under the applicable authority, policy, assurance, regulatory and legal requirements, with evidence that allows the organization to verify the outcome.

**Circularo orchestrates trust. Trusted Execution is the outcome.**

## Where Circularo fits

For enterprises, the story starts with digital work: preparing documents, coordinating participants, obtaining approvals and signatures, and retaining the context behind the completed process.

For governments and organizations with sovereignty requirements, the story extends to how these processes use existing identity and trust infrastructure within the required operating environment. For shared-service operators, the opportunity is to make common trust capabilities reusable across participating entities.

These conversations share a platform vision. The deployment, integrations and responsibilities must be established for each offering and customer environment.

## The next chapter

As organizations give AI agents a role in trusted actions, authority and evidence become more important. An agent's ability to propose an action does not establish its permission to execute it.

Circularo's strategic ambition is to extend the same trust model to agentic execution: connecting a permitted action to its delegated authority, required approvals and retained evidence. This is the next chapter of the narrative; specific agentic capabilities must be described according to their confirmed product status.
