## Post 2 Coordinating the process

A signature is one step in a document transaction.

Before signing, an internal team may need to review the document. A manager may need to approve it. An external participant may need to complete an identity check.

Afterward, the business application needs the result and a record of what happened.

Circularo Unified Trust API connects those steps through a common interface.

Specify who needs to review, approve, accept or sign. Route actions in sequence or in parallel. Apply the configured identity and trust requirements, then follow progress through document events.

The value is in how the required steps work together.
