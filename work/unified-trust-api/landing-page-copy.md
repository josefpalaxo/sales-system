## Connect the steps of a trusted transaction

A document transaction can involve more than signing. Participants need to be verified. Reviews and approvals must happen in the right order. The executed document and its evidence need to return to the business.

Circularo brings these steps together through a common API. Connect your CRM, ERP, portal or business application to a configured document process, follow its progress and retrieve the result.

## Integrate the outcome not the infrastructure

Build the document process your application needs, with the required trust capabilities working together.

### Verify participants

Apply identity verification requirements for external recipients, including configured KYC checks such as ID verification and liveness detection.

### Coordinate reviews and approvals

Define who needs to review, approve, accept or sign. Route actions in sequence or in parallel, with mandatory actions and quorum requirements where needed.

### Sign documents

Connect electronic and digital signing to the document workflow, so signing happens within the required process.

### Apply seals and timestamps

Add a certificate seal, a trusted timestamp or both when your document requires them.

### Retrieve document evidence

Generate a document audit trail showing actions, participants and events throughout its lifecycle.

### Follow progress through events

Use webhooks to keep your application connected to document status changes and workflow progress.

*Identity and trust services depend on the providers, configuration and entitlements available for your use case.*

## From application request to executed document

### Start the transaction

Create a document from a file or template and apply the configured workflow from your application.

### Coordinate the required actions

Set the participants, action order and verification requirements. Connect approvals and signing with the trust services your process needs.

### Return the result

Receive progress events, retrieve the executed document and generate its audit trail for your business record.

## Put trusted execution into your business processes

### Contract execution

Initiate a contract process from your CRM. Coordinate internal approvals and counterparty signatures, then return the executed document and its evidence to the business record.

### Digital service delivery

Connect a portal application to participant verification, required approvals and document execution. Retrieve the resulting document and audit trail as part of the service process.

### Enterprise automation

Use an event in your ERP or business system to initiate a document workflow. Follow required actions through to completion and use events to update the originating application.

## Frequently asked questions

### How does Unified Trust API relate to the Circularo REST API

Unified Trust API brings document workflows, participant verification, signing, sealing, timestamps and evidence together through the Circularo REST API. Developers use the API's documented operations to initiate processes, follow progress and retrieve results.

### Can I use it for a simple signing workflow

Yes. Use the actions and trust services your process requires, from a signing workflow to a document transaction with verification, approvals and additional trust steps.

### Can I connect it to my business applications

The REST API lets your application initiate document processes, receive events and retrieve documents and audit evidence. The integration is designed around your application's requirements and the configured Circularo workflow.
