Thank you for the constructive discussion and for outlining DTC’s evaluation priorities. We are pleased that the Circularo Digital Sign solution appears aligned with key areas of interest, including API capability, bulk-signing workflows, user administration and deployment flexibility.

Please find attached our *Circularo Licensing & Commercial Model Overview — DTC*. It sets out the approved model at a high level and distinguishes the areas that should be tailored once DTC shares its consolidated business and technical requirements.

In summary:

- Circularo supports user-based licensing for human-centric signing and a separate consumption-based model for API, automation, integration, bulk-processing and advanced trust-service use cases.
- Regular Users receive the applicable annual transaction allowance; Lite Users provide a lower-cost, restricted-user option. Administrator is a role assigned to a licensed user rather than a separate user licence, and external recipients/signatories are free and unlimited.
- Enterprise is Circularo-managed SaaS/cloud deployment. Ultimate is the self-hosted/on-premises deployment option.
- QuickSign and bulk-signing use cases should be scoped separately. They should not be assumed to fall within an unlimited human-transaction entitlement, particularly for executive or high-volume approval patterns.

To provide a transparent, long-term commercial proposal, we would welcome DTC’s expected licensed-user mix, annual signing volumes, API/integration workflows, bulk-signing and QuickSign patterns, required trust services, deployment preference, and growth outlook. On that basis, we can propose a predictable annual structure for user-led activity, with clearly defined capacity for automated and high-volume services, rather than asking DTC to repeatedly re-evaluate consumption as ordinary human signing grows.

The attached overview is intentionally not a quotation. Commercial pricing, precise API transaction calculation and consumption tiers, and the final scope of QuickSign/bulk-signing require DTC’s usage inputs and will be confirmed in a tailored proposal.

We look forward to receiving the consolidated requirements and continuing the evaluation.
