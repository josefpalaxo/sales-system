## Coverage and handling

- All 92 populated source-name rows are retained; 91 have a source number. No final unique-entity count is asserted.
- Existing-user entries total 1,830, including RTA's 1,000; the proposed purchase includes them under S07. This is a sourced starting point, not a verified allocation or migration schedule.
- Workforce figures mix periods and scopes and must not be summed as a current portfolio total. Candidate values cannot enter a base case until confirmed.
- No user percentage from `91 Entities Model` has been reused. No survey respondent has been assigned to a named entity without evidence.
- Prioritize resolving alias groups and surveying paid-license roles within large entities before filling smaller headcount gaps. Use official DDA aggregate entity/role data where available.
