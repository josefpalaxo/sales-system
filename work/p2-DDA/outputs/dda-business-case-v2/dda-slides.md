# Digital Dubai Authority — Circularo Central Procurement
## Executive Management Proposal

### Purpose

Create a concise executive presentation for Digital Dubai Authority (DDA) seeking approval for a **centrally procured Circularo Enterprise shared-service framework for Dubai Government entities**.

The presentation must lead toward a clear decision:

> **Approve a five-year centrally funded Circularo Enterprise framework, starting with 10,000 users at AED 1,180 per user/year, billed annually with the agreed subscription unit price locked for five years.**

The presentation should not feel like a conventional software sales deck. It should read as an **executive business case and procurement decision**.

---

# Core Executive Narrative

The story should be:

**Fragmented entity procurement today**  
→ **Central DDA procurement**  
→ **Lower subscription cost**  
→ **Enterprise Plan for participating entities**  
→ **Consistent government-wide service**  
→ **Simpler onboarding and expansion**  
→ **Five-year budget predictability**  
→ **Scalable shared service toward 15,200-user potential**

The financial savings support the decision, but **savings should not be the entire story**.

The stronger proposition is:

> **DDA can use its government-wide purchasing scale to simultaneously reduce subscription cost, upgrade participating entities to Circularo Enterprise, standardize capabilities, simplify procurement and create a scalable Dubai Government shared service.**

# Accuracy / Modeling Guardrails

The slides must preserve the following distinctions.

### 1. Existing customer savings

These use actual supplied subscription prices and are the strongest directly evidenced savings case.

### 2. Survey and Other savings

These are modeled against the AED 2,950 benchmark where comparable actual expenditure is unavailable.

Do not present them as audited existing government expenditure.

### 3. Support

Support is separate from subscription savings.

Do not deduct support from, or add it to, subscription-savings totals.

### 4. Included add-on value

The ~AED 20K/entity/year figure is an indicative additional value / avoided-cost estimate.

Do not add it to subscription savings.

### 5. 15,200 users

This is estimated potential based on:

76,000 employees × 20% adoption.

It is not a committed forecast, guaranteed adoption level or additional cohort to be added to the 10K model.

### 6. 10K adoption bridge

The 10K case consists of:

- 1,310 existing users
- 655 expected Year 1 existing-user growth
- 4,921 survey-supported numerical minimum
- 3,114 additional adoption target

Do not describe the 3,114 users as identified or surveyed demand.

### 7. Survey evidence

The survey supports the direction and scale of demand, but it does not prove that 10,000 users are already committed.

Use terminology such as:

- **survey-supported numerical minimum**
- **demand signal**
- **adoption potential**

Avoid:

- committed demand
- guaranteed demand
- confirmed 10K requirement

### 8. Five-year pricing

The AED 1,180 subscription unit price is locked for the five-year 10K framework under this proposal.

Annual expenditure can increase as paid user volume grows.

Therefore:

**locked unit price ≠ fixed annual expenditure**

### 9. Enterprise Plan

The strategic proposition is not merely a discount.

Participating entities receive the **Circularo Enterprise Plan**, including most agreed feature-based capabilities/add-ons within the central package.

Do not imply that every Circularo optional product or specialist service is included.

KYC, DESC eSeal Certificates, Collaboration & DMS and AI Services remain separately purchasable under the current proposal.

### 10. Support above 10K

The proposed preferential **12.5% support rate applies above 10,000 users**.

Do not show 12.5% as the support rate for exactly 10,000 users unless separately approved.
