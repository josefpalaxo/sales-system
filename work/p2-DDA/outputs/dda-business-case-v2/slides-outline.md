I would use **4 slides**.It gives enough space to separate **proven savings**, **demand/adoption**, and **commercial structure** without making the slides too dense.

The executive story should not feel like a detailed pricing proposal. It should answer four questions: **Why centralize? Why 10K? Is 10K credible? What decision is required?**

## **Slide 1 — One Dubai Government Platform. One Central Commitment.**

**Title:**  

**Central Procurement of Circularo for Dubai Government**

A five-year framework to reduce cost, simplify procurement and accelerate adoption across 92 government entities

Move from fragmented entity-by-entity purchasing to a centrally procured shared service, starting with a **10,000-user commitment** and scaling toward the estimated **15,200-user potential**.

| Existing Circularo users | 1,310 | Included |
| Year 1 existing users incl. growth | 1,965 | Included |
| Estimated total potential | 15,200 users | 10,000 starting commitment |
| Simple average of seven project unit prices | AED 2,057.19 | **AED 1,180** |
| Reduction vs simple project-price average | — | **42.64%** |
| Discount vs AED 2,950 regular price | — | **60%** |

The 15,200 potential is a planning assumption based on 76,000 employees × 20% adoption, so I would deliberately call it **“estimated potential”**, not forecast demand.

## **Slide 4 — The Decision**

**Title:**  
&nbsp;**Proposed DDA Central Procurement Framework**

This should be extremely simple.

| Commercial Term | Proposed Framework |
| ----- | ----- |
| Initial commitment | **10,000 users** |
| Subscription | **AED 1,180/user/year** |
| Annual subscription | **AED 11.8M initially** |
| Term | **5 years** |
| Billing | **Annual** |
| Unit-price indexation | **None for 5 years** |
| Coverage | **Participating Dubai Government entities** |
| Plan | **Enterprise** |
| Onboarding | AED 20,000/new entity, one-time |
| Existing entities | No new-entity onboarding charge |
| Optional services | KYC, DESC eSeal Certificates, Collaboration & DMS, AI Services |
| Support | Separately priced |

### **Decision requested**

**Approve a centrally funded five-year Circularo framework starting with 10,000 users at AED 1,180 per user/year, enabling DDA to consolidate entity-level procurement and progressively onboard Dubai Government entities under one shared-service commercial model.**

Then underneath, rather than distracting executives with detailed alternatives:

**Alternative commitment levels:** 6,000 and 8,000 users can be maintained as fallback commercial options if DDA prefers a smaller initial commitment.

---

### **One important change I would make**

I would **not make the presentation primarily about “savings.”**

The stronger executive proposition is:

**Centralization → lower price → standardization → easier adoption → predictable five-year budget → pathway to government-wide shared service.**

Savings are the financial proof supporting that decision, rather than the entire story.

And I would keep **6K and 8K out of the main four-slide narrative**. Put them in an appendix/commercial options slide if needed. The executive ask should remain unmistakably **10K**.
