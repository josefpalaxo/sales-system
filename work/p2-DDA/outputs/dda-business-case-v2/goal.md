# Our Goal

Our goal is to persuade **DDA’s executive management to centrally procure Circularo for Dubai government entities**, replacing fragmented entity-by-entity purchasing.

We want approval for a **10,000-user starting commitment**, while presenting **6,000 and 8,000 users as alternatives**, with annual billing and a five-year commitment at locked unit prices.

The business case must clearly demonstrate:

- **Savings for existing customers**, using their actual subscription prices.
- **Demand and expansion potential**, supported by the DDA survey.
- **A credible adoption path across 92 entities**, within the estimated 15,200-user potential.
- **Five-year subscription savings and budget predictability**, with support shown separately and assumptions clearly identified.

The intended outcome is a funded central procurement decision and an actionable entity-onboarding plan.

## Additional commercial benefits and terms outside the savings model

Central procurement offers DDA additional value beyond the subscription savings quantified in the Excel model. The following benefits and charges are presented separately and are not added to, or deducted from, the model’s subscription-savings totals.

### Enterprise access across participating entities

All participating entities receive the **Enterprise plan**, with most feature-based add-ons included in the agreed package. This provides a consistent level of functionality across entities and reduces the need for separate add-on purchases.

The estimated additional value from included add-ons is **AED 20,000 per entity per year on average**. This is an indicative avoided-cost benefit, dependent on each entity’s requirements and the agreed included features. It is separate from the subscription savings already demonstrated in the model and must not be counted twice.

### Preferential support pricing

For an annual commitment **above 10,000 users**, the support rate is **12.5% of annual subscription fees**, compared with the **standard rate of 25%**. This halves the support rate—a reduction of 12.5 percentage points—on the same subscription-fee base.

This additional support benefit is outside the subscription-savings comparison. The threshold is above 10,000 users; this statement does not assign the 12.5% rate to the exact 10,000-user option. The Excel model’s existing support assumptions remain unchanged.

### Five-year price certainty with annual budgeting

DDA commits for **five years**, with **annual billing** and **no indexation of the agreed subscription unit price during the five-year term**. This provides predictable unit costs and protection against subscription price increases as adoption expands.

Annual expenditure reflects the number of paid users. A locked unit price therefore provides budget predictability while allowing the service to grow; it does not imply an unchanged annual bill.

### Optional modules and onboarding

Specialist modules and add-ons remain available for separate purchase when required: **KYC, DESC eSeal Certificates, Collaboration and DMS, and AI Services**.

A **one-time onboarding package of AED 20,000 per new entity** is charged separately. Existing entities are excluded from this onboarding charge. Onboarding and optional-module costs are outside the current subscription-savings model and should be identified separately in the commercial proposal.

### Executive message

> Central procurement enables DDA to combine lower subscription prices with Enterprise access across participating entities, included add-on value, preferential support pricing for commitments above 10,000 users, and five-year subscription price certainty. Together, these benefits simplify entity-level purchasing and make wider adoption easier to budget. The Excel model quantifies subscription savings; the additional package and support benefits are presented separately.
