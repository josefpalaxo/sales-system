Workbook status: the user authorized V2-P04 for the simplified v2 model (V2-S08). ## Current design direction — V2-S07

- Present three proposal options: 5,000, 8,000 and 10,000 annually committed users.
- Define a full volume-discount ladder beginning with Tier 1 up to 1,000 users at 25% discount and 25% support, following V2-S07.
- The highest band applies above 12,000 users at a 65% discount and 12% support. Use 12,001+ as the literal whole-user threshold.
- Support is calculated on the discounted annual subscription fee. Intermediate rates and boundaries below are assistant proposals, V2-P04, subject to user acceptance.
- The employee-based potential is 15,200 users under the user's 20% adoption assumption, approximately 15,000 for planning. This is a demand assumption, not confirmed seat allocations; see V2-S05.

The previous V2-P03, V2-P02 and V2-P01 designs and calculations below are retained as discussion history only. They are superseded as the active pricing structure and must not be copied into the v2 proposal as agreed terms. In particular, the 10,000-user proposal option remains; it is not replaced with a 12,000-user option.

## Previous structure — superseded proposal

Keep the requested 5,000, 8,000 and 10,000-user options as three central commitment packages, with progressively lower rates. V2-S02 confirms that the former open-ended tier was internal only and can be redesigned. Existing customer agreements are not changed by this proposal.

Use Regular Basic Price as the primary comparison baseline, following V2-S03 in the v2 fact register. This supersedes the earlier DDA Shared Service comparison baseline for v2. Pricing below is proposed for the previously discussed five-year commitment, annual billing and no indexation. Apply the package rate to every committed user, rather than using progressive marginal bands. Intermediate quantities and upgrade effective-date mechanics need definition when the offer is finalized.

| Central package | Annual committed users | Proposed AED/user/year | Discount vs Regular Basic Price | Annual subscription AED | Annual baseline saving AED | Five-year baseline saving AED |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Entry | 5,000 | 1,416 | 52% | 7,080,000 | 7,670,000 | 38,350,000 |
| Expansion | 8,000 | 1,298 | 56% | 10,384,000 | 13,216,000 | 66,080,000 |
| Strategic | 10,000 | 1,180 | 60% | 11,800,000 | 17,700,000 | 88,500,000 |

For commitments above 10,000 users, V2-S03 requires a discount greater than 60% against the same baseline. This requires a per-user rate below the Strategic package rate. Higher-volume thresholds and exact prices remain undefined; no higher-volume savings are claimed yet. The three package prices above remain proposals for review.

All comparisons above are subscription-only at equal purchased capacity. Five-year figures assume unchanged quantities and prices under both alternatives. Support, onboarding, tax, optional services and transition costs are excluded. These are not net program savings or ROI, and do not establish actual current spending at those quantities.

## Commercial rationale

- A 52% reduction from Regular Basic Price makes the entry package substantial while preserving a reason to choose a higher commitment.
- The middle package gives DDA an intermediate budget option with a 56% discount.
- Moving from 8,000 to 10,000 users adds 25% capacity for AED 1,416,000 more annual subscription spending, a 13.64% increase. This follows from repricing the full pool at the higher commitment.
- The 10,000-user package preserves the previously discussed target price and gives a 60% discount from the v2 baseline. Higher-volume packages should exceed this discount, as instructed in V2-S03.
- The price points are commercial design recommendations, not external market benchmarks or a supplier-margin assessment. Delivery cost and margin inputs were not supplied.
