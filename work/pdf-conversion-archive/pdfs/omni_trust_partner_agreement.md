1. **Framework of the Cooperation**

This Agreement establishes the general terms governing the alliance relationship between the Parties.

Specific collaboration activities, integrations, partner classifications, technical scopes, branding rights, commercial rights, or additional obligations may be further defined in separate exhibits, addendums, or alliance attachments executed by the Parties (“Alliance Addendums”).

An Alliance Addendum shall not exceed the bounds of this Agreement, although OmniTrust and its Partner may explore other contracting solutions if the need arises.

2. **Subject Matter of Alliance Addenda**

The Alliance Addenda define the specific activities that the Parties will be undertaking.  OmniTrust and Partner may agree to more than one Alliance Addenda.

Alliance Addenda include, but are not limited to:

- website cross-linking;
- partner directory or partner listing inclusion;
- co-marketing activities;
- customer engagement activities;
- demonstrations, workshops, and events;
- joint solution alignment;
- ecosystem participation;
- interoperability and integrations;
- APIs, connectors, and technical enablement;
- reference architectures and validation activities.

Neither Party is obligated to engaging in activities defined in an Alliance Addenda without executing the Alliance Addenda.  By signing this Agreement, neither Party is obligated to any minimum level of engagement or to engagement on a particular Alliance Addenda, without executed an Alliance Addenda.

3. **Provision of Benefits**

Subject to Partner’s compliance with the terms and conditions of this Agreement and the relevant Alliance Addenda, OmniTrust will provide Partner with benefits as specified in such Alliance Addenda (hereinafter referred to as "**Benefits**").

4. **Partner Commitments**

In return for the Benefits, Partner shall fulfil the commitments as specified in the relevant Alliance Addenda (hereinafter referred to as "**Partner Commitments**").

5. **Marketing and Commercial Relationships**

The Parties agree to promote their joint efforts in connection with this Agreement according to the principles set out in the relevant Alliance Addenda.

- - 5.1 **Approval Rights: A Party must receive prior written approval from the other Party prior to doing any of the following:**

- Issuing a press release or making a public announcement discussing or disclosing the existance of this Agreement or any activity under this Agreement or an Alliance Addendum;
- Performing a case study or any type of competitive analysis on the other Party’s product offering;
- Making any public customer reference;
- Using the other Party’s logo(s) or trademark(s) in any public-facing communication; or
- Asserting interoperability or certification in any public-facing communication.

Prior written approval may be made via electronic mail (e-mail).

- 5.2 **Branding Guidelines**

Each Party agrees to comply with the other Party’s reasonable branding, logo, and trademark usage guidelines (“Branding Guidelines”).  Each Party will publish their Branding Guidelines to the other Party.  If a Party is deviating from the known Branding Guidelines of the other Party, it shall receive prior written permission.

- 5.3 **Logo & Trademark Usage**

Subject to this Agreement and any applicable branding guidelines, each Party grants the other Party a limited, revocable, non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use its name, logo, and trademarks solely in connection with authorized alliance activities.

OmniTrust’s Branding Guidelines describing allowed usages of its marks and logos is attached to this Agreement as Exhibit 1.

- 5.4 **Customer & Commercial Relationships**

This Agreement, including any attached Alliance Addenda, does not, and shall not be construed to, establish any reseller rights, referral rights, distribution rights, OEM rights, commission structures, or revenue-sharing arrangements.

The Party’s acknowledge that they are free to enter into such an agreement separately.  Each Party to this Agreement (1) independently markets and sells its own products and services; (2) independently contracts with customers; and (3) independently sets pricing and commercial terms.

10. **Term and Termination**

    - 10.1 **This Agreement and each Alliance Addenda shall become effective on the Effective Date and shall remain in effect for a period of Three (3) years, thereafter ("**Initial Term**"). This Initial Term shall be automatically extended for successive periods of one (1) year, unless otherwise terminated by either Party in accordance with this Section 10.**
    - 10.2 **This Agreement or any Alliance Addenda may be terminated by either Party by giving one (1) month prior written notice to the other Party. Nevertheless and to the extent applicable, Partner acknowledges and agrees to stop using OmniTrust's partner signet and logo immediately after said prior written notice is sent by Partner to OmniTrust or received by Partner from OmniTrust. The termination of this Agreement shall automatically terminate any Alliance Addenda. The right of extraordinary termination for cause shall remain unaffected.**
    - 10.3 **Upon termination of this Agreement or upon termination of any Alliance Addenda, OmniTrust shall stop providing and Partner shall stop exercising any Benefits unless otherwise agreed in writing, and Partner shall stop providing and OmniTrust shall stop requiring Partner’s Commitments unless otherwise agreed in writing.**
