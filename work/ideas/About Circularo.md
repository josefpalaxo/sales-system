# About Circularo

## Digital Trust Infrastructure for Organizations, Governments and the Agentic AI Era

Circularo is a digital trust technology company headquartered in Dubai, providing enterprise and government platforms for trusted digital processes.

Our technology brings together:

- Identity and authority
- Digital workflows and approvals
- Electronic signatures and organizational seals
- Trust services and verification
- Evidence and auditability
- Trusted records and archiving
- APIs and automation
- AI-enabled document and business processes

Circularo can be delivered as a managed SaaS platform, deployed within customer-controlled sovereign environments, or used as the foundation for government-wide shared trust services.

Rather than treating signing as an isolated action, Circularo provides the **trusted execution layer** connecting people, organizations, applications and increasingly AI agents.

## Our Focus

**Enterprise Digital Trust**

**Sovereign Digital Trust**  
Providing modern trust infrastructure within customer-controlled environments.

**Government Shared Services**  
Enabling reusable digital trust capabilities across ministries, agencies and public services.

**Agentic AI Trust**  
Providing the identity, authority, execution and evidence layer required when AI agents begin acting on behalf of people and organizations.
