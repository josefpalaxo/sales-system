# DECK 1 — Circularo SaaS

## Enterprise Digital Trust for Modern Organizations

### Primary Audience

- Enterprises
- Government entities consuming SaaS
- Regulated organizations
- Holding companies
- Mid-market organizations
- Digital transformation teams
- CIO / CTO / CISO
- Operations and business process owners

### Core Narrative

Circularo SaaS should not be positioned simply as an eSignature product.

The story is about replacing fragmented document, approval, signature and evidence processes with one enterprise platform for trusted digital work.

---

## 1. Unique Insight

### Slide 1 — Digital Work Has Changed

Organizations no longer simply need to sign documents electronically.

Business processes increasingly combine:

- collaboration
- approvals
- identity
- signatures
- automation
- trusted evidence
- AI

Yet most organizations still operate these capabilities across disconnected systems.

### Slide 2 — Trust Must Extend Beyond the Signature

A signature answers only part of the business question.

Organizations increasingly need to establish:

- Who acted?
- Were they authorized?
- What was approved?
- What process was followed?
- Has the record changed?
- Can the evidence be independently verified?
- Can the entire transaction be audited later?

### Slide 3 — AI Changes the Model Again

Human users will increasingly work alongside AI agents and automated systems.

Trust infrastructure must therefore support both:

**Human actions + Machine actions**

The future requirement is trusted execution, not simply electronic signing.
