# Circularo Core Narrative

## Trusted Execution for the Agentic Era

Digital transformation is moving from **Paper → Digital → Automated →
AI-Assisted → Agentic**.

> **As autonomy increases, the burden of trust increases.**

Greater autonomy requires **Identity · Authority · Policy · Approval ·
Evidence · Audit**.

## Intelligence Does Not Create Authority

AI can understand, analyze, recommend, generate and plan. Consequential
action requires institutional authority.

> **What is this person, application or AI agent authorized to do, under
> which policy, and how can the action be proven?**

## Fragmented Tools Fragment Accountability

**Create → Collaborate → Review → Approve → Sign → Preserve → Analyse →
Act**

Disconnected handoffs weaken context and evidence. Circularo maintains a
continuous trust and evidence chain.

## Trust Starts Before the Signature

**Draft → Co-author → Review → Version → Approve → Execute → Preserve**

The signature is an important trust event, not the entire trust model.

## Identity + Authority

**Who are you?** Identity.\
**Whom do you represent?** Organization + role.\
**What may you do?** Delegated authority.\
**What assurance is required?** Transaction policy.

## Trusted Execution Layer

**People · Organizations · Applications · AI Agents → Circularo →
Trusted Records**

Circularo connects **Identity · Authority · Policy · Collaboration ·
Approval · Sign & Seal · Trust Services · Evidence · Audit · Archive**.

## Institutional Evidence and Memory

A trusted execution creates more than a final file. It creates a
verifiable record.

**Documents → Trusted Records → Searchable Evidence → Institutional
Knowledge**

Traditional storage asks *Where is the file?* A trusted record also
answers who acted, what was approved, which version executed, what
authority applied and what evidence exists.

## Trusted AI

**KNOW** --- Extract · Summarize · Classify · Translate · Retrieve\
**ADVISE** --- Obligations · Risks · Anomalies · Recommendations\
**ACT** --- Initiate workflow · Prepare response · Request approval ·
Permitted API action

**Trusted Records → Trusted Context → Trusted Intelligence → Governed
Action**

## Governed Agentic Action

**Identity → Delegated Authority → Policy → Approval → Execution →
Evidence → Audit**

Circularo does not govern AI reasoning. Circularo governs and evidences
what is authorized to execute.

## Programmable Trust

People, applications, portals, enterprise systems and AI agents can
consume the same governed trust capabilities through the Circularo REST
API.

## System of Authority

A System of Record answers **What information do we have?**

A **System of Authority** helps answer: Who acted? Whom did they
represent? Were they authorized? What was approved? What executed? What
evidence exists? What may an AI agent execute?

> **Digital signatures establish trust in a signature. Circularo
> establishes trust in the entire execution.**
