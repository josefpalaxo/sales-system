# Circularo Company Deck

## Digital Trust Infrastructure for the Agentic Era

**Purpose:** Explain why Circularo exists, the category it belongs to,
and where the company is going.

1.  **Circularo --- Trusted Execution Infrastructure for the Agentic
    Era**\
    People · Organizations · Applications · AI Agents.

2.  **The Shift --- As autonomy increases, the burden of trust
    increases**\
    Paper → Digital → Automated → AI-Assisted → Agentic.\
    Identity · Authority · Policy · Approval · Evidence · Audit.

3.  **The Trust Gap --- Intelligence does not create institutional
    authority**\
    Contrast AI capability with authority required for consequential
    action.

4.  **The Accountability Problem --- Fragmented tools fragment
    accountability**\
    Create × Collaborate × Review × Approve × Sign × Store × Analyse.

5.  **Trust Starts Before the Signature**\
    Draft → Co-author → Review → Version → Approve → Execute → Preserve.

6.  **Circularo Provides the Trusted Execution Layer**\
    Actors → Identity · Authority · Policy · Collaboration · Approval ·
    Sign & Seal · Evidence · Archive → Trusted Records.

7.  **One Platform**\
    Cloud/Deployment · Identity & Authority · Collaboration · Sign &
    Seal · Trusted Vault · AI.\
    **One platform · One governance model · One continuous evidence
    chain · One API.**

8.  **From Execution to Institutional Memory**\
    Trusted Execution → Institutional Evidence → Trusted Records →
    Searchable Evidence → Institutional Knowledge.

9.  **From Institutional Memory to Trusted AI**\
    KNOW → ADVISE → ACT.

10. **Governed Agentic Action**\
    Identity → Delegated Authority → Policy → Approval → Execution →
    Evidence → Audit.

11. **Programmable Trust**\
    People · Applications · Portals · Enterprise Systems · AI Agents →
    Circularo REST API → Verifiable Execution.

12. **Three Delivery Models**\
    Circularo SaaS · Circularo Sovereign · Sovereign Trust Shared
    Services.

13. **Circularo at a Glance**\
    Use verified current footprint, deployments, regions,
    certifications, identity/trust integrations and approved scale
    indicators.

14. **Proven in Demanding Environments**\
    Use 3--5 strong government, regulated, sovereign and shared-services
    proof points.

15. **Strategic Outcome --- From System of Record to System of
    Authority**\
    Who acted? Whom did they represent? Were they authorized? What was
    approved? What executed? What evidence exists? What may an AI agent
    execute?
