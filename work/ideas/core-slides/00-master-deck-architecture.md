# Circularo Master Deck Architecture

## Positioning

-   **Category:** Digital Trust / Trusted Execution Infrastructure
-   **Platform:** Circularo Unified Trust Platform
-   **Role:** Trusted Execution Layer
-   **Strategic outcome:** System of Authority

## Four Master Decks

1.  Circularo Company --- why Circularo exists and the category we are
    building.
2.  Circularo SaaS --- enterprise digital trust without infrastructure
    complexity.
3.  Circularo Sovereign --- trusted execution inside a
    customer-controlled environment.
4.  Sovereign Trust Shared Services --- reusable sovereign trust
    infrastructure across government.

**Paper → Digital → Automated → AI-Assisted → Agentic**

**Identity · Authority · Policy · Approval · Evidence · Audit**

> **One platform · One governance model · One continuous evidence chain
> · One API**

## Guardrails

Do not position Circularo primarily as eSignature. Identity establishes
the actor; authority establishes what the actor may do. Signing is one
trust event, not the whole trust model. Archive preserves evidence and
provenance, not merely files. API means programmable trust. Circularo
governs and evidences permitted AI actions, not model reasoning.
Sovereignty is broader than residency. Shared Services is
infrastructure, not merely multi-tenancy.
