# SignFide company setup and operating model brief

Date: 2026-10-14  
Status: Draft for founder discussion  
Classification: Confidential  
Evidence: Starting principles supplied by Josef in this conversation; implementation and execution status to be confirmed. Internal working material, non-canonical.

SignFide LLC is intended to operate as a distinct US commercial business offering Signfide powered by Circularo under its own customer terms. The starting structure is a Delaware LLC owned 60% by JP Investment LLC and 40% by Reda’s team, with USD 10,000 in starting capital, a US bank account, and Stripe for payments.

Circularo provides the technology; REDA provides US sales capability, market reach, and execution of the commercial customer lifecycle through SignFide. SignFide is the dedicated jointly established company, brand, and US sales entity, rather than a separate third delivery team. The starting operating model is Option A, the Circularo-managed co-branded service.

The central operating dependency is an agreement between Circularo and SignFide LLC covering resale rights and special commercial terms, aligned with the cooperation agreement with REDA. The [proposed responsibilities and RACI appendix](cooperation-responsibilities-and-raci.md) defines the intended Circularo/REDA working split, deliverables, handoffs, and decisions still needed before contract attachment. The [OmniTrust model](info.md) supplies the operating-model reference; Options B and C remain future alternatives subject to separate agreement.

The [REDA contribution commitments and performance schedule](reda-contribution-and-performance.md) adds the resource commitments, measurable outputs, customer outcomes, cost attribution, and review process needed to substantiate the proposed 40% interest. Circularo’s contribution includes technology and the agreed license/service on special favorable supplier terms intended to support SignFide’s US profitability. Actual commercial terms and performance targets remain to be agreed.

## Circularo and SignFide commercial relationship

The intended structure has two distinct contractual relationships:

| Relationship | Intended purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Circularo → SignFide LLC | Authorize the resale and branding arrangement and establish special pricing, supply terms, and the operating obligations between the businesses |
| SignFide LLC → Customer | Offer Signfide powered by Circularo under SignFide’s own agreed customer terms |

SignFide’s customer terms and commitments need to be reconciled with the service scope and obligations agreed with Circularo. Permission to sell under SignFide’s terms does not yet define the underlying license, service commitments, or allocation of risk.

The Circularo agreement needs a discussion and drafting brief covering:

- **Rights and scope:** what SignFide may resell, permitted branding, territory, customer segments, and any exclusivity decision.
- **Commercial mechanics:** the special pricing basis, charging units, included services, payment timing, billing currency, price changes, and treatment of additional services. No numerical terms are agreed in this brief.
- **Delivery and support:** provisioning, configuration, onboarding, support levels, escalation, service commitments, and change management.
- **Data and trust:** hosting configuration, data responsibilities, access, evidence, security communication, and incident coordination.
- **Brand and intellectual property:** use of the Signfide and Circularo identities, product adaptation, and ownership or licensing of newly created materials.
- **Customer continuity and exit:** handling of existing customers, records, outstanding payments, and transition if the relationship changes or ends.

These are proposed agreement topics for the parties and their advisors. Special terms for SignFide remain a separate proposed arrangement; this brief does not amend Circularo’s approved commercial records or establish customer prices.
