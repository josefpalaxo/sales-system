# REDA contribution commitments and performance schedule

Date: 2026-10-14  
Status: Draft for founder discussion and later contract attachment  
Classification: Confidential  
Basis: Josef’s stated contribution principles and the [cooperation responsibilities and RACI](cooperation-responsibilities-and-raci.md). Internal working material, non-canonical.

## Contribution principle

The proposed business combines two contributions:

- **Circularo:** the technology, the agreed license and managed service, and special supplier terms and favorable pricing intended to support SignFide’s commercial success in the United States. The actual scope and commercial terms belong in the Circularo–SignFide agreement.
- **REDA:** an explicitly resourced and measurable US commercial operation that turns market access into qualified opportunities, paying customers, and a functioning customer lifecycle for SignFide.

The founders’ stated expectation is that REDA’s contribution must substantiate its proposed **33% interest in SignFide LLC**. That assessment needs documented commitments, delivery evidence, commercial results, and an agreed treatment of shortfalls. Market reach alone is too broad to measure without specifying the access, people, work, and outcomes being contributed.

This schedule defines the discussion and measurement framework. It neither establishes the financial value of either contribution nor changes the 67/33 ownership proposal. Targets, costs, review dates, and any ownership-related consequences require express agreement.

## Separate contribution from paid work

For each REDA commitment, record who supplies the resource and who bears its cost. The founders need to see which contribution REDA makes at its own cost, which expenses SignFide funds, and which activities attract additional compensation.

| Contribution category | Detail to agree | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Named commercial leadership | Lead, duties, time commitment, authority, start date, and cover during absence | Named assignment and agreed capacity |
| Sales execution | Named sales resources, time commitment, prospecting, qualification, demonstrations, and closing responsibilities | Staffing plan and documented execution |
| US market access | Target segments and the specific channels, relationships, or distribution access REDA will activate | Aggregated record of introductions, meetings, and opportunities |
| Marketing and content delivery | Resources for website, campaigns, sales materials, help content, and maintenance | Delivery plan and accepted outputs |
| Customer operations | Capacity for onboarding, training, first-line support, account management, and renewals | Named coverage, working processes, and customer records |
| Cash or other funded resources | Any REDA-funded cash, staff, campaigns, tools, or services, if proposed | Agreed amounts, dates, cost treatment, and proof of delivery |

A resource register should identify, for every item: **named owner, capacity, delivery period, payer, compensation or reimbursement, approval, and replacement arrangements**. Work paid for by SignFide remains part of REDA’s operational responsibility where assigned, but its cost treatment must be visible when discussing the contribution supporting the 33% interest. It should not be counted again as an unfunded REDA contribution without a specific founder agreement.

REDA’s staffing and spending commitments must be reconciled with SignFide’s starting capital and budget. Do not assume the initial USD 10,000 funds an otherwise unspecified commercial team or marketing program.

Circularo remains responsible for its Option A platform deliverables. REDA’s launch acceptance does not require it to recreate hosting, platform operations, or L2/L3 support. The launch record should distinguish REDA-owned readiness from Circularo-owned dependencies.

## Operating scorecard

Use two groups of measures: **resources and execution** show whether REDA is supplying the committed operation; **commercial outcomes** show whether that operation is producing business results. Report both, with targets and measurement periods agreed in advance.

### Resources and execution

| Measure | Definition and evidence | Target field |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Committed team capacity delivered | Actual assigned capacity against the named resource register; disclose vacancies, substitutions, and other material demands on the team | Capacity by role and period |
| Qualified introductions activated | Introductions that reach the agreed target segment and relevant buyer, with a held meeting or documented next step; deduplicate by prospect | Per agreed period |
| Buyer discovery and demonstrations completed | Held sessions with a relevant prospective customer, documented needs, and an agreed next action; exclude internal sessions and no-shows | Per agreed period |
| Commercial deliverables completed | Website/content, campaigns, sales materials, onboarding, and help-content milestones accepted against their agreed scope | Milestones and due dates |
| Customer operation delivered | Onboarding completion and first-line support performance against agreed response/coverage targets; disclose open cases and escalations | Service targets and reporting period |

### Commercial outcomes

| Measure | Definition and evidence | Target field |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualified opportunities created | Unique opportunities meeting the agreed qualification standard, with buyer need, relevant contact, offer fit, value basis, timing, owner, and next step | Count and value per period |
| New paying customer organizations | Unique customer organizations with an executed order, agreed service activated, and first payment received; report contracted-but-unpaid separately | Per period and cumulative |
| Net customer cash collected | Customer payments received, less refunds and payment reversals, excluding collected taxes; reconcile to finance records | Currency, period, and amount |
| Retained paying customers | Starting-period paying customer cohort remaining paying at period end; report new customers and cancellations separately | Cohort, period, and target |
| Commercial economics | Actual revenue and costs against the approved model, including Circularo charges, payment costs, acquisition, onboarding, support, and other agreed operating costs | Cost categories, calculation, and target |

Favorable Circularo pricing is intended to create an opportunity for a profitable business. The scorecard must still show the retail proposition, cost of acquisition, cost of service, and retained customer results; no profit level is assumed or guaranteed here.

### Counting and evidence rules

- Agree the target segment, qualification criteria, reporting period, and baseline before measuring performance. A contact list or an unaccepted introduction is not automatically a qualified opportunity.
- Use consistent definitions. Separate pipeline, signed orders, activated subscriptions, and collected cash rather than presenting them as equivalent results.
- Avoid duplicate credit for the same organization, opportunity, or payment. Attribute lead source and sales ownership where multiple teams participate.
- Keep records in SignFide’s authorized business systems with company access and usable handover. Store only aggregate performance summaries in this repository; do not add contact lists, raw CRM exports, or customer correspondence.
- Record spend and resource costs alongside outputs. Agree any financial attribution or contribution valuation separately rather than inventing a value for introductions or staff time after the event.
- Record relevant Circularo dependencies, approval delays, offer changes, or service issues separately, with the affected dates and impact. Any target adjustment requires documented agreement.

## Review and shortfall process

The proposed initial planning window is the first 90 days from an agreed start date, followed by agreed review periods. This is a discussion proposal; neither a start date nor targets have been set.

At each review, REDA should present committed versus actual resources, accepted deliverables, pipeline movement, paying customers, collections, customer-operation performance, spend, and blockers. Circularo should report the technology and service dependencies relevant to that period. SignFide’s authorized management records the conclusions and actions.

Treat these situations separately:

| Situation | Review question and proposed next step |
| --- | --- |
| Committed people, capacity, or deliverables were not supplied | Identify the missed commitment, explanation, replacement or corrective action, owner, and an agreed completion date |
| Agreed execution was delivered but sales outcomes were below target | Review offer fit, channels, sales cycle, economics, and external dependencies; agree changes and the next assessment period |
| A Circularo dependency prevented agreed commercial execution | Record the affected activity and period, the technical action owner, and any mutually agreed target/timing adjustment |
| Repeated material shortfalls or withdrawal of the committed team | Escalate to the founders and use the separately agreed company/cooperation provisions; protect customer continuity and access to company records |

The founders must decide before signing how materiality, notice, corrective periods, persistent underperformance, and withdrawal affect the cooperation and the contribution supporting REDA’s 33% interest. Any mechanism concerning how the interest is issued, earned, retained, or transferred must be deliberately agreed and reflected in the appropriate company and contract documents. This schedule creates no automatic forfeiture, dilution, transfer, or other ownership change.

## Founder decision sheet

The internal discussion should finish with explicit answers to these questions:

1. **Who is REDA committing?** Named lead, sales, marketing, and customer-operation resources, with capacity and start dates.
2. **What is REDA funding?** Its own contribution versus SignFide-funded expenses, salaries, fees, commissions, or reimbursement, if any.
3. **What access will become action?** Specific channels and a measurable initial introduction/opportunity plan.
4. **What will be delivered?** Accepted launch milestones and the customer-operation standard.
5. **What results are expected?** Initial targets for qualified pipeline, new paying customers, collections, retention, and economics, with defined periods.
6. **What supports the 33% interest?** The agreed contribution and performance expectations, and their relationship to the ownership arrangements.
7. **What happens if commitments are not delivered?** Review authority, corrective process, continuity obligations, and separately agreed company/contract consequences.

Until those fields are completed, the responsibilities can be discussed clearly, but the measurable contribution supporting the ownership proposal remains unfinished.
