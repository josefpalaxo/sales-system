# **Circularo × OmniTrust — U.S. Partnership Models**

[https\://omnitrust.com/partners/omnitrust-circularo/](https://omnitrust.com/partners/omnitrust-circularo/)

## **1\. Overview**

Circularo and OmniTrust are considering a partnership to introduce Circularo technology to the U.S. market as part of the OmniTrust portfolio.

The recommended starting point is a **simple co-branded commercial offering based on existing Circularo editions**, supported by a dedicated U.S. deployment.

Three partnership models are technically possible, offering progressively greater control and responsibility to OmniTrust:

1. **Option A — Circularo-Managed Co-Branded Service \[PREFERRED\]**  
2. **Option B — OmniTrust-Managed Co-Branded Service**  
3. **Option C — Complete White Label**

The key differences are the level of OmniTrust branding, ecosystem ownership, and operational responsibility.

## **2\. Common Principle Across All Options**

Across all three models, **Circularo is the technology provider**.

Depending on the selected model, Circularo may also provide hosting, platform operations, monitoring, upgrades and technical support.

OmniTrust is always responsible for building, promoting and maintaining the **OmniTrust market-facing proposition**.

This responsibility includes:

- Branding and visual identity  
- U.S. market positioning  
- Plan names and commercial packaging  
- Pricing and commercial proposition  
- Website and landing pages  
- Sales collateral and presentations  
- Marketing materials  
- Customer-facing help content and knowledge base  
- Training and onboarding materials  
- OmniTrust-specific product descriptions and documentation

Circularo can provide its existing materials and technical documentation as source material, but **Circularo does not undertake the co-branding, white-labeling, rewriting or ongoing maintenance of OmniTrust's customer-facing content**.

# **3\. Option A — Circularo-Managed Co-Branded Service**

## **Recommended Starting Model**

Option A provides the simplest and fastest route for OmniTrust to introduce the offering to the U.S. market.

OmniTrust would commercially introduce its own plans based on existing Circularo editions.

The service would be visibly presented as:

**OmniTrust — Powered by Circularo**

OmniTrust can determine the appropriate U.S. plan names, packaging, pricing and market positioning while leveraging existing Circularo product capabilities.

### **Dedicated U.S. Environment**

Circularo would deploy a **dedicated Microsoft Azure environment in the United States** for the OmniTrust offering.

Circularo would manage:

- Hosting and infrastructure  
- Platform deployment  
- Monitoring  
- Platform operations  
- Application upgrades  
- Security and maintenance updates  
- Release management  
- Platform availability and reliability  
- Technical support and incident resolution

This allows OmniTrust to concentrate on sales, marketing, customer acquisition and customer relationships without having to establish a separate technical operations capability.

## **Circularo Ecosystem**

Because this remains part of the Circularo-operated ecosystem, existing external applications and integrations remain published under the **Circularo brand**.

This includes, where applicable:

- Microsoft Marketplace integrations  
- Microsoft Office / Microsoft 365 integrations  
- Apple App Store applications  
- Google Play applications  
- Google Workspace integrations  
- Other existing Circularo connectors and extensions

OmniTrust customers should therefore expect to encounter Circularo branding when using these ecosystem components.

This is an intentional consequence of the co-branding model rather than a limitation of the service.

# **4\. Option B — OmniTrust-Managed Co-Branded Service**

Option B retains the co-branding relationship:

**OmniTrust — Powered by Circularo**

However, it gives OmniTrust substantially greater control over the surrounding product ecosystem.

OmniTrust could publish and maintain its own branded versions of relevant:

- Mobile applications  
- Microsoft Marketplace integrations  
- Microsoft Office / Microsoft 365 integrations  
- Google Workspace integrations  
- Other supported applications, extensions and connectors

## **Operational Consequences**

This additional control requires OmniTrust to assume substantially greater technical and operational responsibility.

OmniTrust would manage:

- Its hosting environment  
- Infrastructure operations  
- Platform deployment  
- Monitoring  
- Operational management  
- Release deployment  
- Marketplace publishing accounts  
- Apple and Google application publishing accounts  
- Marketplace certification and approval processes  
- Deployment and maintenance of OmniTrust-published integrations  
- Operational support for its environment

Circularo would continue to provide the underlying Circularo technology, software releases and agreed technical support.

The fundamental distinction is that **OmniTrust operates and distributes its own Circularo-powered environment**.

# **5\. Option C — Complete White Label**

Option C is fundamentally different from Options A and B.

Under this model, the technology is presented entirely as an **OmniTrust product**.

There would be **no visible “Powered by Circularo” branding** within the customer-facing proposition.

Because this model removes Circularo’s market visibility and grants OmniTrust substantially greater commercial independence, **Option C would require an upfront annual minimum consumption commitment to Circularo**. The applicable annual commitment and commercial terms would be agreed between the parties before implementation.

OmniTrust would assume full responsibility for establishing and operating the product under its own identity.

This includes:

- Complete product branding  
- Product positioning  
- Customer-facing product identity  
- Hosting and infrastructure  
- Platform operations  
- Monitoring  
- Release deployment  
- Application publishing  
- Marketplace publishing  
- Integration publishing  
- Operational maintenance  
- Customer-facing support structure  
- Marketing and sales enablement  
- Help content and documentation

Circularo would operate primarily as the underlying **technology and software provider**, providing software releases and agreed technical support.

# **7\. Recommended Starting Point**

Circularo recommends **Option A** as the initial model for entering the U.S. market.

It provides:

- Fastest time to market  
- Lowest implementation complexity  
- Dedicated U.S. infrastructure  
- Minimal operational burden for OmniTrust  
- Immediate access to the existing Circularo product and integration ecosystem  
- Clear co-branding through **Powered by Circularo**  
- Ability for OmniTrust to establish its own plans, pricing and U.S. market proposition

Most importantly, Option A allows both parties to **validate the U.S. commercial opportunity before investing in the significantly greater operational complexity required by Options B or C**.

If the business develops to a scale where greater OmniTrust control becomes commercially justified, the partnership can subsequently move toward Option B or, if strategically appropriate, Option C.

## **Guiding Principle**

**Circularo provides the technology. OmniTrust owns the OmniTrust market proposition.**

The selected partnership model determines how much of the technology operation and distribution OmniTrust chooses to assume.

* OmniTrust website integration

**Option B:** OmniTrust owns the customer, commercial and operational layers; Circularo provides the technology and technical support.

**Option C:** OmniTrust owns the entire customer-facing brand, commercial proposition and operations; Circularo remains the underlying technology provider.

# **Final Notes**

The **commercial and operational** complexity of the lifecycle **surrounding a SaaS product** can be challenging.

## **Key Implementation Consideration**

**OmniTrust should consider that operating a branded SaaS proposition involves significantly more than defining plans and changing the customer-facing branding.**

Depending on the selected model, additional capabilities and processes may be required, including:

* Integration with OmniTrust’s billing and financial systems  
* Subscription creation, upgrades, downgrades, renewals and cancellations  
* Trial provisioning, expiration and conversion  
* Integration between the OmniTrust website and Circularo  
* Customer registration and account provisioning  
* Customer onboarding  
* Payment and checkout processes  
* Pricing and plan synchronization  
* Entitlement and subscription status management  
* Customer notifications  
* First-line support and escalation processes  
* Reconciliation and reporting

Circularo already has **Stripe integration**, but using OmniTrust’s own billing environment or processes could require additional integration and operational design.

## **Why Option A Is Recommended**

The simplest model is for OmniTrust to operate commercially much like a **Circularo distributor**, while presenting its own co-branded OmniTrust plans.

In this model:

**OmniTrust**  
 → defines and promotes the co-branded plans  
 → generates demand and manages trials  
 → sells and manages customer relationships  
 → manages onboarding and first-line support

**Circularo**  
 → provides and operates the SaaS platform  
 → provisions the underlying subscriptions  
 → manages hosting, monitoring and upgrades  
 → provides L2/L3 technical support  
 → provides the existing platform and Stripe capabilities

This avoids OmniTrust having to recreate a substantial part of the **SaaS operational stack** before the U.S. business has been commercially validated.

### **Recommended Principle**

**Start by treating OmniTrust as a commercially independent Circularo distributor with a co-branded U.S. proposition \- not as the operator of a separate SaaS platform.**

Option B or C can remain available if the scale of the U.S. business later justifies the additional integration, operational ownership and investment.
