# Circularo–SignFide distribution and services agreement principles

## Purpose and parties

Use one principal **Software Distribution & Services Agreement** between the exact Circularo contracting entity and SignFide LLC. Confirm registered names and jurisdictions before legal drafting. Circularo supplies the existing technology and agreed managed service; SignFide is the authorized US distributor and customer-facing commercial entity.

This is the commercial brief for the agreement, not an executed contract. The starting service is **Option A: Circularo-managed, co-branded**. [Options B and C](operating-model-options.md) remain future alternatives requiring separate written scope and commercial agreement.

## Commercial framework

| Area | Intended arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| SaaS distribution rights | Authorize SignFide to market/resell agreed subscriptions and arrange customer access; specify approved distributor/reseller appointments and onward rights |
| Territory | United States; exclusivity only by separate express agreement |
| Products | Circularo Start, Pro, Business and Enterprise, plus specifically included add-ons; map SignFide packaging to underlying licensing/provisioning |
| SignFide buy price | Agreed confidential fixed wholesale unit prices; independent of SignFide customer prices, discounts or actual selling proceeds |
| Retail and channel pricing | SignFide sets its own selling prices, discounts and bundles through its governance; separately agree SignFide-to-partner terms |
| Platform operations | Circularo provides agreed Option A hosting, infrastructure, maintenance and releases |
| Support | SignFide provides L1 through its operating arrangements; Circularo supplies agreed L2/L3 |
| Direct customer route | SignFide contracts, invoices and collects from direct customers |
| Approved channel route | Specify which approved partner sells/invoices/collects and the underlying customer service relationship; use the [channel model](corporate-structure-and-commercial-model.md) |
| Software IP | Circularo retains its underlying software/technology IP and core development control |
| Brand rights | Define Circularo trademark/co-brand use separately from ownership or licensing of the SignFide brand |
| Customer data | Define actual data roles, processing terms, access, security and subprocessors |
| Term and exit | Agree duration, renewal, termination, existing subscriptions, customer records/access and transition arrangements |

Distribution/access rights do not by themselves authorize copying the software, independent white labeling, unrestricted sublicensing, global brand rights or unapproved partner appointments.

## Buy-price economics

**All sales economics start from SignFide's agreed buy price.** Circularo receives that agreed wholesale amount for the relevant billable subscriptions/usage under the supplier payment terms, independently of SignFide's retail discounts, bundles or actual selling price.

This replaces the earlier proposed retail/reference-price discount model. The owning [wholesale pricing model](../commercial/wholesale-pricing.md) defines the three economic layers, candidate cost/service/IP methodology, direct/channel calculations and the illustrative scenario. Actual prices and rates remain open.

SignFide's margin is the result of its selling proceeds less its buy price and relevant costs; the agreement does not guarantee a fixed resale margin or create a supplier revenue share. Customer non-payment, refunds, credits, trials and billing start/stop rules need explicit treatment rather than being inferred from “active paid subscriptions.”

Prefer one consolidated wholesale price including the agreed standard hosting and L2/L3 service scope. State exclusions and separately priced work explicitly: for example, custom integrations, exceptional service scope or shared operational services where not included. Avoid charging the same delivery cost both within the buy price and again as an additional fee.

## Pricing protection and review

The wholesale schedule should specify units, editions/add-ons, currency, effective period, billing/payment terms and agreed exception rates. Fixed unit prices remain fixed for that agreed period; they do not mean a flat total invoice regardless of volume or prices fixed forever.

Use the review approach in the wholesale model when drafting: a proposed annual review with transparent inputs, agreed notice/effective dates and explicit treatment of existing customer commitments. Review is not permission for arbitrary unilateral changes or retroactive repricing. Protect both Circularo's remuneration and SignFide's ability to honor its customer contracts.

## Ownership, governance and tax review

The working ownership proposal is **JP Investment LLC 67% and REDA's designated holders 33%**. Circularo and JP Investment are distinct entities. This proposal does not establish Circularo's ownership/control, the exact supplier jurisdiction or the tax characterization of the supply.

Have the appropriate cross-border adviser assess actual ownership/control, the transactions and any related-party pricing, software/service characterization, withholding and documentation requirements. The [wholesale model](../commercial/wholesale-pricing.md#commercial-and-tax-validation) contains the limited primary-source references and the proposed validation approach. Commercial approval of a buy-price principle does not approve a tax method or markup.

## Term, performance and customer continuity

Leave the initial term open until the parties agree commercially workable renewal, non-renewal and termination paths. Do not adopt a fixed initial term without addressing Circularo's concern about indefinite commitment and SignFide's customer/investment protection.

Define payment/breach remedies and any agreed performance conditions, who may exercise them, notice/correction arrangements, and transition responsibilities. Deal with active subscriptions, renewal authority, records/export, outstanding payments and transition costs. Supplier termination, REDA's operating appointment, JP Investment's equity exit and any brand license are separate relationships; any intended links must be explicit.

## Agreement and schedules

| Document | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| Principal agreement | Parties, US distribution/partner rights, IP, governance of changes, duration/exit and standard legal provisions to draft |
| Schedule A — Products and Wholesale Buy Prices | Edition/usage mapping, approved buy-price list, inclusions, charging units, billing/payment, review and agreed deal exceptions |
| Schedule B — Service Levels and Responsibilities | Option A hosting/operations, support/escalation, service scope and the relevant cooperation RACI |
| Schedule C — Data Processing and Security | Actual data responsibilities, access, customer data, security, subprocessors and continuity requirements |

The [commercial wholesale document](../commercial/wholesale-pricing.md) owns the working methodology; the executed Schedule A will govern agreed prices and obligations. Resolve differences expressly instead of maintaining competing price rules.

SignFide remains responsible for its customer-facing obligations. REDA's staffing, measurable contribution and equity-related arrangements belong in the separate [cooperation RACI](cooperation-responsibilities-and-raci.md), [performance schedule](reda-contribution-and-performance.md) and company documents. This two-party supply agreement cannot be assumed to bind REDA directly.

Global SignFide brand ownership remains open; a US license may require the actual selected brand owner as a separate party. Shared Odoo/Zoho Desk and business-data ownership follow [Operations](../operations/README.md), without changing the legal parties.
