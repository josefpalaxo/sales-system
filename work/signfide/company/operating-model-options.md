# SignFide operating model options and responsibilities

Date: 2026-10-14  
Status: Draft for founder discussion; not executed  
Classification: Confidential  
Basis: Josef’s direction to retain all three partnership options, start with Option A, and establish SignFide jointly with REDA. The original OmniTrust model has been adapted to the SignFide company and team structure. Internal working material, non-canonical.

**Start with Option A: Circularo operates the technology and REDA operates the US commercial business for SignFide.** Options B and C remain future alternatives requiring separate agreement, resources, and operational acceptance. They do not become available automatically when SignFide reaches a particular size.

## Company roles across all options

SignFide LLC is the proposed Delaware legal and US sales entity, with JP Investment LLC holding 67% and REDA’s designated holders holding 33%. Circularo supplies the underlying technology and the agreed license/service on special favorable commercial terms intended to support SignFide’s profitability. JP Investment and Circularo remain distinct entities.

REDA supplies US sales capability, market reach, and execution of the complete commercial customer lifecycle on behalf of SignFide. SignFide sells directly to end customers and through approved US distributors/resellers. The [corporate structure and commercial model](corporate-structure-and-commercial-model.md) describes the entities, contracts, channel routes, invoicing, and collection flows.

SignFide is not a third delivery team. Under B or C, obligations assigned to SignFide require named, funded personnel or approved providers. REDA’s commercial team does not acquire infrastructure expertise merely through the selection of an option. Technical operations must be explicitly assigned before a transition.

Operational responsibility for the Signfide proposition does not settle trademark title, global rights, territorial exclusivity, or ownership of new IP. Those decisions remain in the [open questions registry](../OPEN-QUESTIONS.md). Selecting B or C does not change the proposed equity split or grant rights outside the agreed US territory.

## Option A Circularo managed co-branded service

SignFide offers US plans based on existing Circularo editions and an agreed co-branded configuration. REDA develops plan names, packaging, proposed pricing, and positioning within SignFide’s approval authority and Circularo’s confirmed product scope.

The baseline is a dedicated Microsoft Azure environment in the United States, operated by Circularo. Circularo manages hosting and infrastructure, platform deployment, monitoring, availability and reliability operations, application upgrades, security and maintenance updates, release management, underlying subscription/tenant provisioning, and agreed technical incident resolution and L2/L3 support. Configuration, availability commitments, support hours, charges, and readiness remain to be recorded in service schedules.

Applicable Microsoft Marketplace, Office/Microsoft 365, Apple App Store, Google Play, Google Workspace, and other existing connectors/extensions remain published under the Circularo brand. These are examples of ecosystem categories to confirm for the launch scope, not a promise that every listed component is included. Customers may encounter Circularo branding when using them. Option A does not include separately published SignFide apps or a SignFide integration ecosystem.

REDA operates customer acquisition, channel management, the website and registration journey, trial follow-up where offered, sales conversion, onboarding, training, L1 support, account management, billing communication, and renewals. Circularo supplies technical inputs; REDA creates and maintains SignFide collateral, help content, and customer-facing documentation.

The [cooperation responsibilities and RACI](cooperation-responsibilities-and-raci.md) provides the detailed Option A team assignments and handoffs. This model allows the founders to test the US commercial opportunity while Circularo runs the platform; it does not remove the work needed to operate SignFide’s business.

## Option B SignFide managed co-branded service

The service retains **Signfide — Powered by Circularo**. SignFide operates and distributes its own Circularo-powered environment rather than relying on Circularo to run it.

SignFide must fund and assign a qualified operations team/provider to manage:

- Hosting, infrastructure, platform deployment, monitoring, operational incidents, and environment support.
- Deployment of Circularo software releases, security updates, and maintenance changes into its environment.
- Its publishing accounts for relevant mobile applications, marketplaces, and supported integrations.
- App/marketplace submissions, certification and approval processes, distribution, and ongoing maintenance of the components it publishes.

Subject to the agreed rights and supported technical scope, SignFide may publish branded versions of relevant mobile apps, Microsoft Marketplace and Office/Microsoft 365 integrations, Google Workspace integrations, and other connectors/extensions. This is not an entitlement to every component, source code, or third-party approval. The component inventory and work needed for each publication must be agreed.

Circularo continues to supply the underlying software, core releases, technical source material, and agreed product support / L2-L3. The contract must distinguish product defects from SignFide infrastructure, deployment, integration, and publishing incidents. Consultation by Circularo does not transfer those operating responsibilities back to it.

REDA’s commercial and customer obligations continue. The additional operations capability requires its own staffing, budget, access controls, service arrangements, and replacement coverage. SignFide’s authorized management must identify who provides it and how REDA coordinates with that team.

## Option C Complete white label

The agreed customer-facing product is presented under Signfide’s identity without a visible **Powered by Circularo** endorsement. Circularo remains the underlying software technology provider and supplies core releases and agreed technical support. White labeling does not transfer Circularo’s technology ownership.

SignFide assumes the platform and publishing operations described for Option B and the full visible product identity: product naming and positioning, visual branding, website/product presentation, applications, marketplaces, integrations, customer support structure, sales enablement, help content, and documentation. REDA continues to execute the commercial/customer remit, with the qualified technical operations team/provider expressly assigned for the additional work.

**The model requires an upfront annual minimum consumption commitment to Circularo.** Before implementation, the parties must agree its amount, measurement basis, period, payment timing, treatment of usage and any shortfall, renewal, and termination consequences. No numerical commitment or detailed payment mechanism is agreed here. The minimum reflects the model’s removal of Circularo’s market visibility and the additional commercial independence; the final commercial schedule must state the actual bargain.

The white-label scope must identify which interfaces, apps, integrations, notices, and third-party surfaces can be branded and who funds and maintains the changes. It does not by itself settle trademark ownership, disclosure requirements, global brand rights, or third-party publishing permissions.

## Responsibilities that continue under every option

REDA is responsible for producing and maintaining SignFide’s US proposition: brand implementation, positioning, plans and proposed prices, website, landing pages, presentations, sales collateral, campaigns, help center, knowledge base, training, onboarding, and SignFide-specific product documentation. Circularo provides available source materials and technical input; it does not automatically undertake rewriting, co-branding, white labeling, or ongoing maintenance of that content.

REDA also operates the customer lifecycle: lead response, registration, trials where offered, conversion, onboarding, training, L1 support, customer communication, account management, billing communication, subscription changes, renewal, cancellation, and offboarding. Approved channel delegation must have an explicit customer coverage and escalation path. The [REDA contribution and performance schedule](reda-contribution-and-performance.md) defines the framework for measuring its contribution separately from work paid for by SignFide.

Circularo provides the confirmed technology scope, software releases, agreed technical support, and technical input for accurate product/security statements. Additional development, custom integrations, implementation services, or content production require separately agreed scope, cost, timing, and ownership.

## Customer journey and payment integration

Josef's operations direction reuses Circularo's existing Odoo, Stripe and product-led capabilities. Start SignFide with sales-assisted Odoo quotations/invoices and Stripe or bank payment; configure the SignFide company/payment arrangements and coordinate underlying Circularo provisioning. Self-service checkout and automated activation are later extensions. REDA retains commercial execution with authorized finance resources. The [operations strategy](../operations/README.md#business-data-ownership) owns system-of-record boundaries and sequencing: financial subscriptions/billing belong in Odoo, service entitlements/usage in Circularo, and payment transactions in Stripe/banking.

The launch design must address each of the following, even under Option A:

| Process | Required agreement or test |
| --- | --- |
| Website and customer registration | Approved data capture, consent/notices, account creation, and platform handoff |
| Trials where offered | Eligibility, provisioning, expiry, follow-up, conversion, and customer notifications |
| Orders, plans, and entitlements | Price/plan mapping, authorized provisioning, entitlement status, and exception handling |
| Subscription lifecycle | Creation, upgrades, downgrades, renewals, cancellations, and synchronized commercial/platform status |
| Payments and financial systems | Checkout, invoicing, collection, refunds/disputes, reconciliation, and any additional billing-system integration |
| Customer service | Onboarding, training, L1 triage, technical escalation, and customer communications |
| Reporting and exit | Reconciled customer/service records, offboarding instructions, and agreed technical actions |

Direct and channel sales can have different sellers, invoicers, and collectors. Partner retail receipts do not automatically pass through SignFide’s Stripe account. Use the [commercial model](corporate-structure-and-commercial-model.md) to define the applicable route. No trial, self-service signup, automatic provisioning, or billing automation is committed until its scope and flow have been agreed and tested.

## Responsibility changes for B and C

The table below identifies proposed accountability boundaries for a future revised RACI. It does not appoint an unnamed team or replace the detailed Option A appendix.

**A/R** means accountable for delivery and responsible for execution; **C** means consulted; **I** means informed. Each row has one accountable owner. “SignFide operations” means a named qualified team/provider that SignFide must appoint and fund before activation.

| Activity under B or C | Circularo team | REDA commercial team | SignFide operations |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Underlying Circularo technology and core software releases | A/R | I | C |
| Agreed product technical support / L2-L3 | A/R | C | C |
| Hosting, infrastructure, deployment, monitoring, and environment incidents | C | I | A/R |
| Deployment of releases, security updates, and maintenance changes | C | I | A/R |
| Underlying tenant/service provisioning in SignFide’s environment | C | C | A/R |
| SignFide app, marketplace, and integration publishing and maintenance | C | C | A/R |
| Publishing accounts and submission/certification processes | C | I | A/R |
| US offer, brand implementation, website/content, sales, and channels | C | A/R | C |
| Registration/trial journey, sales conversion, and service-change instructions | C | A/R | C |
| Onboarding, training, L1 support, customer/incident communication, and renewals | C | A/R | C |
| Checkout, billing communication, reconciliation, and commercial reporting | C | A/R | C |
| Core platform payment capability supplied by Circularo, where agreed | A/R | C | C |
| Deployment/configuration of the agreed capability in SignFide’s environment | C | C | A/R |
| Full visible product identity and branded content under C | C | A/R | C |
| Technical deployment and maintenance of agreed white-label changes under C | C | C | A/R |

If REDA provides the operations team, name that team and its commitments explicitly in REDA’s scope. If a provider supplies it, identify the contract owner, service obligations, coordination with REDA, and fallback arrangements. RACI accountability does not confer company signing authority, IP ownership, or equity rights.

## Conditions for changing the model

Before moving from A to B or C, obtain a documented decision from the relevant SignFide and Circularo authorities covering:

1. Commercial rationale, selected scope, rights, and component inventory; for C, the annual minimum consumption commitment.
2. Named operations resources, funding, capacity, access, incident coverage, and publishing-account administration.
3. Revised supplier/service schedules and RACI, with boundaries for platform defects, environment failures, customer support, and charges for additional work.
4. Deployment, maintenance, data/security responsibilities, monitoring, service reporting, and customer communication arrangements.
5. Migration and acceptance plan covering accounts, subscriptions, entitlements, billing, integrations, records/data, cutover, rollback, and customer continuity.
6. Brand permissions, content maintenance, marketplace/app approvals, customer contract changes, and exit arrangements.

There is no automatic migration date, volume threshold, price, exclusivity, or white-label right in this draft. Continue with A until the parties authorize and accept the revised arrangement.
