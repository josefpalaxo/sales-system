# JP Investment setup advice

Date: 2026-10-14  
Status: Draft for founder discussion and professional review  
Classification: Confidential  

## Recommended starting point

Obtain a complete quote for a **DIFC Prescribed Company / special purpose vehicle (SPV)** in Dubai. Compare it with **RAK ICC** if incorporation elsewhere in the UAE is acceptable. Select the structure using the total cost over three years, banking suitability, and the combined UAE and US tax outcome.

DIFC is the preferred first option to investigate because its SPV regime is designed for passive investment holding. Its published licence fee is relatively low, but the generally required corporate service provider can materially increase the total cost. No option can yet be called the cheapest overall without comparable provider quotes.

## Proposed purpose and ownership

JP Investment would be owned **50/50 by two co-founders** and would hold investments, including the intended interest in SignFide LLC. It would have no direct trading, sales, customer service, or other commercial operations. The initial comparison assumes no employees or residency visas are needed; the founders must confirm this.

Keep the proposed investment holding role separate from SignFide's US commercial operations and Circularo's technology supply. Any later management services, IP licensing, or other activity requires a fresh review of permitted activities and tax treatment. The final registered name and legal-form suffix remain subject to the selected jurisdiction and name approval; do not assume that the existing working name “JP Investment LLC” will be the registered name.

## Tax issues to resolve before formation

**Passive ownership does not automatically exempt a UAE company from corporate tax or compliance.** Free-zone companies are within the UAE corporate-tax framework. Budget for registration, accounting, and applicable filings; have an adviser confirm any exemption or preferential treatment for the actual investment income. The treatment of an individual's private investments should not be assumed to apply to a company. [UAE Ministry of Finance](https://mof.gov.ae/en/public-finance/tax/corporate-tax/)

**SignFide's US tax classification must be assessed alongside JP Investment's structure.** A US LLC with two or more members defaults to partnership treatment for federal income tax unless it elects corporate treatment. If SignFide is taxed as a partnership and earns effectively connected US business income, withholding can apply to the income allocated to its foreign partners even without a cash distribution. The UAE holding company's passive role does not remove that issue. [IRS LLC classification](https://www.irs.gov/faqs/small-business-self-employed-other-business/entities/entities-3), [IRS foreign-partner withholding guidance](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/helpful-hints-for-partnerships-with-foreign-partners)

Commission a focused US/UAE tax comparison of SignFide's partnership and corporate tax treatments, JP Investment's treatment, distributions, exit proceeds, and the founders' own tax residences before finalizing either entity. The formation decision should follow that comparison rather than a headline licence price.

## Quote and banking requirements

Request the same scope from each shortlisted provider:

- Two individual shareholders with equal 50/50 interests, holding an investment in a Delaware LLC.
- No employees or visas initially, subject to founder confirmation.
- Itemized year-one setup cost, year-two and year-three renewal costs, and total three-year cost.
- Authority fees, registered address, corporate service provider or agent, shareholder and beneficial-owner filings, bookkeeping, corporate-tax registration and returns, and audit where required.
- Any additional charges for bank applications, certifications, document legalization, changes to shareholders or directors, and eventual closure; identify applicable VAT and exclusions.
- Written confirmation that the proposed foreign shareholding activity is permitted and that the bank being approached accepts this ownership, activity, and residency profile.

Treat bank opening as a separate decision. RAK ICC expressly distinguishes incorporation from the bank application process. Provider assistance is not bank approval. [RAK ICC FAQ](https://www.rakicc.com/faqs/rak-icc-ibcs/)

## Founder governance

Prepare a founders' agreement and compatible constitutional documents covering the 50/50 split, director and signing authority, capital contributions, future funding, distributions, reserved decisions, deadlock, transfers, exit, incapacity, and death. Equal ownership needs an agreed mechanism for resolving disagreement.

## Questions and next actions

1. Confirm each founder's tax residence and any US citizenship, green-card, or other US tax status for the adviser.
2. Confirm whether registration specifically in Dubai is required or whether Ras Al Khaimah is acceptable.
3. Confirm whether JP Investment needs a UAE bank account, residency visas, or investments beyond SignFide.
4. Obtain the US/UAE tax comparison and comparable DIFC, RAK ICC, and, if useful, JAFZA quotes.
5. Select the jurisdiction, legal form, permitted activity, bank approach, and governance arrangements before instructing incorporation.
