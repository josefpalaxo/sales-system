# SignFide cooperation responsibilities and RACI

Date: 2026-10-14  
Status: Proposed appendix for discussion with REDA; not executed  
Classification: Confidential  

## Purpose and operating principle

Circularo contributes the technology and, under the starting model, operates the platform. REDA contributes US sales capability, market access, and the team that develops and operates the commercial business. The parties are establishing a dedicated company and brand together: **SignFide LLC is the US legal, brand, and sales entity through which the offering reaches customers.**

The initial service is **Signfide — Powered by Circularo**, using **Option A: Circularo-managed co-branded service**. SignFide’s joint company structure does not shift responsibility for US commercialization onto Circularo or reduce REDA’s contribution to introductions alone.

This appendix proposes the working obligations, deliverables, and handoffs between the Circularo and REDA teams. SignFide is the entity on whose behalf the commercial work is performed, not a third operating team to which unresolved work can be assigned.

## Parties and authority

For this draft, “Circularo” means the technology provider and its designated team; “REDA” means the legal counterparty representing Reda’s team and the personnel it assigns. Exact legal names, representatives, and the agreement to which this appendix will attach must be completed before adoption.

SignFide LLC will contract with customers under its own approved terms and obtain the agreed resale and service rights through a separate agreement with Circularo. The final agreement structure must connect REDA’s delivery obligations, Circularo’s supply obligations, and SignFide’s customer commitments. The parties must decide how SignFide joins, adopts, or receives the benefit of the relevant obligations; this draft does not assume an unsigned entity is bound.

The RACI assigns **operational accountability**, not equity, intellectual property ownership, budget authority, or authority to sign contracts. REDA performs commercial activities on behalf of SignFide within the authority and budgets approved by SignFide’s authorized management. Company approvals remain with the people empowered under its governance documents. JP Investment LLC’s ownership interest must not be treated as making it interchangeable with Circularo.

## Initial service model

The parties will start from existing Circularo editions and an agreed co-branded configuration. The Option A baseline is a dedicated Microsoft Azure environment in the United States, operated by Circularo. The actual configuration, edition scope, launch readiness, charges, and service commitments must be recorded in the Circularo–SignFide service and commercial schedules before launch.

Existing Circularo mobile applications, marketplace applications, and integrations remain part of the Circularo-branded ecosystem where applicable. REDA will explain this to customers and reflect it accurately in SignFide materials. Option A does not include a separately published SignFide app or integration ecosystem.

The source model identifies an existing Circularo Stripe capability. Circularo will validate and enable the agreed platform-side capability for the chosen configuration. REDA will define and operate SignFide’s checkout, merchant-account administration, payment communication, and commercial subscription journey. The platform integration does not itself create that journey or establish the status of SignFide’s Stripe account.

No trial, self-service signup, automation, integration, or commercial allowance is promised merely because it appears in a workflow below. The launch scope and end-to-end flow must be confirmed and tested first.

## Circularo obligations under Option A

Circularo’s commercial contribution also includes the agreed technology license and special favorable supplier pricing and terms for SignFide. Their purpose is to support a competitive and profitable US proposition; the actual terms require a commercial schedule, and profitability depends on the business’s complete economics.

Circularo will:

1. **Provide the agreed technology.** Identify the editions, capabilities, configuration, and technical interfaces available for the SignFide offer, and identify dependencies or exclusions before REDA markets them.
2. **Deploy and operate the platform.** Deliver the agreed US environment and manage its infrastructure, application deployment, monitoring, platform operations, software releases, and security and maintenance updates.
3. **Provision the underlying service.** Process complete, authorized requests for the agreed subscriptions, tenants, and technical configuration. Provide the information REDA needs to communicate status to the customer.
4. **Provide L2/L3 technical support.** Receive qualified escalations from REDA, investigate platform problems, coordinate technical incident response, and provide findings and progress updates through the agreed support channel.
5. **Supply technical source material.** Provide available product documentation, configuration guidance, release information, and approved technical or security statements needed for REDA’s customer-facing work.
6. **Validate technology commitments.** Review requested capability, integration, hosting, security, and service-level representations before REDA includes new or nonstandard commitments in customer materials or contracts.

Circularo’s contribution does not include creating or maintaining SignFide’s website, campaigns, sales collateral, branded help center, customer training materials, sales funnel, or first-line customer operation. REDA may adapt Circularo source materials, but remains responsible for producing and maintaining the SignFide versions. Additional development, custom integrations, content production, or professional services require separately agreed scope, ownership, cost, and timing.

## REDA obligations for the SignFide business

REDA will:

1. **Build and execute the US go-to-market plan.** Assign the commercial team, define target segments and channels, develop the pipeline, and turn US market access into qualified opportunities and customer relationships.
2. **Develop the SignFide market proposition.** Produce and maintain positioning, visual identity implementation, plan names, packaging, proposed retail pricing, website, landing pages, presentations, and marketing materials within approved company decisions and the available service scope.
3. **Operate the sales and trial journey.** Own lead response, qualification, demonstrations, proposals, trial registration and follow-up where offered, conversion, and the commercial handoff into provisioning. Coordinate required website integration rather than assuming Circularo will build it.
4. **Own customer onboarding and enablement.** Prepare the onboarding process, customer help content, knowledge base, training materials, and customer-facing explanations. Run onboarding and training, using Circularo technical input where needed.
5. **Provide first-line customer support.** Maintain a reachable customer contact channel, handle usage and account questions, collect the information needed for technical escalation, and remain the customer’s communication owner while Circularo resolves platform issues.
6. **Manage the customer relationship.** Own account management, adoption follow-up, renewal, cancellation, complaints, commercial communication, and the customer-facing subscription lifecycle.
7. **Operate commercial payments and administration.** Administer SignFide’s approved checkout and Stripe setup, coordinate invoicing, subscription changes, refunds, payment disputes, reconciliation, and payment follow-up, with SignFide’s authorized finance and accounting resources.
8. **Maintain accurate commitments and records.** Use approved capability and assurance statements; maintain the opportunity, customer, subscription, and support records needed for delivery and reporting. Obtain approval for exceptions instead of committing Circularo to unagreed work or service levels.

REDA will resource these activities with its own assigned staff or approved service providers. Any request for Circularo to perform a REDA-owned task requires an express agreement; technical consultation does not transfer delivery ownership. Staffing levels, performance targets, budgets, and time commitments remain to be agreed in the launch plan.

The [REDA contribution and performance schedule](reda-contribution-and-performance.md) defines how to make this contribution measurable and assess it against the founders’ expectation supporting REDA’s proposed 40% interest. It separates REDA-funded contributions from SignFide-funded work and provides target definitions without inventing agreed quotas or ownership consequences.

“Own” in these obligations means responsibility for execution and upkeep. It does not assign personal ownership of the SignFide brand, customer relationships, materials, or accounts to REDA; ownership and access rights belong in the relevant company and commercial agreements.

## RACI for Option A

**R** performs the work. **A** is the operational owner who ensures the outcome is delivered. **C** supplies input before the relevant decision or handoff. **I** receives the resulting information. **A/R** combines ownership and execution. Each activity has one A; company approvals and authorized signatures still apply.

## Working handoffs and expectations

| Handoff | REDA responsibility | Circularo responsibility | Completion evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offer to launch | Supply the commercial proposition, customer journey, content, and operating contacts | Confirm scope, platform configuration, technical readiness, and approved claims | Joint launch checklist with an owner for each open item; SignFide launch authorization |
| Sale or trial to provisioning | Submit the complete approved request with the customer, plan, scope, and authorized commercial status | Provision the agreed underlying service or identify missing information/dependencies | Recorded provisioning result and REDA customer confirmation |
| First-line case to L2/L3 | Record impact, reproduction details, relevant diagnostics, and a customer contact using agreed secure channels | Triage and investigate the technical issue; return progress and next actions | Shared case reference, technical owner, and REDA communication owner |
| Release or maintenance to customers | Update help/training content and send the necessary customer communication | Supply release scope, expected impact, and agreed advance notice | Release communication and any required customer-facing updates |
| Payment/subscription event to service change | Resolve the commercial event and send an authorized service-change instruction | Apply the agreed platform change or identify a technical dependency | Reconciled commercial and platform status; customer confirmation |
| Exit or data request to technical action | Confirm the authorized instruction and coordinate the customer process | Execute only the agreed technical action within the contracted scope | Recorded instruction, execution result, and communication |

The parties will nominate a commercial lead and a technical/service lead, with deputies and escalation contacts. Launch meetings, operating reviews, reporting cadence, support hours, response targets, maintenance notice, and incident-update frequency must be recorded in the launch and service schedules. No numerical SLA, sales quota, or staffing promise is created here.

Unassigned work must be raised to the named cooperation leads and assigned explicitly. A task does not pass to Circularo because it involves software, or to REDA because a customer first reported it. New scope requires a written decision identifying deliverables, cost, timing, and ownership before either team promises it to customers.

## Company decisions and approval boundaries

The following sit outside the two-team operational RACI and must have named authorized decision-makers:

- **SignFide company governance:** ownership arrangements, managers or other authorized representatives, funding, budgets, banking controls, and signing authority under the company’s agreed structure.
- **SignFide commercial approval:** the final brand, retail offer, customer terms, spending, and exceptions through SignFide’s designated authority; REDA prepares and executes within that remit.
- **Circularo supply approval:** upstream special pricing, product scope, technical commitments, service levels, and any changes to Circularo’s obligations through Circularo’s designated authority.
- **Cross-party changes:** a change affecting both the SignFide customer promise and Circularo’s delivery obligation requires the relevant authorization on both sides. Neither team may create the other’s obligations by making a unilateral customer promise.

Before adoption, counsel should reconcile the proposed approval and delegation arrangements with SignFide’s LLC agreement and the actual contract parties. Delaware’s management and authority rules expressly depend on the LLC agreement; see [Delaware Code, sections 18-402 and 18-407](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title6/c018/sc04/index.html). This appendix is not a substitute for those company arrangements.

The Option C minimum-commitment concept and other model conditions remain in the [source model, Option C](info.md); they require their own negotiated terms before implementation. Joint establishment of SignFide does not by itself activate white-label rights or move the operating model to B or C.
