# SignFide launch roadmap

All windows below are proposed. **Day 0 is an authorized kickoff, not the document date.** No kickoff or gate acceptance is recorded. Proposed functions must become named owners in TASKS. These windows do not approve budgets, quotas or contractual deadlines.

The immediate operational priority is configuring SignFide LLC in the existing Odoo environment and enabling sales-assisted Quote-to-Cash (SF-T-015). Begin this setup alongside foundation work rather than waiting for the later build window. [Operations](operations/README.md) owns the functional sequence: assisted selling first, self-service later, then freemium adoption when available.

| Phase | Proposed window | Milestone | Exit evidence | State |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | D0–15 | Prioritize founder questions; scope advisers, company/IP route, REDA resources, cash controls and technical discovery | Critical rights/performance direction, named approvers and funded discovery; no unapproved irreversible commitments | Not started |
| Validation | D16–30 | Buyer discovery, design-partner interest, use case, product gaps, supplier term sheet and economics | Specific wedge, acceptable gaps and viable forecast; targets approved separately | Not started |
| Build and contract | D31–45 | Relevant agreements, service configuration, existing website adaptation, CRM, payments/tax and support controls | Rights and controls sufficient for selected pilot; scope/readiness accepted | Not started; prototype already available |
| Controlled pilot | D46–60 | Approved firms/workflows; test support, service changes, cancellation, export and incidents | No critical unresolved legal/security/functionality blockers; agreed value/usage threshold met | Not started |
| Commercial beta | D61–75 | Limited acquisition, first paid cohort, agreed migration help and enabled sales/support | Paid funnel works; actual collections and costs measured; spend authorized | Not started |
| Public launch decision | D76–90 | Review cohorts, claims, permissions, service, budget and experiments | Named authority records go/no-go; remain beta if gates fail | Not started |

## Dependencies and ownership

Company rights, REDA staffing and Circularo readiness can progress in parallel. None is inferred complete from another. Discovery acceptance does not authorize public launch. Teams supply operational evidence; authorized management records gate acceptance and any bounded exception in DECISIONS.

Proposed numerical research and recruitment targets are in [research](research/README.md); pilot thresholds remain SF-OQ-029. Agree definitions, periods and thresholds before using them to judge REDA or release funds. Weekly steering and D30/D60 reviews are proposed, not scheduled.

## Updating progress

Use [TASKS](TASKS.md) for actions, [OPEN-QUESTIONS](OPEN-QUESTIONS.md) for gate definitions, and [DECISIONS](DECISIONS.md) for accepted gates. Replan when approved scope/dependencies change. A phase is not complete because its nominal date passed.
