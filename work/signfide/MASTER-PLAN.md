# SignFide US launch master plan

## Direction and status

Develop a focused US legal agreement business powered by Circularo. Small and midsize US law firms and engagement letters, NDAs and routine business agreements are initial discovery hypotheses. Legal specialization, understandable evidence, predictable economics and attentive service are proposed switching benefits to validate, not proven superiority.

The initial horizon is a gated 90 days from approved kickoff. Kickoff has not been recorded. A working local marketing prototype exists; live service, executed company arrangements and an operational signup-to-cash journey are not established by it. Validate customer value and operating economics before public launch or scaled acquisition.

## Confirmed internal directions

Use [DECISIONS](DECISIONS.md) for exact scope and evidence. The working ownership proposal is JP Investment LLC 67% and REDA's designated holders 33%. SignFide is the US legal/brand/sales entity; Circularo provides technology and starting Option A managed co-branded service. REDA supplies the measurable commercial operation for direct sales and approved distributors/resellers. B and C remain future negotiated alternatives.

Use Good Faith oxblood/parchment and the serif wordmark without a separate symbol. The main motto is “Signing in faith”; campaign headlines are separate. Trademark title, US license, global expansion, operating appointments, supplier economics, equity mechanisms and actual launch remain unresolved.

## Gates and scope

[ROADMAP](ROADMAP.md) owns phase timing and gate evidence; [TASKS](TASKS.md) owns execution. Establish necessary rights, product/security acceptance, actual support/payment operation, accurate claims, spending authority and customer continuity before affected paid-customer or public promises. Exact pilot/public thresholds remain SF-OQ-018 and SF-OQ-029.

The initial commercial route is sales-assisted Odoo Quote-to-Cash, with Stripe/bank payment and assisted Circularo provisioning where needed. Configuring SignFide LLC in Odoo is the immediate operational priority. Self-service integration and freemium adoption extend that route later; they are not prerequisites for initial sales-assisted selling. The [operations overview](operations/README.md) owns system-of-record boundaries and implementation sequence. Migration priority remains an explicit conflict in SF-OQ-024.

Test the US wedge before broad consumer expansion, larger enterprise motions, full white labeling or separate platform operations. The current offering uses Circularo's existing SaaS platform; SignFide requirements feed Circularo's shared roadmap. Global brand/cross-border rights need separate founder decisions.

Competitive and price claims require fresh like-for-like evidence. Numerical experiments and research targets remain proposed in their owning briefs. Executed agreements govern obligations within scope; differences from working documents must be surfaced. Organization approval does not adopt the supplied launch plan wholesale.
