# SignFide research and evidence

## Discovery brief

The supplied launch plan proposes **15–20 qualified US legal buyer interviews by D30** and **3–5 design-partner commitments by D30/D45**. These are unapproved planning targets, not REDA quotas or scheduled activity. Confirm budget, definition, owner, consent and kickoff before execution through SF-OQ-026 and SF-OQ-029.

Prioritize the hypothesis of law firms with approximately 5–100 employees. Explore business/corporate practices first, alongside estate-planning and employment practices. Validate decision-maker, recurring agreement types, volume, current process/vendor economics, administrative pain, required permissions/evidence/integrations, client accessibility, migration barriers and willingness to pay. Exclude unsupported document/formality cases rather than assuming all practice workflows qualify.

Use a common interview script and aggregate objection/usage log. Separate prospect statements, observed behavior and analyst inference. Record consent and customer identities in the authorized business system; do not commit raw transcripts, contact lists or customer documents. A later research summary should identify date, method, sample limits, aggregate findings, evidence references and implications without promoting a small sample into market fact.

Recheck changing public prices, laws and product statements before giving new substantive advice or publishing claims. Historical source citations are not a fresh verification. Keep original observation date separate from edit date; frontmatter updated does not mean sources were rechecked. [Prototype heritage evidence](../website-prototype/references/heritage-evidence.md) preserves limited prior checks and owner-reported hosting.

Do not treat research instructions as authorization to contact prospects, send messages, obtain paid data or appoint partners.
