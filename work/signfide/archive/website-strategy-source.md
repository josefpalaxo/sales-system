# US Website Strategy & Content

> Historical source. The current [marketing brief](../marketing/README.md), [brand direction](../brand/README.md), and [local prototype](../website-prototype/README.md) supersede older implementation recommendations. Source recommendations do not establish available features, prices, or approved claims. The source body is retained below.

**Brand:** Signfide  
 **Tagline:** Signing in faith.  
 **Market:** United States  
 **Primary audience:** Law firms, attorneys, and legal teams  
 **Competitive target:** DocuSign

I recommend launching a focused, conversion-oriented website rather than a broad corporate website. The objective is to convince legal professionals that Signfide is a credible, easier-to-use alternative to DocuSign, with a clear reason to switch.

### **Homepage content sections**

**Section 2 — More than a signature**

*From document preparation to final signature, keep your legal agreements moving.*

Show four capabilities: Prepare documents, Collect signatures, Automate approvals, Preserve evidence.

**Section 3 — Built for legal professionals**

*Less paperwork. More time for your clients.*

Explain how firms can streamline engagement letters, service agreements, NDAs, employment documents, and other recurring agreements.

**Section 4 — Why switch to Signfide?**

*Everything you expect from eSignatures. A better way to work.*

Emphasize transparent pricing, straightforward workflows, reusable templates, and accessible support. Support comparative claims with verified evidence.

**Section 5 — Trust built into every agreement**

*Know who signed, what was signed, and when.*

Explain identity options, document integrity, audit trails, and verifiable records, with the actual assurance level and evidence capabilities clearly disclosed.

**Section 6 — Final CTA**

*Ready to simplify your legal agreements?*

Start signing with Signfide.

## **3\. Design and implementation direction**

**Visual direction**

Midnight navy, teal, ivory, refined typography, generous white space, and actual product interface screenshots. Avoid gavels, courthouse imagery, and generic legal stock photography.

---

**Product-led design**

Demonstrate the real signing experience, document preparation, audit evidence, and client workflows. Real screenshots will be more convincing than abstract marketing graphics.

## **4\. My launch recommendations**

1. **Launch with these eight pages**, not a 30-page marketing site.  
2. **Prioritize the DocuSign comparison page** for high-intent customer acquisition, with substantiated comparisons.  
3. **Build a working self-service trial** before spending aggressively on paid advertising.  
4. **Create legal-specific templates** and demonstrate actual workflows instead of generic signing scenarios.  
5. **Treat security and credibility as conversion features**, especially given the new brand and international technology foundation.

The biggest strategic decision remains whether Signfide will have its own branded product interface or primarily a branded marketing website connected to Circularo. I recommend a **fully branded Signfide customer experience on the shared Circularo technology platform**. This provides a coherent US brand without duplicating the underlying product.
