# SignFide — US Launch Master Plan

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 14 October 2026 | **Status:** Founder working plan — proposed, not approved | **Classification:** Confidential

## 1. Executive direction

**Mission:** Build SignFide into a trusted, legal-first US alternative to DocuSign, combining focused agreement workflows, evidence and records, predictable economics and attentive service, powered by Circularo.

**Brand:** SignFide / visual wordmark `signfide` · **Tagline:** *Signing in faith.*  
**Customer-facing proposition:** *Legal agreements, signed with confidence.*  
**Primary launch market:** US small and midsize law firms; first hypothesized segment firms with 5–100 employees, particularly business/corporate, estate planning, and employment practices. Validate the wedge before committing significant spend.

**90-day objective:** Establish a legally and commercially workable venture; validate a repeatable product/offer with 3–5 design partners; launch an operational site, sales funnel and controlled pilot; win first referenceable paying firms. **Not** an unqualified goal to match DocuSign feature-for-feature in 90 days.

**Operating premise:** SignFide is the US contracting and commercial business; Circularo provides underlying technology and manages the Option A co-branded service; REDA provides a measurable, staffed US commercial operation. Options B (SignFide-managed co-brand) and C (white label) stay as contingent future alternatives.

## 3. Market positioning and competitive thesis

### What SignFide sells

A focused legal agreement workspace to **prepare, send, approve, sign and retain verifiable records**. The initial tangible benefit is less friction in routine law-firm agreements, faster onboarding, clear evidence retrieval and predictable usage costs. “Powered by Circularo” supports trust/provenance where appropriate without eclipsing the SignFide brand.

**Proposed narrative:** “Inspired by *bona fide* — good faith — SignFide makes legal agreements easier to execute and easier to trust. From preparation to signature to the trusted record, every step is clear and verifiable.” Avoid suggesting that the name or tagline establishes any legal assurance.

### Initial ICP and use cases (hypotheses to test)

| Segment | Buyer | First workflow | Critical buying signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small business/corporate law firms (first priority hypothesis) | Managing partner / operations director | Engagement letters, NDAs, corporate approvals, client agreements | Recurring volume, administrative pain, poor existing contract economics |
| Estate-planning practices | Firm owner / practice administrator | Engagement/intake and eligible routine authorizations | Strong evidence and client UX; explicitly screen out witnessing/notarization/formal-execution cases not supported |
| Employment law boutiques | Partner / operations | Engagements, settlement-related workflow where appropriate, HR-related documents | Template reuse, signer accessibility, audit history |
| Legal support providers / consultants | Founder / operations lead | Repeated client authorizations | Channel potential, straightforward distribution |

**Exclusions at first launch:** Do not imply all wills, deeds, notarized documents, court filings or jurisdiction-specific instruments can be executed electronically. Validate document-type and state-specific exceptions before marketing those workflows.

### How SignFide beats an incumbent

1. **Legal focus:** opinionated templates, matter/team permissions and legal-record access rather than another generic signing interface.
2. **Predictable economics:** simple, published allowances with distinct charges for API, automation, KYC/identity and exceptional usage; no misleading “unlimited everything.”
3. **Migration:** hands-on switch package (templates, user training, workflow mapping, import only where supported).
4. **Trusted records:** exportable signing history and evidence with retention, retrieval and portability clearly explained.
5. **Service:** responsive US-facing onboarding and support, with documented availability and actual responsibility split.

**Competitive claim policy:** DocuSign has established signing, identity, audit, AI, integration and agreement-management products. Compare exact plan to plan, on the same usage basis. Do not advertise unsupported product superiority or price savings.

## 4. Corporate, ownership and contracting foundation

**Target structure (subject to advice):** JP Investment and designated REDA holder(s) own SignFide LLC; SignFide licenses/markets the SignFide brand and resells a Circularo-operated service to end customers or approved channel partners. Circularo and JP Investment are not interchangeable entities.

**P0 founder decisions — complete before definitive signatures or irreversible brand commitments:**

- **33% REDA interest:** identify owner(s); decide immediate issue vs milestone-based earning/vesting vs contractually specified buyback; define good/bad-leaver treatment and objectively evidenced underperformance remedies. Equity cannot be presumed to disappear upon REDA's removal as operator.
- **Governance:** member/manager appointment, delegated signing authority, budget approvals, reserved matters, conflicts and related-party approval, deadlock, funding calls, dilution and exits.
- **Supplier vs shareholder exit:** independently specify terms/termination of Circularo supply, REDA commercial appointment, SignFide brand license and JP Investment equity; preserve customers and records through transitions.
- **Brand/IP:** select global trademark/domain ownership (recommend parent/Circularo-aligned IP owner with a defined US territorial license); register and protect assets, define derivative works, enforce brand standards and debranding.
- **Territorial rights:** US scope; exclusivity only with performance and revocation conditions; cross-border accounts and international expansion; no automatic worldwide rights.
- **Related-party economics:** approve Circularo wholesale charges, change rights, included services, support transfer, audit and intercompany charges with documented conflict safeguards.
- **Distribution rights:** one- and two-tier model, who may appoint resellers, who holds the end-user contract, who bills, channel conflict, credit, refunds, pricing policy and anti-franchise review.

**Minimum contract package:** LLC operating agreement; members’ contribution/vesting or equity transfer arrangement; REDA services/performance schedule and RACI; Circularo–SignFide master supplier/reseller agreement and order/SLA/DPA; global trademark/domain ownership documentation and US brand license; customer terms, order form, privacy policy and data processing terms; reseller/distributor template; IP/contractor assignment and confidentiality agreements.

**Professional workstream:** US counsel (Delaware plus operating-state registration, consumer/commercial, privacy, franchise/channel, trademark); US CPA experienced in foreign-owned LLCs and cross-border transfer pricing; counsel/tax advice for proposed JP Investment holding route. Verify applicable federal, state and local tax filings rather than assuming an LLC is tax-free or administratively simple.

## 5. Product, trust and compliance readiness

### Product acceptance matrix (P0 = pilot blocking)

| Priority | Required customer outcome | Acceptance test |
|---|---|---|
| P0 | Email invitation, browser/mobile signing, accessible signer experience | External signer completes without paid account; tested across platforms |
| P0 | Repeatable templates and fields | Law-firm admin prepares, sends and reuses common agreements |
| P0 | Audit evidence and export | Download finalized document + attributable events/evidence; explain integrity and retention |
| P0 | Team/admin permissions | Correct tenant separation, workspace roles and privileged action logs |
| P0 | US tenancy / hosting disclosures | Confirm actual region, providers, subprocessors, access and deletion process; no unverified US-hosting promise |
| P0 | Trial → paid lifecycle | Signup, verification, provisioning, payment, renewal, tax, receipt, refund and cancellation work end to end |
| P0 | Support process | L1 REDA contact, L2/L3 Circularo route, incident owner and actual response commitments validated |
| P0 | Legal/security materials | Reviewed Terms, privacy, DPA, trust/security page, documented incident flow |
| P1 | Firm-facing migration | Templates/users/workflows migration method documented and successfully tested |
| P1 | Integrations | Prioritize Microsoft 365 and specific practice management platforms based on interview evidence |
| P1 | Legal workflow improvements | Review/approval and matter-oriented organization validated with design partners |
| P2 | AI and agentic automation | Only after confidential data governance, accuracy controls, explicit product scope and user oversight |

**Legal/compliance review:** ESIGN Act; UETA and state variation; state document-type exemptions; consent, attribution and retention; admissibility/evidence framing; attorney-client confidentiality and data protections; applicable state privacy laws; CAN-SPAM, TCPA and opt-in choices for outreach; payment card responsibility (use Stripe-hosted payment capture where feasible); security and breach response. Claims such as “legally guaranteed,” “court-approved everywhere,” “HIPAA compliant,” “SOC 2 certified” or “US-hosted” require specific evidence and approval.

**AI guardrail:** If legal document intelligence is offered, document whether customer content trains models, which subprocessors handle it, where it is processed, how consent works, what is retained and how outputs are checked. ABA Formal Opinion 512 underscores lawyer duties concerning competence, confidentiality and supervising AI use. Do not market automated legal advice.

**Technical policy:** No fork or duplicate codebase by default. Maintain a SignFide configuration/branding layer and prioritized core-roadmap changes within Circularo. Keep separate account and data boundaries even when infrastructure is shared.

## 6. Pricing, unit economics and cash controls

**Initial offer to test, not approve:**

- **Recipient:** Free signing; optional free record-view account, with clear retention/access terms.
- **Professional:** Simple per-user monthly/annual plan with clear human signing allowance and workspace features.
- **Firm:** Team controls, templates, support/onboarding and volume predictability; negotiated business packages for higher usage.
- **Metered additions:** API, automated workflows, identity verification, SMS/notary or other cost-intensive services when available.

**Pricing experiment:** Compare against the customer's real DocuSign renewal quote and usage; target 20–30% lower equivalent annual cost where gross margin permits. Public DocuSign Standard and Business Pro list benchmarks are about **$25 and $40 per user/month, billed annually**, with published envelope limits; refresh before publication. Do not publish SignFide price points until Circularo wholesale economics and usage policies are signed off.

**Unit economics model (one customer/month):** Net subscription + net usage revenue − Circularo platform/wholesale charge − payment fees − variable onboarding/support − commissions/channel margin − refunds/chargebacks. Track gross margin and contribution margin separately; add acquisition costs to evaluate CAC payback. Include sales tax nexus, taxable services classification and payment dispute reserves.

**Funding reality:** USD 10,000 is proposed seed capital, **not** evidence of adequate 90-day runway. Produce a cash-flow plan covering legal/tax, brand clearance, website, tools, contractor spend, REDA staffing (including what REDA contributes at its own expense), paid acquisition, Circularo charges and contingencies before authorizing spend. Agree capital-call and spending authority in writing.

## 7. Go-to-market engine

**Motion A — high-intent direct:** SEO/paid-search tests around “DocuSign alternative for law firms,” “electronic signatures for attorneys,” and specific workflows; targeted founder/partner outbound to qualified firms; demos showing a complete legal workflow. Use claims reviewed for accuracy.

**Motion B — channels:** Legal-tech consultants, managed IT providers, local law-firm associations and practice-management specialists. Start with 2–3 non-exclusive referral/design partners; expand to full resellers only after contract, support and margin model is proven.

**Motion C — switching program:** 30-minute workflow audit, competitive plan comparison, assistance with approved template migration, administrator onboarding, 30-day activation checklist and documented data-export options.

**Website minimum sitemap:** Home; Product; Solutions for Law Firms (practice-specific landing pages); Pricing; Security & Trust; Compare with DocuSign (fact-checked); Resources; Contact/Demo; Terms/Privacy/DPA. CTA should match reality: “Request early access” before self-serve exists, “Start free” only once self-serve works.

**Funnel definitions:** Visitor → lead → qualified discovery → demo → trial/design partner → first real completed agreement → paying account → active at 30/90 days → retained/expanded. Own CRM, marketing consent, attribution and source-of-truth definitions at SignFide.

**Evidence-building:** Secure written permissions for testimonials, logos and migration stories. Publish a first legal-specific case study only after there is real usage and documented approval.

## 12. Decision register: next founder meeting

**Must decide now:** (1) Exact JP Investment vehicle and SignFide entity/tax route; (2) REDA 33% issuance/earning and underperformance remedies; (3) who globally owns SignFide brand/domain and the US license; (4) US exclusivity and overseas expansion boundaries; (5) Circularo supplier economics/term/termination and customer continuity; (6) governance, budget and additional funding; (7) named REDA capacity, initial 90-day resources and unpaid-vs-paid distinction; (8) initial channel model and contracting/billing counterparty.

**Approve after discovery:** ICP wedge, initial pricing/packages, migration scope, first third-party integrations, headline and claims, acquisition budgets and day-90 growth target.

**Do not expand yet:** Broad consumer offer, 50-state niche-document guarantees, two-tier resale, custom white-label hosting, agentic legal advice or unbounded usage allowances.

1. **Founders / counsel:** Turn open-question registry SF-OQ-001…023 into decisions, with owners, decision date and governing-document reference; prioritize P0.
2. **Founders / REDA:** Obtain named REDA team, weekly time allocation, committed funding, expected introductions and first 90-day delivery schedule; agree shortfall process.
3. **Circularo / SignFide:** Produce upstream service and wholesale term sheet including US deployment, security, support, billing units, SLA, DPA, termination and data continuity.
4. **IP lead / trademark counsel:** Verify signfide.com registrant and control, perform federal/state/common-law/confusingly similar and phonetic mark clearance, then determine trademark application owner.
5. **US CPA / counsel:** Prepare a short memo comparing entity and holding choices, registrations, reporting, federal/state taxes and transfer-pricing exposure before incorporation.
6. **REDA sales:** Schedule 15–20 buyer interviews using one script and common objection/usage log; produce shortlist of 3–5 design partners.
7. **Circularo product:** Audit actual existing feature availability against P0 matrix, including US hosting assertions and template/evidence capabilities; list gaps with effort and owners.
8. **SignFide management / finance:** Build a 12-month model and realistic first-90-day cash budget (base, downside, upside) before paid marketing.
9. **REDA marketing:** Draft site information architecture and messaging; do not make unverifiable comparative, legal or security claims.
10. **Management:** Set weekly steering meeting and one living risk/decision tracker; formal day-30 and day-60 stage gates.
