# Earlier assistant product readiness draft

> Superseded assistant-generated draft. This used launch-plan proposals as the product overview and overstated a new product/pilot framing. It is not SignFide product scope, an agreed roadmap or a required acceptance checklist. Use [the product strategy](../product/README.md) for the existing Circularo edition foundation and packaging approach. Historical text follows.

## Scope and evidence

This is the owning proposed product/pilot acceptance matrix. No row establishes a shipped SignFide feature. Circularo product/security must map each outcome to existing supported capability, configuration/adaptation, new work, or exclusion; add evidence, build/configuration, tester, actual result and acceptance date. REDA supplies buyer evidence and customer-process acceptance. Assigned people and approved scope remain open.

The [prototype PRODUCT](../website-prototype/PRODUCT.md) governs local illustrative behavior, not service availability. [Operating models](../company/operating-model-options.md) define supply/operations boundaries. Proposed initial workflows are engagement letters, NDAs, corporate approvals and routine client agreements; actual document/state suitability requires review before marketing. Do not assume every will, deed, court filing, witness/notary process or jurisdictional form is eligible.

## Pilot and technical boundaries

The source plan proposes 3–5 design partners and at least three pilot firms completing real agreements as launch evidence. Those thresholds are unapproved; [research](../research/README.md) owns recruitment targets and SF-OQ-029 owns gate acceptance. A fictional website demonstration does not count as real usage. An available export must be tested; no import capability is assumed.

The proposed approach is one shared Circularo core with a SignFide configuration/branding layer, not an automatic fork. Assess required changes, ownership, funding and support before development; see SF-OQ-014 and SF-OQ-038. A US Azure hosting location is owner-reported in [heritage evidence](../website-prototype/references/heritage-evidence.md), not an independently verified configuration or certification.

Use SF-T-010 for the audit and SF-T-014/SF-T-021 for acceptance execution in [TASKS](../TASKS.md). Product questions, migration conflict, self-service choice and AI remain in [OPEN-QUESTIONS](../OPEN-QUESTIONS.md), not a second backlog here.
