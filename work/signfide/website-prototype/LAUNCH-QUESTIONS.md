# Signfide launch questions

Prepared 2026-10-14. Internal working questions; no launch or product approval implied.

| Area | Accountable owner needs to confirm | Prototype treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offer and availability | Which Signfide product exists, who can use it, and when it can be offered | Persistent proposed-experience notice; no signup or trial claim |
| Product identity | Fully branded product versus marketing brand on a shared platform | Clearly labelled conceptual demonstration |
| Core signing journey | Sender/recipient behavior, signing methods, account requirements, mobile/accessibility support | Fictional engagement letter with no real signing |
| Workflow | Templates, version handling, approval roles, automation, reminders, and exceptions | Proposed sequence; no feature availability promises |
| Integrations and migration | Supported systems, template import, historical records, implementation responsibilities | Buyer questions only |
| Identity and integrity | Available identity checks, actual assurance levels, document integrity and independent verification | Pending topics on Security & Trust |
| Evidence and retention | Event records, time sources, export formats, storage, retention, deletion, backup, and exit behavior | Fictional record; no real evidence export |
| Hosting and privacy | Contracting entity, regions, subprocessors, privacy notices, data handling terms | No invented locations, entities, or privacy guarantees |
| Security and legal review | Documented controls, applicable certifications, approved security claims and legal statements for the actual service | Questions and pending confirmations; no badges or compliance assertions |
| Commercial terms | Approved prices, plans, allowances, implementation, support scope, and additional service charges | Enquiry preview; no prices, discounts, or unlimited-use language |
| Booking and contact | Approved contact destination, actual booking system, consent/retention requirements, and response process | Local form previews; no endpoint or data retention |
| Competitor evaluation | Current like-for-like evidence, commercial terms, and owner-approved comparative claims | Neutral questions asked of both providers |
| Brand | Final custom lettering/vector logo, small-size tests, domain/brand clearance and required legal footer information | Provisional live-text Newsreader wordmark and serif s favicon |
| Proof and imagery | Authorized product screenshots, customer proof, names/logos, and any permitted testimonials | No fabricated screenshots, customer logos, or testimonials |
| Publication | Approved final copy, legal/privacy material, hosting configuration, domain control, and explicit publication instruction | Local preview only; no hosted Site or deployment |

Keep the proposed-experience notice and local-only form disclosures until the relevant product and service claims can be substantiated and approved. Public launch requires a separate implementation and approval pass.
