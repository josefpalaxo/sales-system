# Circularo heritage and service model website handover

Prepared: 2026-10-14  
Status: Recommendations and instructions for the next prototype update  
Classification: Internal working material, non-canonical

The Signfide website should explain what “Powered by Circularo” means, how Circularo’s experience supports confidence in the technology, and why customers may encounter Circularo branding in the service ecosystem. A short endorsement alone does not explain the agreed Option A model.

This handover records the recommendations requested by Josef. Saving it does not implement the changes or authorize publication. Keep the update local and within this project unless the active implementation request states otherwise.

## Core message

**SignFide is the US company, brand, and sales entity. REDA’s team operates its commercial customer lifecycle. Circularo supplies and operates the underlying technology under Option A.**

For customer-facing copy, explain the SignFide/Circularo relationship. REDA’s internal delivery role and the ownership split need not become website marketing content.

| Layer | Explain to customers |
| --- | --- |
| SignFide | The focused US offering, commercial relationship, onboarding, and first-line customer support |
| Circularo | The underlying agreement technology, managed platform operations, software maintenance, and technical escalation |
| Existing ecosystem | Applicable applications and integrations retain Circularo branding; explain exactly which components are available with the agreed SignFide offer |

Circularo’s track record supports confidence in the technology. SignFide must establish its own service reputation. Attribute each organization’s experience and responsibilities accurately.

## Governing service model

Use the [SignFide cooperation responsibilities and RACI draft](../company/cooperation-responsibilities-and-raci.md), especially “Initial service model,” as the internal Option A delivery reference. Read the [SignFide operating model options](../company/operating-model-options.md) for the complete A/B/C comparison, ecosystem responsibilities, and transition conditions. Both remain working documents, not evidence that the service is already live.

Preserve these distinctions in the website:

- The starting model is **Option A, Circularo-managed co-branded service**, based on existing Circularo editions and an agreed configuration.
- The baseline is a **planned dedicated Microsoft Azure environment in the United States, operated by Circularo**. Actual configuration, edition scope, readiness, charges, and service commitments still need to be recorded before launch. Do not imply deployment has already occurred.
- Existing applicable mobile applications, marketplace applications, and integrations remain Circularo-branded. Option A does not include a separately published SignFide application or integration ecosystem.
- SignFide handles the commercial relationship, onboarding, and first-line support through its designated operating team; Circularo handles the underlying platform and L2/L3 technical escalation.
- Options B and C are future contractual alternatives, not current website promises. Do not present the initial service as complete white label.

Do not publish the internal RACI, shareholder details, starting capital, or special supplier terms as part of explaining this model.

## Suggested prototype copy

These are editorial starting points for the existing internal prototype. Retain proposed/planned wording until launch facts are confirmed.

### Homepage section

**The technology behind Signfide**

**A focused US offering. An established technology foundation.**

Signfide is being developed for US legal professionals using Circularo’s agreement technology. SignFide will manage the commercial relationship, onboarding, and first-line support, while Circularo will provide and operate the underlying platform.

The initial service is planned around a dedicated US Microsoft Azure environment. Applicable applications and integrations will remain part of the Circularo-branded ecosystem.

Link: **Learn about Circularo and Signfide** → `/about#powered-by-circularo`.

### About section

**What Powered by Circularo means**

Signfide brings a US legal-market focus to an offering built on Circularo’s agreement technology. Under the planned initial service model, Circularo operates the underlying platform, while SignFide manages the customer relationship, onboarding, and first-line support.

Circularo publishes customer stories describing its use in legal practices and other organizations. These examples illustrate Circularo’s technology experience; they are not SignFide customer references.

The service will use applicable parts of the existing Circularo ecosystem. You may therefore encounter the Circularo name in supported applications, integrations, or documentation. The available components and their requirements will be identified with the final SignFide offer.

External link label: **Visit Circularo’s website** → `https://www.circularo.com/`.

### Ecosystem FAQ

**Why might I see the Circularo name?**

Circularo provides the technology behind Signfide. Certain supported mobile applications and integrations carry the Circularo name. We will identify the components available with your SignFide service and explain how to use them.

**Who will help me if I need support?**

Under the planned service model, SignFide will be your first point of contact for onboarding, account questions, and support. Technical platform issues will be escalated to Circularo as needed. Support channels and service commitments will be confirmed before launch.

**Where is the service planned to run?**

The initial model calls for a dedicated Microsoft Azure environment in the United States, operated by Circularo. The final configuration and hosting details remain to be confirmed for launch.

### External links and current checks

The current build rejects any `http` or `https` string in generated HTML, and QA assumes all anchors are local. Adding the intended Circularo links will therefore require a deliberate test refinement:

- In `scripts/build.mjs`, distinguish approved external navigation links from external runtime dependencies. Permit the specific reviewed official URLs as anchors; continue to reject external scripts, styles, fonts, images, embeds, and unexpected URLs. Do not simply remove the check.
- In `scripts/qa.mjs`, keep internal-route and fragment checks intact. Classify reviewed external anchors separately and validate their exact destinations and accessible labels. Do not fetch or follow external sites as part of the local page-load/network assertions.
- Preserve the zero-external-runtime-request check on local page loads, along with no submissions and no browser storage. A user deliberately following a labelled external link is separate from the prototype making a background request.
- External links may use normal navigation, or open a new tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer"` and a clear indication that they leave the site. Do not add remote logos, analytics, or embedded pages.

7. Inspect the revised homepage, About, Security & Trust, and FAQ at 1440, 768, 390, and 320px. Verify heading measure, endorsement hierarchy, anchor visibility below the sticky header, touch targets, focus, and contrast. Save representative screenshots locally.
