# SignFide marketing and website planning

## Audience and acquisition hypotheses

Start discovery with small/midsize US law firms. Business/corporate, estate-planning and employment practices are hypotheses, not validated segments. [Research](../research/README.md) owns discovery targets and findings; [brand](../brand/README.md) owns identity and positioning. Avoid broad consumer and large-enterprise expansion before the wedge and economics are understood.

The supplied launch plan proposes three motions:

- High-intent direct acquisition: search/limited paid tests around law-firm signing and alternatives, targeted outbound, and demos of complete supported workflows.
- Channels: legal-tech consultants, managed IT providers, associations and practice-management specialists, subject to authorized appointments and [commercial rules](../commercial/README.md).
- Switching assistance: proposed 30-minute workflow audit, like-for-like comparison, supported template migration, admin onboarding and 30-day activation checklist. Scope, staffing and migration capability require approval before promotion.

Measure activation and customer economics rather than traffic alone. Outreach, consent, tracking and retention require the relevant review. No campaign, spend, outbound message or partner appointment is authorized by this planning brief.

## Website and content

The existing [eight-page local prototype](../website-prototype/README.md) is the starting implementation, not a live commercial service. Its fictional demonstrations and forms send nothing. Keep it self-contained and runnable. Customer-facing content must not disclose confidential owners, internal RACI, capital or supplier economics.

The proposed public information architecture adds/organizes Product, Law Firm Solutions, Pricing, Security & Trust, comparison, useful resources, contact/demo and reviewed legal/privacy/data terms. Do not add empty destinations or imply a future sitemap is already implemented. Use the selected Good Faith identity; original navy/teal recommendations in the [archived website source](../archive/website-strategy-source.md) are superseded.

CTAs must match actual operation: early-access/enquiry while that is the only available path, free/self-service language only once approved and tested. Replace local form previews only after an authorized contact destination, handling/consent rules and working flow exist. Terms/privacy pages are launch deliverables requiring review, not generic boilerplate to claim readiness.

## Proof and publication

Use current like-for-like evidence for comparisons. Do not invent testimonials, prices, availability, savings, certifications, legal guarantees or product screenshots. Circularo-published experience remains attributed to Circularo and does not become SignFide customer proof. Written permission is needed for actual customer names, logos and case studies. See [heritage evidence](../website-prototype/references/heritage-evidence.md).
