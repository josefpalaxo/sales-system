# SignFide commercial planning

## Offer experiments

The product foundation is Circularo's existing SaaS editions. SignFide can retain their packaging, introduce its own customer-facing plans or develop industry bundles as described in [Product](../product/README.md). Final names, features, limits and US prices remain to be selected; the older free/professional/firm package proposal does not replace that edition baseline.

The [wholesale buy-price model](wholesale-pricing.md) owns supplier pricing methodology. SignFide's sales calculations start from its agreed buy price, independent of its retail discounts and actual selling price. The [distribution/services agreement principles](../company/circularo-distribution-agreement.md) describe the related contractual framework. Actual wholesale rates and service/IP remuneration remain open.

The source pricing experiment is to test 20–30% lower comparable annual total cost than a buyer's real DocuSign quote, where margins allow. It is not an approved discount or public claim. Capture exact plan, annual volume, features, term, geography, date and evidence; do not reuse historical public list-price benchmarks as current prices. Refresh comparisons before any use and approve SignFide wholesale/retail economics first. SF-OQ-032 owns this choice.

## Economic definitions and funding

For a customer/month, model net subscription and usage revenue less Circularo charges, payment fees, variable onboarding/support, commissions/channel margin and refunds/chargebacks. Define accounting treatment to avoid deducting a channel discount twice. Track gross margin and contribution margin separately, add fully loaded acquisition costs for CAC/payback, and distinguish contracted revenue from collected cash. Address taxes, credit and reserves with finance/advisers.

The [company brief](../company/README.md) owns the starting-capital premise and unresolved funding split. That amount is not evidence of 90-day runway. SF-T-007 proposes first-90-day and 12-month base/downside/upside cash models covering advisers, brand, tools, staffing, acquisition, Circularo, taxes and contingencies. Separate REDA-funded resources from company-paid work using the [contribution schedule](../company/reda-contribution-and-performance.md).

## Sales and channel model

Direct sales and approved US distributors/resellers are confirmed internal direction; [the corporate/commercial model](../company/corporate-structure-and-commercial-model.md) owns contract, seller, invoicer, collector and tier definitions. Referral, one-tier resale and optional two-tier models have different economics and customer responsibilities. No appointments, margins, exclusivity or onward rights are automatic.

The supplied plan proposes starting with 2–3 non-exclusive referral/design partners and delaying full/two-tier resale until contracts and support are proven. This is a sequencing proposal, not a change to the approved direct-plus-channel direction; SF-OQ-031 needs a decision. REDA runs partner recruitment, enablement, performance and direct/channel conflict coordination under the [RACI](../company/cooperation-responsibilities-and-raci.md).

## Funnel and measurement

Proposed funnel: visitor → lead → qualified discovery → demo → trial/design partner where offered → first completed real agreement → paying account → active at 30/90 days → retained/expanded. Agree qualification, deduplication, source attribution, stage entry/exit and data access before reporting. Raw prospects and CRM records remain in the CRM, not this repository.

Use the [contribution schedule](../company/reda-contribution-and-performance.md) for resource/execution versus outcome measures and paying-customer/cash definitions. [Operations](../operations/README.md) owns process/reporting handoffs. Actions are SF-T-011/SF-T-012/SF-T-016/SF-T-022 in [TASKS](../TASKS.md); unresolved commercial choices remain in [OPEN-QUESTIONS](../OPEN-QUESTIONS.md).

The initial transaction route is sales-assisted Quote-to-Cash using a separate SignFide company in shared Odoo. CRM owns leads/opportunities; Odoo owns quotations, invoices and financial subscriptions. Stripe/banking records payments and Circularo holds entitlements/usage. Refer to the [operations architecture](../operations/README.md#business-data-ownership) for authoritative boundaries; self-service checkout is a later extension, not a requirement for initial assisted selling.
