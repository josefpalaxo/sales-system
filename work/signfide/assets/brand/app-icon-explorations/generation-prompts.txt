A / SF CLASSIC

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: flat app-icon concept study for Signfide.
Input image 1 is a style reference only: preserve the Good Faith oxblood/parchment palette and the refined serif DNA of its lowercase signfide wordmark. Do not recreate the whole reference board.
Create a square, expertly typeset design presentation. Parchment #F5F0E6 background, oxblood #4B2432 icon with parchment lettering. Graphite #29272A for small labels. No clay inside the main icon: the core icon must work in one ink. No shadows, gradients, paper grain, 3D, embossing, stock mockups, device frames, rings, circles, seals, shields, pens, checkmarks, or extra symbols.
Layout: quiet small option title at top-left. One very large rounded square app icon centered in upper two-thirds, generous clearspace, corner radius about 19% of tile width, crisp flat vector-like edges. Below, three small repeated versions of EXACT same monogram: standard oxblood tile, reversed parchment tile with oxblood lettering and subtle graphite border, tiny oxblood tile. This row shows relative small-size legibility, no size claims or pixel labels. Bottom: small elegant lowercase wordmark "signfide", spelling s i g n f i d e, with no separate symbol beside it. No tagline in icon. Clear editorial hierarchy, professional identity design, not a contact sheet of random marks.
Lettering: sturdy contemporary serif related to the reference, moderate contrast and open counters, substantial stems that can survive reduction, no fragile hairlines. Optical centering in the square; the monogram occupies about 60% of the tile width with enough margins. Two letters must remain readable. No other text except option title and the small wordmark.
Direction A. Exact title "A / SF CLASSIC". Lettering exactly "SF", both UPPERCASE. Put the two letters side-by-side on the same baseline, equal cap height. Beautiful custom editorial serif, a balanced S and a crisp F, tight but clearly separated kerning. NO overlap, NO ligature, NO extra swash. Use the natural contrast of the S curve and F vertical as the memorable shape. Legibility is the primary objective.
