# SignFide product strategy

## Product foundation

SignFide is a US-focused commercial brand powered by Circularo's existing SaaS platform. Its offering is based on four existing Circularo SaaS editions:

1. Start
2. Pro
3. Business
4. Enterprise

Reference: [Circularo edition comparison](https://www.circularo.com/pricing/#compare-plans).

Circularo remains responsible for the core platform, product capabilities, development and technical roadmap. Under the current Option A, Circularo also operates the service; SignFide does not operate a separate technology stack. Future [Options B and C](../company/operating-model-options.md) can change who operates the environment and publishes applications while retaining Circularo's underlying technology.

## Commercial packaging

SignFide may market and sell the underlying Circularo editions using different commercial packaging. Three approaches are under consideration:

- **Direct mapping:** retain Circularo's existing editions and feature packaging.
- **SignFide-specific plans:** introduce different customer-facing names, pricing and packaging.
- **Industry-specific bundles:** package existing capabilities for particular US legal-market segments and use cases.

These approaches are not mutually exclusive. The initial recommendation is to preserve the existing product editions while exploring differentiated legal-industry bundles and US-specific pricing. Packaging must remain compatible with Circularo's licensing and provisioning capabilities.

Edition naming, bundle composition and SignFide pricing remain open. [Commercial planning](../commercial/README.md) covers pricing and economics; this folder explains the product foundation and edition-to-offer mapping.

## Product roadmap

### Q1 2027 freemium target

Josef reports an internal Circularo roadmap target to introduce a freemium offering based on Start by the end of Q1 2027. This is a planning target, not a guaranteed public release date or an approved SignFide launch commitment.

SignFide should evaluate adopting it for US customer acquisition. Potential audiences include solo attorneys, small law firms and external signing recipients. Circularo availability and SignFide adoption are separate decisions; the latter remains open.

## Product principles

1. **Reuse before customization:** prioritize existing Circularo capabilities.
2. **Commercial flexibility:** distinguish underlying editions from customer-facing plans.
3. **Legal-industry focus:** differentiate through positioning, packaging, workflows and customer experience.
4. **Operational simplicity:** avoid unnecessary licensing, provisioning, billing and support complexity.
5. **Shared roadmap:** coordinate SignFide requirements with Circularo product development.
