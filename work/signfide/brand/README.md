# SignFide brand direction

## Selected identity

Use **Good Faith, alternative A**: a lowercase serif `signfide` wordmark without a separate leading symbol. The main motto and primary tagline is **Signing in faith**. See SF-D-003 and SF-D-004 in [DECISIONS](../DECISIONS.md). This direction supersedes navy/teal, generic sans-serif logo treatments and the original circular loop. It does not establish trademark clearance or final production assets.

| Role | Reference color | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oxblood | `#4B2432` | Wordmark, key headings, primary actions and selected dark surfaces |
| Parchment | `#F5F0E6` | Light canvas and light text on oxblood |
| Graphite | `#29272A` | Functional and body text |
| Clay | `#C38D70` | Restrained accents; verify contrast before text use |

Newsreader is the editorial serif and provisional wordmark reference; Inter serves body/UI text. The raster board is [alternative A](../assets/brand/good-faith-alternatives/a-wordmark.png). Final custom lettering, kerning, vector/monochrome masters and actual small-size tests remain unfinished. A serif s is the prototype favicon, not an approved final app icon; the two-letter explorations remain unselected.

## Positioning and language

SignFide is the proposed US legal agreement business powered by Circularo. The initial positioning hypothesis is a focused agreement experience for US legal professionals: preparation, signature and retrievable records with understandable economics and attentive service. Proposed supporting headline: “Legal agreements, signed with confidence.” A supporting headline does not replace the selected motto.

The name is inspired by bona fide and good faith; it is an invented name rather than a literal Latin phrase or legal assurance. Use precise, warm, modern language. Avoid claims of guaranteed validity, universal court approval, inherited certifications or competitor deficiencies. Legal specialization, migration ease and service quality are goals to prove.

Use SignFide LLC for the intended legal company and Signfide for the customer-facing brand; the visual wordmark is lowercase. The agreed presentation under Option A is Signfide — Powered by Circularo, with applicable ecosystem components retaining Circularo branding. Exact endorsement placement and customer explanations must follow [the operating model](../company/operating-model-options.md) and confirmed launch scope.
