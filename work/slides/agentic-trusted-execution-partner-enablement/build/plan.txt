Communication job
By the end, Circularo teams and partners should be able to explain why agentic systems need an institutional execution boundary, position Circularo as that boundary, and lead a practical discovery conversation around action, authority, assurance, and evidence.

Narrative arc
AI moves from insight to action -> capability is not authority -> fragmented handoffs weaken accountability -> trust must cover the full lifecycle -> Circularo creates one governed execution environment -> evidence becomes trusted AI context -> partners turn the model into an engagement.
