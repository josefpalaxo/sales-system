About Circularo — external company and platform overview

Communication job

what the company builds, why trusted execution matters now, and which delivery model
creates the right path to engagement.

Audience
- Investors evaluating Circularo's strategic position and platform scope.
- Enterprise and government leaders evaluating digital trust infrastructure.
- Technology, channel, and implementation partners evaluating where they connect.

Evidence discipline
- Do not assert headquarters, tenure, customers, deployment scale, certifications,
  market share, revenue, growth, or quantified outcomes.
- Do not use customer logos or certification marks without approved evidence.
- Treat category and platform descriptions as a working external narrative requiring
  normal human approval before publication.
