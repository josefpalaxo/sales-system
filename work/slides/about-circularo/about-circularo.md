# About Circularo

> Working deck reference. This file describes a non-canonical presentation under `work/` and does not approve or verify its claims.

## Deck

- PowerPoint: [about-circularo.pptx](output/about-circularo.pptx)
- Slides: 15
- Audience: investors, enterprise and government leaders, technology partners, channel partners, and implementation partners
- Use: external company and platform overview, subject to normal human approval

## Purpose

Explain who Circularo is, what the company builds, why trusted execution matters as digital work becomes more autonomous, and how customers or partners can engage through the appropriate delivery model.

The intended outcome is for the audience to understand Circularo as digital trust infrastructure for consequential work—not only as an electronic-signature provider.

## Key points

- Digital work increasingly involves people, organizations, applications, and AI agents.
- Intelligence and automation do not create institutional authority by themselves.
- Circularo positions its platform as the Trusted Execution Layer connecting identity, authority, policy, approval, execution, evidence, and records.
- Business processes, trust services, and trusted records form one connected lifecycle.
- The same trust model can extend across user experiences, enterprise systems, APIs, and governed agentic actions.
- Circularo supports three delivery narratives: Circularo SaaS, Circularo Sovereign, and Sovereign Trust Shared Services.
- The recommended starting point is a consequential action and the trust boundary required around it.

## Story structure

Company role → rising autonomy → authority gap → Trusted Execution Layer → connected trust lifecycle → programmable trust → delivery models → engagement decision.

## Recommended use

- Introduce Circularo to a new investor, customer, or partner audience.
- Establish the strategic platform narrative before moving into a product-specific deck.
- Route the next conversation toward managed SaaS, customer-controlled sovereign deployment, or shared sovereign infrastructure.

## Qualification

The deck intentionally avoids unapproved claims about company location, tenure, customers, deployments, scale, market share, revenue, certifications, compliance, or quantified outcomes. The company and category narrative remains working external positioning until approved through the normal governance process.
