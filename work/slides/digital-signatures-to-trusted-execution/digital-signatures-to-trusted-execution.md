# From Digital Signatures to Trusted Execution

## Deck

- PowerPoint: [digital-signatures-to-trusted-execution.pptx](output/digital-signatures-to-trusted-execution.pptx)
- Slides: 14
- Audience: Circularo colleagues, technology partners, implementation partners, and channel partners
- Use: internal strategy, partner education, discovery, and solution-design conversations

## Purpose

Explain why digital trust is expanding beyond signatures, distinguish customer-valued Trusted Executions from the underlying Trust Events, and provide a model partners can use to structure business and solution conversations.

## Key points

- The operating model is shifting from human-led digitization toward application- and agent-executed work.
- Customer value increasingly sits in trusted business execution rather than access to an isolated signature capability.
- A Trusted Execution is the consequential business outcome the customer needs to complete and prove.
- One Trusted Execution may orchestrate multiple Trust Events, including identity, approval, signing, sealing, timestamping, verification, evidence, and preservation.
- Assurance should be selected as a business and risk decision, not treated as one universal technical level.
- Execution Profiles adapt the trust recipe to the action, actors, market, and required evidence.
- Circularo connects the complete trust chain across process, trust services, execution, and records.
- Agentic work increases the burden of proof because software may prepare, initiate, or execute actions.
- Access, execution, and trust combine into one strategic platform proposition.
- Partners can turn the model into a repeatable discovery, design, and delivery engagement.

## Story structure

Human digitization → agentic operating model → value moves to execution → Trusted Execution and Trust Event model → assurance and profiles → Circularo trust chain → partner application.

## Recommended use

- Align internal and partner terminology around Trusted Executions and Trust Events.
- Reframe a signature-led conversation around the complete customer outcome.
- Structure assurance and execution-profile discovery.
- Introduce the three delivery routes without prescribing an unvalidated architecture.

## Primary next step

Choose a customer outcome, decompose it into Trust Events, and validate the required assurance, jurisdiction, providers, workflow, and evidence model.

## Qualification

Assurance levels and Execution Profiles are conceptual. Market, provider, jurisdiction, and use-case requirements must be validated during solution design. The deck introduces no pricing, volume, certification, performance, customer, or regulatory claims.
