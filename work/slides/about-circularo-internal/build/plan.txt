Content guardrails

- Use “trusted actions” instead of “consequential actions” in visible copy.
- Treat planned capabilities and Agentic Trusted Execution as strategic direction unless supported by separate current product evidence.
- Do not imply ownership of external identity, PKI, national, sovereign, or third-party trust services that Circularo integrates or orchestrates.
- Keep all customer, certification, and compliance references qualified for internal use pending current approval checks.
- Present SaaS, Sovereign, and Shared Services as delivery models of one platform.
