# About Circularo — Internal Strategy Deck

## Purpose

This deck provides the internal master narrative for Circularo as a company, platform, and strategic direction. It connects the company’s evolution from digital signatures to Trust Orchestration, Trusted Execution, and Agentic Trusted Execution.

## Audience

Circularo leadership, product, sales, marketing, and customer-facing teams.

## Key points

- Circularo is a digital trust technology company focused on trusted digital processes for enterprises, governments, and digital services.
- Digital signatures remain foundational while the scope expands to identity, authority, workflow, trust services, evidence, trusted records, and agentic execution.
- Trust Orchestration describes the platform capability. Trusted Execution describes the resulting trusted and verifiable business outcome.
- The Circularo Trust Orchestration Platform contains six connected areas: Content & Collaboration, Identity & Authority, Workflow & Approval, Trust Services, Evidence & Trusted Records, and Programmable Trust & Agentic Execution.
- Circularo SaaS, Circularo Sovereign, and Sovereign Trust Shared Services apply one trust model through different delivery and operating models.
- Circularo’s agentic opportunity sits at the execution boundary where authority, policy, approval, action, and evidence meet.

## Reusable platform chapter

Slides 7–14 are designed to function as an independent deck. Slide 7 is the chapter cover, slides 8–13 explain one platform area each, and slide 14 provides the connected platform summary.

## Internal-use guardrails

- Use “trusted actions” instead of “consequential actions” in audience-facing language.
- Verify customer-reference permissions and current approved wording before external use.
- Verify certification, compliance, identity-integration, and product-availability statements against current approved records.
- Keep planned capabilities and the broader Agentic Trusted Execution model clearly identified as strategic direction where applicable.
- Do not imply that Circularo owns external trust infrastructure that it integrates or orchestrates.
