# Circularo and OmniTrust US pilot and positioning

**Proposal for discussion with OmniTrust | 30 September 2026**

We propose starting with OmniTrust’s own Docusign workflows, then testing a focused replacement offer with ten existing OmniTrust customers that use Docusign. The objective is to replace Docusign at **three of those ten customers** before a broader launch, building practical evidence about customer value, pricing, implementation effort and reasons to switch.

OmniTrust’s internal evaluation would be the first learning step and would be counted separately from the ten customer opportunities. The three-customer objective is a pilot target, not a forecast or a claim about wider market conversion.

## The proposition to test

> **Your documents. Your identity. Your control.**
>
> Circularo connects document preparation, approvals, signing and follow-up. Business supports everyday team workflows; Enterprise adds organizational identity, access administration and operational oversight. Together with OmniTrust, selected workflows can extend into enterprise-controlled identity and cryptographic trust.

The commercial ambition is approximately **40% lower recurring cost for a comparable scope**, while giving customers concrete operational reasons to switch. This is a pricing target to validate through customer quotes, not an established average saving or a guaranteed discount.

The strongest benefits to demonstrate are:

| Customer benefit | What to demonstrate |
| --- | --- |
| Recognizable signing requests | Enterprise configured with the customer’s own domain and email infrastructure. |
| Approvals that keep moving | A pending transaction identified through reporting, an ownership issue resolved by an authorized controller, and the agreement completed. |
| Less account administration | Enterprise single sign-on and directory-based user provisioning. |
| Better follow-up after signing | Contract deadlines, renewal reminders and retrieval of the document and its history. |
| Trust governance for demanding workflows | A separately scoped Circularo–OmniTrust solution connecting document processes to identity, certificate and key governance. |

These benefits should be shown in the customer’s workflow. Docusign already offers substantial signing, collaboration, integration and agreement-management capabilities. Our case should rest on the fit, packaging, service and cost of the proposed solution.

## Start with OmniTrust

OmniTrust’s own use of Docusign provides a practical starting point. We suggest selecting two or three recurring processes, such as an NDA, a supplier agreement or an internal approval, based on actual usage.

Together, we would document the current subscription, users, transaction volumes, renewal date, templates, integrations and required controls. We would then configure the selected processes in Circularo and let the people who use them compare the experience.

The internal evaluation should produce a working demonstration, a realistic cost comparison, an implementation checklist and an honest list of gaps. If the agreed requirements are met, the next step would be moving the selected processes into production and planning the corresponding Docusign reduction or replacement. Historical agreements and their evidence must remain accessible throughout the transition.

This gives OmniTrust’s SDR firsthand experience of the product and a more credible conversation with customers. Any wider internal replacement should follow the results of that evaluation.

## Select ten existing customers using Docusign

Begin by identifying Docusign users across OmniTrust’s existing customer base, then agree an initial group of ten. Favor accounts with an accessible process owner, a known renewal or expansion decision, and a workflow both teams can evaluate directly. Include a range of volumes and requirements so the feedback is useful beyond a single type of account.

Once the ten accounts are agreed, track all ten through the exercise. Record customers who decline, cannot switch or need capabilities outside the offer; those outcomes are part of the benchmark.

| Stage | Proposed action | Proposed responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identify | Confirm Docusign usage and introduce the relevant business or IT owner. | OmniTrust account team and SDR |
| Qualify | Capture the workflow, renewal timing, actual cost, usage and reasons to consider a change. | SDR, supported by Circularo |
| Demonstrate | Show the customer’s process in the appropriate Circularo plan. | Circularo with OmniTrust |
| Quote | Compare equivalent scope and identify subscription, services and consumption separately. | Joint commercial team |
| Pilot | Validate the workflow, recipient experience, administration, evidence and export. | Customer process owner with the joint delivery team |
| Convert or learn | Agree production rollout and Docusign replacement, or record why the customer will not proceed. | OmniTrust account owner with Circularo |

**Proposed success definition:** Three customers purchase Circularo, use it in production for the agreed replacement scope, and retire the corresponding Docusign usage or commit to ending it at renewal. Report full-account replacements separately from departmental replacements. Demos, trials and parallel use alone do not count as replacements.

## Give the SDR a focused opening

The initial call should offer a review of an existing cost and workflow:

> “As an OmniTrust customer using Docusign, would you be open to comparing one of your current signing workflows with Circularo? We can show how the process would work, what would be included and whether there is a worthwhile saving before you make any decision to switch.”

The SDR should establish six things: current use case, current plan and annual spend, users and volume, renewal timing, required integrations or controls, and willingness to test a representative workflow. Technical or regulatory requirements should be passed to the joint team before an offer is made.

For enablement, we suggest a short product walkthrough, a demonstration based on OmniTrust’s internal evaluation, a qualification sheet, a plan comparison and a cost-comparison template. Agree who handles demonstrations and technical questions so the SDR has a clear next step after qualification.

## Pricing and services to test

We recommend separating the subscription from a clearly scoped implementation service. The options below are **proposed packages for this pilot**; they are not presented as proven outcomes from other co-branded partners. Final prices, partner economics, responsibilities and any incentives would be agreed together.

| Proposed offer | Suitable customer | Suggested service scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business with assisted onboarding | Teams replacing routine signing and approval processes | Workflow review, an agreed number of templates, administrator setup, training and a defined period of go-live assistance. |
| Enterprise with configured identity and administration | Customers needing domain/email identity, centralized access and operational oversight | Business onboarding scope plus agreed domain, email, SAML/SCIM and reporting configuration; renewal tools where relevant. |
| Enterprise with OmniTrust trust services | Customers with a specific certificate, identity or cryptographic-governance requirement | Separately scoped OmniTrust products, technical design, integration, testing and operational handover. |

Use a fixed implementation fee where the scope is repeatable. State the number and complexity of templates, integrations, training sessions and support period in advance. Scope historical-document migration separately where needed. Agree whether to offer a limited pilot fee credited against implementation after conversion; this is an option to discuss, not an existing commitment.

For every customer, show recurring annual cost and first-year total cost separately. Compare the actual Docusign contract or renewal quote with the required Circularo configuration, including add-ons, usage, support and any OmniTrust charges. Preserve a realistic implementation margin rather than hiding service effort inside a headline discount.

## Capture evidence before the broader launch

Use a common scorecard for OmniTrust’s internal evaluation and each customer, with the internal result reported separately.

| Measure | Evidence to capture |
| --- | --- |
| Commercial outcome | Offer, decision, actual saving for matched scope, and whether replacement is full or departmental. |
| Usability | Sender, approver, administrator and external-recipient feedback; preparation and completion time where measurable. |
| Delivery effort | Configuration and template work, training, integration effort, elapsed time and support demand. |
| Product fit | Required capabilities met, gaps, workarounds and reasons for declining or delaying. |
| Switching practicality | Access to historical agreements, evidence/export checks and Docusign retirement timing. |
| Partner economics | Subscription margin, services revenue, delivery cost and sales effort. |

After the ten-account exercise, review the three-replacement target alongside these results. Successful replacements should tell us which customers to pursue, what services to standardize and which benefits buyers value. Unsuccessful opportunities should shape qualification and product priorities. If the target is missed, revise the offer or customer selection before expanding.

The pilot will provide an initial benchmark, not a statistically representative US market average. Any case study, customer reference or public savings claim should use the customer’s permission and the documented comparison.

When Nick and Eric return, agree the internal workflows, the ten-account selection, named owners, SDR enablement and the proposed pricing/services model. Set the pilot timetable around customer availability and renewal dates. Use the resulting evidence to decide the scope and timing of the broader launch.

## Appendix A Plan capabilities and service boundaries

This appendix provides the product and commercial detail needed to evaluate the proposal without separate background materials. Final inclusions, activation requirements and allowances are defined in the customer quotation and order schedule.

### Business and Enterprise

| Capability | Business | Enterprise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signing, approvals, shared templates, document versioning and search | Included | Included |
| Custom domain and customer email infrastructure | Paid add-ons | Included capabilities; configuration required |
| SAML single sign-on | Paid add-on | Included; identity-provider setup required |
| SCIM user provisioning | Not offered in the published Business matrix | Included; directory configuration required |
| Advanced Reporting and Transaction Controller | Paid add-ons | Included |
| Contract Deadlines & Renewals | Paid add-on | Included |
| REST API, external-data pre-fill and PDF form field recognition | Paid add-ons | Included capabilities; integration work is separate |
| AI Document Assistant | Paid add-on | Included capability; AI provider usage is separate |
| Signature provider and signature mode selection | Paid add-ons | Included capabilities; providers and certificates are separate |
| Custom Web Forms, Custom Upload Form and Custom Application Name | Not offered in the published Business matrix | Paid add-ons |
| Custom branding/styling, custom signing certificate, KYC and qualified trust services | Paid add-ons | Paid add-ons |

Plan matrix checked on 30 September 2026. See [Circularo plans](https://www.circularo.com/pricing/#compare-plans).

**Operational controls.** Advanced Reporting helps administrators find pending transactions and outstanding actions. Transaction Controller allows an authorized role to inspect transaction metadata and take over or reassign ownership; this does not automatically grant access to document contents. Renewal tools support contract dates and reminders, rather than establishing full legal contract-lifecycle-management equivalence.

**Organizational identity.** Custom E-mail Identity uses the customer’s email infrastructure and domain. Custom Domain provides an organization-owned address for accessing Circularo. Configuration is required. Logos, styling, notification wording and application naming have separate scope. A custom domain does not change the underlying hosting model. See [email identity](https://help.circularo.com/en/add-on-custom-email-identity) and [custom domain](https://help.circularo.com/en/add-on-custom-domain).

**Access and integrations.** SAML handles sign-in; SCIM handles directory-driven user creation, updates and deactivation. REST API access enables integration development but does not include custom implementation or automated transaction/trust-service consumption. New API deployments require the applicable user-based licensing and separately licensed consumption; include all required internal users in the proposal.

**AI assistance.** The assistant supports summaries, key points and document classification suggestions, with users deciding what to apply. Customer-managed provider credentials and separately billed provider usage are required. It should not be positioned as autonomous execution or a replacement for professional review.

**Signing and usage.** Provider selection applies to enabled services. Signature mode selection does not itself create a qualified signature. Certificates, qualified services, SMS, KYC and automated consumption may require separate licensing and activation. Any unlimited allowance must be expressly quoted and applies to eligible manual use subject to fair use; it does not mean unlimited API, bulk or trust-service consumption.

**Storage and deployment.** Transactional-document retention follows the contracted scope; standalone document management and expanded storage are separate. Business and Enterprise are SaaS plans. Customer-hosted deployment and sovereign shared services belong to a separately scoped Ultimate offer. Confirm hosting locations and any sector-specific requirements for the actual customer deployment.

**OmniTrust scope.** The partnership combines Circularo’s document workflows with OmniTrust’s identity and cryptographic lifecycle governance and PKI capabilities. Confirm the supported integration, licenses and operational responsibilities for each proposed solution. OmniTrust products and services are not automatically included in Circularo Business or Enterprise. See the [joint solution overview](https://omnitrust.com/partners/omnitrust-circularo/).

### Comparison points for customer qualification

Docusign has branding, APIs, PKI-based signatures, AI functionality and broader agreement-management offerings. Its Business Pro plan also includes web forms, payments and bulk sending. Identify these dependencies early; Circularo is not a like-for-like replacement for every Docusign configuration. See [Docusign US plans](https://ecom.docusign.com/en-US/plans-and-pricing/esignature) and [digital signatures](https://www.docusign.com/products/digital-signature).

Do not assume that a Circularo transaction equals a Docusign envelope. Document grouping, recipients, API initiation and bulk processing can change the usage calculation. Replay representative workflows and price the actual volume before claiming a saving.

## Appendix B US price benchmark

The following US prices were observed on 30 September 2026 with **Annual | Billed upfront** selected. They are public list-price reference points, not customer-specific renewal prices. Taxes, services and additional consumption are excluded. [Docusign US pricing](https://ecom.docusign.com/en-US/plans-and-pricing/esignature).

| Docusign plan | Annual price per user | Monthly equivalent | Equivalent price at a 40% reduction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | US$300 | US$25 | US$180/year or US$15/month equivalent |
| Business Pro | US$480 | US$40 | US$288/year or US$24/month equivalent |
| Enhanced or negotiated enterprise configuration | Customer-specific quote | Customer-specific quote | 60% of the comparable annual price |

A proposed US$24-per-user monthly equivalent, billed annually, would be 40% below Business Pro’s base subscription but only 4% below Standard’s. This is an illustration, not a committed partner offer, a feature-parity claim or evidence of average savings. Circularo Enterprise requires a quote; the US offer and partner terms should be agreed before customer use.

For example, 25 users at US$24 per month would cost US$7,200 annually, versus US$12,000 at US$40. The base difference is US$4,800. Add the required implementation, usage and services to each side to determine the actual first-year and recurring savings.

For the pilot, record both the percentage saving on matched recurring cost and the first-year saving after transition costs. Recheck public prices when quoting and use the customer’s negotiated price wherever available.
