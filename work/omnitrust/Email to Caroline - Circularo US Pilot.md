# Email draft to Caroline

**Subject:** Circularo next steps: OmniTrust pilot and first 10 customer opportunities

Hi Caroline,

Thanks for the update. A follow-up once Nick and Eric are back works well. In the meantime, I suggest we use the next few weeks to shape a focused pilot for your SDR, starting with OmniTrust itself and then existing customers who use Docusign.

I’ve prepared a short proposal covering the positioning, pricing approach and services we could offer together. My suggested next steps are:

1. **Start with OmniTrust’s own Docusign workflows.** Let’s select two or three everyday processes and run them in Circularo, comparing the user experience, functionality and cost. If the fit is right, we can plan the replacement of those workflows. This would also give your SDR firsthand experience and a practical demonstration to use with customers.

2. **Identify ten existing OmniTrust customers using Docusign.** We can start with accounts where there is a clear process owner and an upcoming renewal or reason to review the current setup. Our goal should be to replace Docusign at three of those ten customers before a broader launch, with OmniTrust’s own evaluation counted separately. For each customer, we would agree whether the replacement covers the whole account or a specific department.

3. **Test a clear pricing and services offer.** I suggest targeting approximately 40% lower recurring cost for comparable requirements, validated against each customer’s actual Docusign costs. Alongside the subscription, we could offer a fixed-scope onboarding package covering workflow setup, template recreation, administrator training and go-live assistance. Enterprise customers could add the relevant identity configuration, while integrations, historical migration and OmniTrust trust services would be scoped separately. These are proposals for us to test and refine together, rather than a settled package.

4. **Equip the SDR and capture the feedback.** Let’s agree a simple qualification sheet, demo flow and side-by-side cost comparison. Across all ten accounts, we should record what motivates customers to switch, what prevents them from switching, the delivery effort and the actual savings. That will give us a useful initial benchmark before investing in the wider launch.

Beyond price, I would lead with recognizable signing requests through the customer’s own domain and email infrastructure, better control over approvals and renewals, and centralized employee access. Those are strong Enterprise conversations, with OmniTrust’s identity and cryptographic governance available for customers who need deeper assurance.

Could your team nominate the owner of OmniTrust’s current Docusign setup and start identifying the first ten customer accounts? We can use the follow-up call to agree the pilot scope, responsibilities and commercial package.
