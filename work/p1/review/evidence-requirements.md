## Claim gates

### Company identity and corporate proof

Required before external use:

- approved company description;
- legal entity and public naming guidance where relevant;
- headquarters and office-location claims;
- founding history;
- employee or office counts;
- markets and geographic footprint;
- customer and deployment counts;
- named customer references and logo permissions;
- transaction, user, or document volumes; and
- awards, analyst recognition, and partner status.

### Product positioning and nomenclature

Owner approval required for:

- Digital Trust Infrastructure;
- Trusted Execution Infrastructure;
- Circularo Unified Trust Platform;
- Trusted Execution Layer;
- continuous evidence chain;
- trusted record;
- institutional memory;
- programmable trust; and
- System of Authority.

The terms may be used internally as a coherent working system, but repeated use does not make them approved category facts.

### Product capabilities

Evidence or current approved product records are required for present-tense claims about:

- identity verification and assurance levels;
- delegated authority and representation;
- policy enforcement;
- collaboration and co-authoring;
- version control;
- workflows and approvals;
- personal signatures and organizational seals;
- timestamps and verification;
- evidence generation and independent verification;
- audit trails;
- trusted archive, DMS, vault, retention, and retrieval;
- REST APIs, events, and automation;
- integrations with IAM, national identity, PKI, CA, HSM, TSA, and trust-service providers;
- mobile and web channels;
- multi-entity or multi-tenant operation; and
- scale, availability, resilience, and performance.

### SaaS delivery

Validate before claiming:

- hosting regions and data-residency options;
- security architecture and controls;
- certifications and compliance coverage;
- service levels and availability;
- backup, disaster recovery, and retention;
- release and upgrade commitments;
- data portability and exit; and
- support and implementation models.

### Sovereign delivery

Validate before claiming:

- supported deployment environments;
- offline, air-gapped, or disconnected operation;
- customer control of infrastructure, data, keys, identity, policy, AI, and evidence;
- component-level operating responsibility;
- upgrade and patching model;
- observability, continuity, backup, and disaster recovery;
- hardware and cryptographic integration support; and
- local or sovereign support arrangements.

### Shared Services

Validate before claiming:

- national or government-wide deployments;
- multi-entity governance and tenant isolation;
- cross-entity evidence or reporting;
- onboarding speed and repeatability;
- transaction or entity scale;
- centralized versus delegated policy control;
- data and record ownership boundaries;
- interoperability; and
- shared-service operating and funding models.

### AI

For each item in KNOW, ADVISE, and ACT, label the status internally as current, configuration-dependent, integration-dependent, planned, or strategic direction.

Evidence is required for claims about:

- extraction, classification, summarization, translation, and retrieval;
- obligation, risk, anomaly, or recommendation generation;
- permission-aware context retrieval;
- agent identity and delegated authority;
- policy enforcement for agent actions;
- workflow initiation and approval requests;
- service or API invocation;
- auditability and evidence of agent actions; and
- model hosting or data-boundary controls.

Do not claim that Circularo verifies AI reasoning, eliminates hallucinations, or makes AI inherently trustworthy.

## Proof-slide standard

Every proof slide should identify:

1. the customer, environment, or approved anonymized context;
2. the business problem;
3. what Circularo actually delivered;
4. the relevant deployment or integration boundary;
5. the attributable outcome;
6. the source and owner of the claim; and
7. any qualification needed for external reuse.

## Open decisions

- Is “System of Authority” approved as an external strategic outcome, an internal framing, or a candidate category for testing?
- Is “Circularo Unified Trust Platform” the approved platform name across all delivery models?
- Which parts of KNOW, ADVISE, and ACT are current product capabilities?
- Does “trusted record” have an approved product and legal definition?
- Should “Trusted Vault,” “Trusted Archive,” or “DMS” be the preferred product-facing term?
- Which named deployment models are currently supported and externally claimable?
- Which customer or government references may be used in each deck?
- Which measurable outcomes are approved and reproducible?

## Publication gate

The P1 narrative can proceed to visual exploration for internal review, but no deck should be treated as externally final until:

- terminology is owner-approved;
- present-tense capabilities are validated;
- proof slides use governed evidence;
- sensitive deployment and compliance claims are reviewed; and
- unresolved placeholders or qualifications are removed from audience-facing copy.
