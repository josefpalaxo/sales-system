Add customer relationship folders when permitted content needs them. Keep one organization profile, its relevant preparation and permitted activity summaries together. Reference shared knowledge rather than maintaining another company or commercial rulebook.

These are operational records, outside the reusable knowledge catalogue. Drafts, evidence gaps and confidentiality remain visible. Keep raw CRM exports, credentials, customer dumps and personal data in approved business systems or ignored local storage, subject to the existing explicitly authorized investor-outreach exception. Preparing a message or deliverable does not authorize sending or publication.
