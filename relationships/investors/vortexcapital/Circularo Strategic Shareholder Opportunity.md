# **Circularo Strategic Shareholder Opportunity**

Prepared for Vortex Capital
Confidential discussion draft | 11 October 2026

## **The Business and Strategic Position**

Circularo is a **digital trust technology company headquartered in Dubai, UAE**, helping organizations and governments keep digital processes connected, controlled and verifiable. We are profitable, debt free and founder-owned, with government shared-service deployments and an enterprise customer base in the GCC.

We are exploring bringing in a **strategic minority shareholder, with an interest of up to approximately 25%**. Our objectives are to accelerate GCC and international expansion and provide meaningful founder liquidity. We are open to discussing the transaction structure, including primary capital where it supports a defined growth plan.

### **The problem we address**

An agreement, approval or official document carries a decision: who acted, what they were authorized to do, what was approved and what evidence remains. When preparation, approvals, signing and records move between applications and organizations, those connections can become difficult to reconstruct.

Circularo's strategic proposition is **Trust Orchestration**: bringing content, identity, authority, workflows, approvals, supported trust services and evidence together around a business process. The intended outcome is **Trusted Execution**: a governed action under applicable requirements, supported by evidence that allows the organization to verify the outcome.

**Circularo orchestrates trust. Trusted Execution is the outcome.** The central principle is to keep the action connected to its authority and evidence. Capability, integration and evidence coverage depend on the offering and deployment.

### **How Circularo has evolved**

**Digital Signatures → Trust Orchestration → Trusted Execution → Agentic Trusted Execution**

eSignature gave Circularo an entry point into identity, approvals, trust services and process evidence. Our scope is expanding around the complete business action. This is an evolution in strategic scope, not four completed product generations: richer native **Trusted Records** and deeper authority models are in development; agentic execution remains a longer-term direction.

### **The operating business**

Circularo is offered as managed SaaS and in customer-controlled infrastructure. Shared services describe an operating model in which a common service supports multiple organizations; deployment and responsibilities are established for each environment.

Our revenue model is subscription-based, complemented by consumption-based add-ons, advanced trust services and support services. We have built the business without external institutional equity funding.

## **Growth Through Platforms and Distribution**

Our growth strategy builds on the operating business and platform positions already established. Trust Orchestration provides a common strategic story across enterprise processes, government environments and shared services. The growth opportunity is to broaden paying adoption within those environments and replicate the model through new platforms and partners.

### **Three engines for scale**

1. **Direct Enterprise Adoption** expands through new customers and additional users, departments and use cases within existing accounts.

2. **Sovereign & Shared-Service Platforms** expand through broader paid adoption across underlying entities, additional processes and replication in other jurisdictions.

3. **Strategic Distribution Partners** commercialize Circularo through their own brands, services and customer ecosystems.

### **Platform Multiplication as the key growth mechanism**

**Direct Enterprise:** Circularo → Enterprise Customer

**Government Shared Services:** Circularo → Government Shared-Service Platform → Multiple Government Entities

**Strategic Distribution:** Circularo → Strategic Partner / Service Provider → Multiple Downstream Customers

**One Circularo platform deployment or strategic relationship can potentially create access to many underlying organizations, departments or downstream customers.** Together, these engines create the **Platform Multiplication effect**: growth through direct adoption, expansion within shared platforms and distribution across partner ecosystems.

The next phase focuses on multiplying a proven operating base. The investment case depends on converting ecosystem access into paying adoption and recurring revenue; replication in new markets still requires commercial validation and execution.

This is why the choice of shareholder matters. A strategic shareholder with relevant government, enterprise or portfolio-company access can accelerate the same mechanism through specific deployments, distribution initiatives and expansion opportunities.

### **Existing platforms and expansion opportunities**

For governments with existing identity and trust infrastructure, the orchestration opportunity is to connect those foundations to agency workflows, approvals and retained evidence. A shared-service operator can make common capabilities reusable across participating entities while preserving each entity's mandate and decision rights. Each deployment must establish the supported services and responsibilities.

Expansion remains dependent on adoption, procurement and commercial terms; it is not contracted revenue.

### **Strategic value and execution risks**

The combination of deployment flexibility, regional identity integrations, enterprise workflows and operating experience in government environments can make Circularo relevant to a buyer seeking GCC market access or complementary trust technology. The strategic value should be tested through specific commercial opportunities the buyer can unlock.

The main risks are GCC and customer concentration, long procurement cycles, and the pace at which platform access converts to paid adoption. International replication and partner distribution also require execution resources. We would welcome a candid assessment of these constraints.

### **The AI opportunity and longer term product direction**

As AI moves from advising people to acting on behalf of organizations, it creates an institutional authority problem. Permission to prepare an agreement need not include permission to approve or execute it.

**Intelligence does not create institutional authority.** Organizations need to establish whom an agent represents, what it is permitted to do, which approvals apply and what evidence must remain.

Circularo's strategic ambition is to extend Trust Orchestration to the **execution boundary**, where a proposed action becomes an authorized business action. Its workflow, signing, API and process-evidence experience provides a foundation; deeper authority models and richer Trusted Records are in development.

The potential role is to keep agent-initiated actions routed through the governed process connected to their mandate, controls and resulting evidence. Connected applications must provide evidence of actual completion. Records with provenance and appropriate permissions could also support future institutional memory and better-grounded AI assistance.

**Agentic Trusted Execution is longer-term strategic upside**, separate from current revenue and released capabilities.

### **What we would like to establish with Vortex**

We would also value comparable transaction examples and clarity on whether Vortex is approaching us on behalf of specific capital partners or proposing a sell-side advisory engagement.
