# Investor outreach batch 02 observations and recommendations

## Selection implications

- **Founder liquidity is a gate, not an optional later discussion.** A primary-only corporate investment would fail the new mandate even if the buyer offers distribution. Qualify willingness to acquire founders' existing shares before lengthy strategic discussions.
- **An investor's published minimum can exceed Circularo's minimum.** FTV Ascend publishes USD 20–60m; Spectrum's 2024 strategy says USD 30–200m. These are conditional larger-ticket routes, not practical USD 4m cheque assumptions. Sources: [N014](evidence-register.md#n014), [N015](evidence-register.md#n015).
- **Published liquidity language is materially stronger than acquisition activity.** Spectrum, Inflexion, Keensight and FTV publish shareholder-liquidity or flexible liquidity strategies. Confirm the eligible strategy and actual founder proceeds; that still does not prove Circularo interest.
- **Do not convert a primary precedent into secondary evidence.** e&'s Bespin announcement specifies primary capital and a JV. Its strategic relevance remains a hypothesis, while secondary willingness stays unknown.
- **A control transaction may contain founder liquidity without matching the desired operating model.** Signicat's Inverid acquisition includes sellers and reinvestment; it demonstrates a useful transaction mechanism, not acceptance of a standalone minority stake. Unfixed equity does not settle control, rollover or independence.
- **Geography applies to the investee as well as the investor.** Investcorp ITP specifies European headquarters, control, revenue/EBITDA and USD 20–100m equity criteria. Main targets northwestern Europe and North America. A GCC relationship does not bypass those policies.
- **Separate financial quantities.** Keensight's EUR 10–400m range is company revenue. Inflexion Fund III's published range is enterprise value. Neither is an equity-ticket range. No current approved Circularo metrics establish fit.
- **Old fund criteria can mislead a new batch.** Five Elms' USD 5–75m range belongs to Fund V in 2021. Fund VI and the current home page emphasise software growth and AI; their checked pages do not establish the current ticket range. Ask for current policy.

## Operating companies and non-VC routes

Entrust, DigiCert, OneSpan, Hyland, Signicat, Namirial, Thales, G+D, e&, G42, stc and Elm are included for potential business/technology value as actively as financial investors. Their relevant acquisitions support strategic activity; they do not establish minority-secondary appetite. G+D uses the parent route only; its Ventures mandate and Ventures contacts were removed. stc uses group/operating-company sponsorship rather than Tali. Other conventional corporate-venture routes from batch 01 were not carried into the new first screen.

The proposed strategic mechanism should be specific: enterprise distribution, identity assurance, trusted transactions, sovereign deployment or international operating support. Circularo's draft sovereign and future agentic narratives are qualification context, not verified capability or financial proof.

## Recommended next qualification

Before sending, establish the investment vehicle and responsible decision-maker, founder-secondary acceptance, practical ticket, eligible Circularo legal entity and verified financial criteria. Then test the proposed operating benefit. For a buyer that prefers control, clarify retained management, founder rollover, IP, customer obligations and governance instead of assuming the removal of the percentage cap approves those terms.
