# Comparison of investor outreach batches 01 and 02

| Dimension | Batch 01 | Batch 02 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Investment amount | Approx. USD 10–15m | At least USD 4m, no fixed maximum |
| Equity percentage | Up to approx. 25% | No fixed percentage or cap |
| Founder liquidity | Primarily purchase of founders' shares | Mandatory secondary component in every acceptable transaction |
| Primary capital | Not primarily a capital raise | Can accompany secondary; primary-only does not qualify |
| Investor types | Included corporate venture and growth/PE discovery | Conventional VC excluded; operating parents assessed separately from their venture arms |
| Geographic attention | US, Europe and UAE/GCC equally important, with additional worldwide discovery | Equal US/Europe/UAE/GCC focus preserved in first screen; other regions not disqualified |
| Output | 100 discovery candidates, 20 detailed profiles, 31 named contacts | 24 conditional profiles, 26 personal links across 19 organisations |
| Outreach sent | None during research | None |

The revised mandate is broader in amount and ownership but stricter about VC and secondary proceeds. Batch 02 is a focused screen, not a relabelled claim that the original 100 all fit.

## Treatment of the original discovery universe

The [disposition CSV](batch-01-disposition.csv) accounts for all 100 original discovery rows. It is an internal selection record, not newly verified evidence of each unselected organisation's current mandate.

Conventional VC routes such as M12, Salesforce Ventures, Workday Ventures, Okta Ventures, Aramco Ventures and G+D Ventures are outside this mandate. A large operating parent is not automatically excluded merely because it owns a venture arm; its corporate-development route requires separate evidence. stc remains through its operating group, with Tali excluded.

TA, Hg, Summit, General Atlantic, Francisco Partners and other unselected non-VC/global candidates remain in batch 01 for later requalification. Not being in these 24 does not establish unsuitability. For example, TA's current approach publishes USD 150–600m equity investments; that can be relevant only if a much larger transaction and company-scale fit are realistic. [TA approach](https://www.ta.com/approach/) checked 2 October 2026.

The batch 01 brief and seven CSV/JSON source/output files retain their content. The brief received only a terminal newline on relocation. Normalising the folder structure did not change its old assumptions, candidate data or research conclusions.
